Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TRF means Sandefjord Airport, Torp. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: ENTO. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 59.1867° N, 10.2586° E. It stands 87 metres (286 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Drammen, about 62 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 17/35, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 17 is heading about 170° on the compass, and one using the other end about 350°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 87 metres, Sandefjord Airport, Torp stands higher than about 51% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 47 airports of Norway with a height in the data, it is number 6 from the top. It is about 6,580 km north of the equator, far enough from it for very long summer days and very short winter ones.
