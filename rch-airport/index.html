Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, RCH means Almirante Padilla Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SKRH. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 11.5262° N, 72.9260° W. It stands 13 metres (43 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 09/27, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 09 is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 13 metres above sea level, Almirante Padilla Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 50 airports of Colombia with a height in the data, it is number 42 from the top. It is about 1,280 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
