Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MCI means Kansas City International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KMCI. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 39.3017° N, 94.7139° W. It stands 313 metres (1,026 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 01L/19R, lies roughly south to north. An aircraft using the end marked 01L is heading about 10° on the compass, and one using the other end about 190°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 3 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.
