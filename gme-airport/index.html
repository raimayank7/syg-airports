Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, GME means Gomel Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: UMGG. ICAO codes that begin with U belong to Russia and neighbouring states. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 52.5270° N, 31.0167° E. It stands 144 metres (472 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 10/28, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 10 is heading about 100° on the compass, and one using the other end about 280°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 144 metres, Gomel Airport stands higher than about 59% of the 4,004 airports on this site. It is about 5,840 km north of the equator.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Gomel Airport there are 2 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. That is few: the region is thinly served by air. The nearest large airport is Minsk National Airport (MSQ), about 249 km away.
