Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, NLT means Xinyuan Nalati Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: ZWNL. ICAO codes that begin with Z belong to China, Mongolia and North Korea. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 43.4318° N, 83.3786° E. It stands 930 metres (3,050 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 08/26, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 08 is heading about 80° on the compass, and one using the other end about 260°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.
