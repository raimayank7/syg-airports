Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SUR means Summer Beaver Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CJV7. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 52.7086° N, 88.5419° W. It stands 254 metres (832 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Summer Beaver Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,067 metres (3,500 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 245 airports of Canada on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 197.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 17/35, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 17 is heading about 170° on the compass, and one using the other end about 350°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 254 metres, Summer Beaver Airport stands higher than about 69% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 255 airports of Canada with a height in the data, it is number 83 from the top. It is about 5,860 km north of the equator.
