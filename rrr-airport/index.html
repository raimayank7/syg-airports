Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, RRR means Raroia Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: NTKO. ICAO codes that begin with N belong to the South Pacific. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 16.0505° S, 142.4766° W. It stands 5 metres (18 feet) above sea level.
