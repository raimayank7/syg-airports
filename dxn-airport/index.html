Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, DXN means Noida International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VIND. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 28.1799° N, 77.6118° E. It stands 196 metres (644 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 10/28, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 10 is heading about 100° on the compass, and one using the other end about 280°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 196 metres, Noida International Airport stands higher than about 64% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 115 airports of India with a height in the data, it is number 55 from the top. It is about 3,130 km north of the equator.
