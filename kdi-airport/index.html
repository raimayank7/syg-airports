Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, KDI means Haluoleo Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: WAWW. ICAO codes that begin with W belong to Indonesia, Malaysia and nearby island countries. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 4.0816° S, 122.4180° E. It stands 164 metres (538 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 08/26, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 08 is heading about 80° on the compass, and one using the other end about 260°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 164 metres, Haluoleo Airport stands higher than about 61% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 121 airports of Indonesia with a height in the data, it is number 15 from the top. It is about 450 km south of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Haluoleo Airport there are 5 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. The nearest large airport is Sultan Hasanuddin International Airport (UPG), about 336 km away, roughly 55 min in the air.
