Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, YWJ means Déline Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CYWJ. ICAO codes that begin with C belong to Canada. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 65.2111° N, 123.4360° W. It stands 214 metres (703 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Déline Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,199 metres (3,933 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 245 airports of Canada on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 158.
