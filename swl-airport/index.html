Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SWL means San Vicente Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: RPSV. ICAO codes that begin with R belong to East Asia, including Japan, Korea and the Philippines. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 10.5243° N, 119.2729° E. It stands 7 metres (24 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Puerto Princesa, about 105 km away in a straight line.
