Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MTF means Mizan Teferi Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: HAMT. ICAO codes that begin with H belong to east and north-east Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 6.9571° N, 35.5547° E. It stands 1,340 metres (4,396 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Jimma, about 162 km away in a straight line.
