Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ABX means Albury Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: YMAY. ICAO codes that begin with Y belong to Australia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 36.0668° S, 146.9591° E. It stands 164 metres (539 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 07/25, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 07 is heading about 70° on the compass, and one using the other end about 250°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.
