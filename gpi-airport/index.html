Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, GPI means Guapi Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SKGP. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 2.5701° N, 77.8980° W. It stands 50 metres (164 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Popayán, about 143 km away in a straight line.

Runways

Guapi Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,297 metres (4,256 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 48 airports of Colombia on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 41.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 01/19, lies roughly south to north. An aircraft using the end marked 01 is heading about 10° on the compass, and one using the other end about 190°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 50 metres, Guapi Airport stands higher than about 43% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 50 airports of Colombia with a height in the data, it is number 37 from the top. It is about 290 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Guapi Airport there are 11 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. The nearest large airport is Alfonso Bonilla Aragon International Airport (CLO), about 200 km away.
