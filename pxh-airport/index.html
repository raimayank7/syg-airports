Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PXH means Prominent Hill Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: YPMH. ICAO codes that begin with Y belong to Australia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 29.7160° S, 135.5244° E. It stands 227 metres (745 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Prominent Hill Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 2,200 metres (7,218 feet). That suits narrow-body jets and regional aircraft. Among the 156 airports of Australia on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 30.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 18/36, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 18 is heading about 180° on the compass, and one using the other end about 360°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 227 metres, Prominent Hill Airport stands higher than about 67% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 152 airports of Australia with a height in the data, it is number 35 from the top. It is about 3,300 km south of the equator.

Other airports around it
