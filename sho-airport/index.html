Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SHO means King Mswati III International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: FDSK. ICAO codes that begin with F belong to central and southern Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

The airport lies at 26.3586° S, 31.7169° E. It stands 333 metres (1,092 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 02/20, lies roughly south to north. An aircraft using the end marked 02 is heading about 20° on the compass, and one using the other end about 200°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.
