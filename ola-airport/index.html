Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, OLA means Ørland Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: ENOL. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 63.6989° N, 9.6040° E. It stands 9 metres (28 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Trondheim, about 49 km away in a straight line.
