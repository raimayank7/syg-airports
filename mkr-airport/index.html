Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MKR means Meekatharra Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: YMEK. ICAO codes that begin with Y belong to Australia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 26.6117° S, 118.5480° E. It stands 522 metres (1,713 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 09/27, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 09 is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 522 metres, Meekatharra Airport stands higher than about 82% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 152 airports of Australia with a height in the data, it is number 8 from the top. It is about 2,960 km south of the equator.
