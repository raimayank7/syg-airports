Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, KSJ means Kasos Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: LGKS. ICAO codes that begin with L belong to southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 35.4214° N, 26.9100° E. It stands 11 metres (35 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Irákleion, about 161 km away in a straight line.
