Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, KKS means Kashan Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: OIFK. ICAO codes that begin with O belong to the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 33.8953° N, 51.5770° E. It stands 1,056 metres (3,465 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 11/29, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 11 is heading about 110° on the compass, and one using the other end about 290°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 1,056 metres, Kashan Airport stands higher than about 91% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 45 airports of Iran with a height in the data, it is number 19 from the top. In a standard atmosphere the air pressure at this height is about 88% of the pressure at sea level. Engines and wings both work less well in thinner air, so the same aircraft needs more runway here than at the coast. It is about 3,770 km north of the equator.
