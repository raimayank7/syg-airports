Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SQG means Tebelian Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: WIOS. ICAO codes that begin with W belong to Indonesia, Malaysia and nearby island countries. How the two kinds of code differ.
