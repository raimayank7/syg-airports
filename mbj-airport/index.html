Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MBJ means Sangster International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MKJS. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 18.5034° N, 77.9132° W. It stands 1 metres (4 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Spanish Town, about 116 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 07/25, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 07 is heading about 70° on the compass, and one using the other end about 250°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 1 metre above sea level, Sangster International Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. It is about 2,060 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Sangster International Airport there are 8 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. The nearest other large airport is Norman Manley International Airport (KIN), about 135 km away, close enough to reach by road in most cases.
