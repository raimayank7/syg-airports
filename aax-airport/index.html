Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, AAX means Romeu Zema Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SBAX. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 19.5632° S, 46.9604° W. It stands 999 metres (3,276 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 16/34, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 16 is heading about 160° on the compass, and one using the other end about 340°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 999 metres, Romeu Zema Airport stands higher than about 90% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 129 airports of Brazil with a height in the data, it is number 2 from the top. It is about 2,180 km south of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
