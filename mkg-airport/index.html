Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MKG means Muskegon County Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KMKG. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 43.1695° N, 86.2382° W. It stands 192 metres (629 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Grand Rapids, about 52 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 06/24, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 06 is heading about 60° on the compass, and one using the other end about 240°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.
