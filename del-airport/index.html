Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, DEL means Indira Gandhi International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VIDP. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 28.5556° N, 77.0952° E. It stands 237 metres (777 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 11R/29L, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 11R is heading about 110° on the compass, and one using the other end about 290°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 4 runways point in 3 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 237 metres, Indira Gandhi International Airport stands higher than about 68% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 115 airports of India with a height in the data, it is number 43 from the top. It is about 3,180 km north of the equator.
