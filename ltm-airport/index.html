Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, LTM means Lethem Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SYLT. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 3.3728° N, 59.7894° W. It stands 107 metres (351 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Boa Vista, about 116 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 07/25, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 07 is heading about 70° on the compass, and one using the other end about 250°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 107 metres, Lethem Airport stands higher than about 54% of the 4,004 airports on this site. It is about 380 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Lethem Airport there are 3 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. That is few: the region is thinly served by air. The nearest large airport is Atlas Brasil Cantanhede International Airport (BVB) in Brazil, about 116 km away, close enough to reach by road in most cases.
