Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SSA means Deputado Luiz Eduardo Magalhães International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SBSV. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.
