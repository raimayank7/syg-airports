Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, LGL means Long Lellang Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: WBGF. ICAO codes that begin with W belong to Indonesia, Malaysia and nearby island countries. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 3.4210° N, 115.1540° E. It stands 427 metres (1,400 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Miri, about 169 km away in a straight line.
