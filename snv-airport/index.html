Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SNV means Santa Elena de Uairén Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SVSE. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 4.5547° N, 61.1452° W. It stands 896 metres (2,938 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Boa Vista, about 200 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 11/29, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 11 is heading about 110° on the compass, and one using the other end about 290°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 896 metres, Santa Elena de Uairén Airport stands higher than about 89% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 22 airports of Venezuela with a height in the data, it is the highest. It is about 510 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
