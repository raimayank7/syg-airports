Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PNY means Pondicherry Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VOPC. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 11.9680° N, 79.8120° E. It stands 41 metres (134 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 07/25, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 07 is heading about 70° on the compass, and one using the other end about 250°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 41 metres, Pondicherry Airport stands higher than about 40% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 115 airports of India with a height in the data, it is number 98 from the top. It is about 1,330 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
