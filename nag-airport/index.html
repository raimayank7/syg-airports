Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, NAG means Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VANP. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.
