Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, REX means General Lucio Blanco International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MMRX. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

The airport lies at 26.0089° N, 98.2285° W. It stands 42 metres (139 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 13/31, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 13 is heading about 130° on the compass, and one using the other end about 310°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.
