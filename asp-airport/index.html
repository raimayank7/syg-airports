Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ASP means Alice Springs Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: YBAS. ICAO codes that begin with Y belong to Australia. How the two kinds of code differ.

The airport lies at 23.8066° S, 133.9034° E. It stands 545 metres (1,789 feet) above sea level.
