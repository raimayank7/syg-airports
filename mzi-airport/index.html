Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MZI means Mopti Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: GAMB. ICAO codes that begin with G belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 14.5128° N, 4.0796° W. It stands 276 metres (906 feet) above sea level.
