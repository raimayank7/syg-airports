Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, GLT means Gladstone Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: YGLA. ICAO codes that begin with Y belong to Australia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 23.8698° S, 151.2254° E. It stands 20 metres (64 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 10/28, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 10 is heading about 100° on the compass, and one using the other end about 280°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 20 metres, Gladstone Airport stands higher than about 28% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 152 airports of Australia with a height in the data, it is number 97 from the top. It is about 2,650 km south of the equator.
