Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, YOH means Oxford House Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CYOH. ICAO codes that begin with C belong to Canada. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 54.9333° N, 95.2789° W. It stands 202 metres (663 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Oxford House Airport has 2 runways in the data. The longest is 1,167 metres (3,828 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 245 airports of Canada on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 164.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 04/22, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 04 is heading about 40° on the compass, and one using the other end about 220°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 202 metres, Oxford House Airport stands higher than about 65% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 255 airports of Canada with a height in the data, it is number 112 from the top. It is about 6,110 km north of the equator.
