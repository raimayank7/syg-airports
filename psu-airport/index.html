Pangsuma Airport (PSU)

Putussibau-Borneo Island, Kalimantan Barat (West Kalimantan), Indonesia

Pangsuma Airport is a medium-sized airport serving Putussibau-Borneo Island, in Kalimantan Barat (West Kalimantan), Indonesia. It has scheduled airline flights. Its IATA code, the three letters printed on tickets and baggage tags, is PSU.

Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PSU means Pangsuma Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: WIOP. ICAO codes that begin with W belong to Indonesia, Malaysia and nearby island countries. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 0.8346° N, 112.9402° E. It stands 91 metres (297 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Kapit, about 129 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 10/28, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 10 is heading about 100° on the compass, and one using the other end about 280°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 91 metres, Pangsuma Airport stands higher than about 51% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 121 airports of Indonesia with a height in the data, it is number 26 from the top. It is about 90 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it
