Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BBR means Basse-Terre Baillif Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: TFFB. ICAO codes that begin with T belong to the Caribbean. How the two kinds of code differ.
