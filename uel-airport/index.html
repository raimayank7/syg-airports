Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, UEL means Quelimane Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: FQQL. ICAO codes that begin with F belong to central and southern Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 17.8555° S, 36.8691° E. It stands 11 metres (36 feet) above sea level.
