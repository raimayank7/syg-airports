Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CYS means Cheyenne Regional Jerry Olson Field. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KCYS. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 41.1557° N, 104.8120° W. It stands 1,877 metres (6,159 feet) above sea level. That is high for an airport. Thin air at this height means aircraft need a longer run to take off. The nearest large city in our data is Fort Collins, about 67 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 09/27, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 09 is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 1,877 metres, Cheyenne Regional Jerry Olson Field stands higher than about 97% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 645 airports of United States with a height in the data, it is number 20 from the top. In a standard atmosphere the air pressure at this height is about 80% of the pressure at sea level. Engines and wings both work less well in thinner air, so the same aircraft needs more runway here than at the coast. It is about 4,580 km north of the equator.
