Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, AAC means El Arish International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: HEAR. ICAO codes that begin with H belong to east and north-east Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 31.0553° N, 33.8280° E. It stands 36 metres (118 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Gaza, about 78 km away in a straight line.
