Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, AAE means Annaba Rabah Bitat Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: DABB. ICAO codes that begin with D belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 36.8268° N, 7.8133° E. It stands 5 metres (16 feet) above sea level.
