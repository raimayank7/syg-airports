Puerto Barrios Airport (PBR)

Puerto Barrios, Izabal Department, Guatemala

Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PBR means Puerto Barrios Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MGPB. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 15.7309° N, 88.5838° W. It stands 10 metres (33 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is San Pedro Sula, about 65 km away in a straight line.

Runways

Puerto Barrios Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 2,707 metres (8,880 feet). That is long enough for most passenger jets. Among the 5 airports of Guatemala on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 3.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 12/30, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 12 is heading about 120° on the compass, and one using the other end about 300°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 10 metres above sea level, Puerto Barrios Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 6 airports of Guatemala with a height in the data, it is the lowest. It is about 1,750 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Puerto Barrios Airport there are 23 other airports with scheduled flights, and 5 of them are within 100 km. The nearest large airport is Ramón Villeda Morales International Airport (SAP) in Honduras, about 77 km away, close enough to reach by road in most cases.
