Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, JNS means Narsaq Heliport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: BGNS. ICAO codes that begin with B belong to Greenland, Iceland and Kosovo. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 60.9173° N, 46.0599° W. It stands 25 metres (83 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Narsaq Heliport has 2 runways in the data. The longest is 60 metres (197 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 18 airports of Greenland on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 16.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 04/22, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 04 is heading about 40° on the compass, and one using the other end about 220°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 25 metres, Narsaq Heliport stands higher than about 33% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 54 airports of Greenland with a height in the data, it is number 23 from the top. It is about 6,770 km north of the equator, far enough from it for very long summer days and very short winter ones.
