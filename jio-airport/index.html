Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, JIO means Jos Orno Imsula Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: WAPM. ICAO codes that begin with W belong to Indonesia, Malaysia and nearby island countries. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 8.1407° S, 127.9097° E. It stands 13 metres (43 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Lospalos, about 109 km away in a straight line.

Height and position

At 13 metres above sea level, Jos Orno Imsula Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 121 airports of Indonesia with a height in the data, it is number 78 from the top. It is about 910 km south of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Jos Orno Imsula Airport there are 2 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. That is few: the region is thinly served by air. The nearest large airport is Presidente Nicolau Lobato International Airport (DIL) in Timor-Leste, about 266 km away.
