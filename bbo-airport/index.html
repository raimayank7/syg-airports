Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BBO means Berbera Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: HCMI. ICAO codes that begin with H belong to east and north-east Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 10.3850° N, 44.9367° E. It stands 9 metres (30 feet) above sea level.
