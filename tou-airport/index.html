Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TOU means Touho Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: NWWU. ICAO codes that begin with N belong to the South Pacific. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 20.7901° S, 165.2595° E. It stands 3 metres (10 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Dumbéa, about 196 km away in a straight line.
