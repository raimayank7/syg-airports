Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, URG means Rubem Berta Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SBUG. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 29.7822° S, 57.0382° W. It stands 78 metres (256 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 9/27, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 9 is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.
