Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TUK means Turbat International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: OPTU. ICAO codes that begin with O belong to the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 25.9848° N, 63.0289° E. It stands 152 metres (498 feet) above sea level.
