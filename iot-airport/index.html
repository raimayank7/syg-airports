Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, IOT means Illorsuit Heliport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: BGLL. ICAO codes that begin with B belong to Greenland, Iceland and Kosovo. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 71.2419° N, 53.5629° W. It stands 47 metres (154 feet) above sea level.

Height and position

At 47 metres, Illorsuit Heliport stands higher than about 42% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 54 airports of Greenland with a height in the data, it is number 10 from the top. It is about 7,920 km north of the equator, inside the Arctic Circle, where the sun does not set for part of the summer and does not rise for part of the winter.
