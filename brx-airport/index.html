Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BRX means Maria Montez International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MDBH. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 18.2515° N, 71.1204° W. It stands 3 metres (10 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is San Cristóbal, about 109 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 12/30, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 12 is heading about 120° on the compass, and one using the other end about 300°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 3 metres above sea level, Maria Montez International Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 8 airports of Dominican Republic with a height in the data, it is the lowest. It is about 2,030 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
