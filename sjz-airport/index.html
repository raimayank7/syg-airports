Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SJZ means São Jorge Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: LPSJ. ICAO codes that begin with L belong to southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 38.6655° N, 28.1758° W. It stands 95 metres (311 feet) above sea level.

Runways

São Jorge Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,412 metres (4,633 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 19 airports of Portugal on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 12.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 13/31, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 13 is heading about 130° on the compass, and one using the other end about 310°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 95 metres, São Jorge Airport stands higher than about 52% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 19 airports of Portugal with a height in the data, it is number 7 from the top. It is about 4,300 km north of the equator.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of São Jorge Airport there are 7 other airports with scheduled flights, and 4 of them are within 100 km. The nearest large airport is João Paulo II Airport (PDL), about 240 km away.
