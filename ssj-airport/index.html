Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SSJ means Sandnessjøen Airport, Stokka. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: ENST. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 65.9568° N, 12.4689° E. It stands 17 metres (56 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Mo i Rana, about 85 km away in a straight line.
