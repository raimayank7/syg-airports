Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, XSI means South Indian Lake Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CZSN. ICAO codes that begin with C belong to Canada. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 56.7928° N, 98.9072° W. It stands 290 metres (951 feet) above sea level.

Runways

South Indian Lake Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,070 metres (3,509 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 245 airports of Canada on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 194.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 18/36, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 18 is heading about 180° on the compass, and one using the other end about 360°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 290 metres, South Indian Lake Airport stands higher than about 72% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 255 airports of Canada with a height in the data, it is number 70 from the top. It is about 6,310 km north of the equator, far enough from it for very long summer days and very short winter ones.
