Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, DBB means El Alamein International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: HEAL. ICAO codes that begin with H belong to east and north-east Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 30.9243° N, 28.4616° E. It stands 47 metres (154 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Marsá Maţrūḩ, about 126 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 13/31, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 13 is heading about 130° on the compass, and one using the other end about 310°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 47 metres, El Alamein International Airport stands higher than about 42% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 17 airports of Egypt with a height in the data, it is number 13 from the top. It is about 3,440 km north of the equator.
