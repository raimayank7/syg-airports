Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ELU means Guemar Airport - مطار قمار بالوادي. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: DAUO. ICAO codes that begin with D belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.
