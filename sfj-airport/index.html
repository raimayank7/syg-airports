Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SFJ means Kangerlussuaq International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: BGSF. ICAO codes that begin with B belong to Greenland, Iceland and Kosovo. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 67.0104° N, 50.7153° W. It stands 50 metres (165 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Kangerlussuaq International Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 2,810 metres (9,219 feet). That is long enough for most passenger jets. Among the 18 airports of Greenland on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 1.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 09/27, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 09 is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 50 metres, Kangerlussuaq International Airport stands higher than about 43% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 54 airports of Greenland with a height in the data, it is number 9 from the top. It is about 7,450 km north of the equator, inside the Arctic Circle, where the sun does not set for part of the summer and does not rise for part of the winter.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Kangerlussuaq International Airport there are 14 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. The nearest large airport is Ilulissat Airport (JAV), about 249 km away.
