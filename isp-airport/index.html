Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ISP means Long Island MacArthur Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KISP. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 40.7963° N, 73.1017° W. It stands 30 metres (99 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Queens, about 63 km away in a straight line.
