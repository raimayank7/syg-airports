Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, KEJ means Alexei Leonov Kemerovo International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: UNEE. ICAO codes that begin with U belong to Russia and neighbouring states. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 55.2701° N, 86.1072° E. It stands 263 metres (863 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 05/23, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 05 is heading about 50° on the compass, and one using the other end about 230°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 263 metres, Alexei Leonov Kemerovo International Airport stands higher than about 70% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 147 airports of Russia with a height in the data, it is number 29 from the top. It is about 6,150 km north of the equator, far enough from it for very long summer days and very short winter ones.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Alexei Leonov Kemerovo International Airport there are 4 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. The nearest other large airport is Tomsk Kamov Airport (TOF), about 136 km away, close enough to reach by road in most cases.
