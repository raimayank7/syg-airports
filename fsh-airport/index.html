Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, FSH means Syekh Hamzah Fansyuri Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: WIMI. ICAO codes that begin with W belong to Indonesia, Malaysia and nearby island countries. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 2.2696° N, 97.9679° E. It stands 5 metres (17 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Subulussalam, about 45 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 12/30, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 12 is heading about 120° on the compass, and one using the other end about 300°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 5 metres above sea level, Syekh Hamzah Fansyuri Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 121 airports of Indonesia with a height in the data, it is number 98 from the top. It is about 250 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
