Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ISC means St. Mary's Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: EGHE. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 49.9134° N, 6.2919° W. It stands 35 metres (116 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Plymouth, about 161 km away in a straight line.
