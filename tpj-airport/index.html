Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TPJ means Taplejung Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VNTJ. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 27.3509° N, 87.6953° E. It stands 2,435 metres (7,990 feet) above sea level. That is high for an airport. Thin air at this height means aircraft need a longer run to take off. The nearest large city in our data is Dārjiling, about 67 km away in a straight line.
