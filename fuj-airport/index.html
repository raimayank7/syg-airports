Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, FUJ means Fukue Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: RJFE. ICAO codes that begin with R belong to East Asia, including Japan, Korea and the Philippines. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 32.6663° N, 128.8330° E. It stands 83 metres (273 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Nagasaki, about 99 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 03/21, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 03 is heading about 30° on the compass, and one using the other end about 210°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 83 metres, Fukue Airport stands higher than about 50% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 83 airports of Japan with a height in the data, it is number 24 from the top. It is about 3,630 km north of the equator.
