Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, LCR means Virgilio Barco Vargas (La Chorrera) Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SKHZ. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.
