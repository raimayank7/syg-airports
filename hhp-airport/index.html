Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, HHP means Shun Tak Heliport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VHSS. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 22.2889° N, 114.1524° E. It stands 33 metres (107 feet) above sea level.
