Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BGK means Big Creek Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MZBG. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 16.5194° N, 88.4079° W. It stands 7 metres (22 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Puerto Barrios, about 90 km away in a straight line.
