Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ZEL means Bella Bella (Campbell Island) Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CBBC. ICAO codes that begin with C belong to Canada. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 52.1850° N, 128.1570° W. It stands 43 metres (141 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Bella Bella (Campbell Island) Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,128 metres (3,700 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 245 airports of Canada on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 171.
