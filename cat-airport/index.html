Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CAT means Cascais Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: LPCS. ICAO codes that begin with L belong to southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 38.7250° N, 9.3552° W. It stands 99 metres (325 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 17/35, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 17 is heading about 170° on the compass, and one using the other end about 350°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 99 metres, Cascais Airport stands higher than about 53% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 19 airports of Portugal with a height in the data, it is number 6 from the top. It is about 4,310 km north of the equator.
