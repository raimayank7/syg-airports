Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, RTM means Rotterdam The Hague Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: EHRD. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 51.9569° N, 4.4372° E. It stands 5 metres (15 feet) below sea level.
