Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, NUL means Nulato Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: PANU. ICAO codes that begin with P belong to the North Pacific, including Alaska and Hawaii. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 64.7293° N, 158.0740° W. It stands 122 metres (399 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Nulato Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,223 metres (4,011 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 675 airports of United States on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 533.
