Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MLX means Malatya Erhaç Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: LTAT. ICAO codes that begin with L belong to southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 38.4353° N, 38.0910° E. It stands 862 metres (2,828 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 03/21, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 03 is heading about 30° on the compass, and one using the other end about 210°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 862 metres, Malatya Erhaç Airport stands higher than about 88% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 51 airports of Turkey with a height in the data, it is number 19 from the top. It is about 4,270 km north of the equator.
