Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PUD means Puerto Deseado Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SAWD. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 47.7353° S, 65.9041° W. It stands 82 metres (268 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Puerto Deseado Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,500 metres (4,921 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 45 airports of Argentina on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 43.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 6/24, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 6 is heading about 60° on the compass, and one using the other end about 240°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 82 metres, Puerto Deseado Airport stands higher than about 50% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 45 airports of Argentina with a height in the data, it is number 26 from the top. It is about 5,310 km south of the equator.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Puerto Deseado Airport there are 2 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. That is few: the region is thinly served by air. The nearest large airport is General Enrique Mosconi International Airport (CRD), about 247 km away.
