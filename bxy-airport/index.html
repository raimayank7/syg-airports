Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BXY means Baikonur Krayniy International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: UAOL. ICAO codes that begin with U belong to Russia and neighbouring states. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 45.6220° N, 63.2108° E. It stands 97 metres (317 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Kyzylorda, about 199 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 05/23, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 05 is heading about 50° on the compass, and one using the other end about 230°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 97 metres, Baikonur Krayniy International Airport stands higher than about 53% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 23 airports of Kazakhstan with a height in the data, it is number 20 from the top. It is about 5,070 km north of the equator.

Other airports around it
