Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MUE means Waimea Kohala Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: PHMU. ICAO codes that begin with P belong to the North Pacific, including Alaska and Hawaii. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 20.0013° N, 155.6680° W. It stands 814 metres (2,671 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Hilo, about 67 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 04/22, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 04 is heading about 40° on the compass, and one using the other end about 220°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

It is about 2,220 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
