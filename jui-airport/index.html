Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, JUI means Juist Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: EDWJ. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 53.6814° N, 7.0564° E. It stands 2 metres (7 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Groningen, about 61 km away in a straight line.
