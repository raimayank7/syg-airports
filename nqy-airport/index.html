Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, NQY means Cornwall Airport Newquay. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: EGHQ. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

The airport lies at 50.4406° N, 4.9954° W. It stands 119 metres (390 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Plymouth, about 61 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 12/30, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 12 is heading about 120° on the compass, and one using the other end about 300°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.
