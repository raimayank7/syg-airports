Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, RBR means Rio Branco-Plácido de Castro International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SBRB. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 9.8690° S, 67.8940° W. It stands 193 metres (633 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 07/25, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 07 is heading about 70° on the compass, and one using the other end about 250°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

It is about 1,100 km south of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
