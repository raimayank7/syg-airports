Airports in Sweden

Airport codes of Sweden: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Sweden has 52 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 38 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Stockholm (ARN), Göteborg (GOT), Kiruna (KRN), Luleå (LLA), Linköping (LPI), Malmö (MMX), Nyköping (NYO), Malung-Sälen (SCR), Umeå (UME), Visby (VBY), Stockholm / Västerås (VST). The longest runway is at Luleå Airport: 3,350 metres. The highest is Scandinavian Mountains Airport, at 490 metres above sea level.

The airports of Sweden in numbers

Of the 52 airports in Sweden that carry an IATA code, 38 have scheduled airline flights and 14 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 11 large airports, 23 medium airports, 4 small airports. That places Sweden at number 25 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Kiruna Airport (KRN) and the most southerly is Malmö Sturup Airport (MMX). The two that are farthest apart are Kiruna Airport and Malmö Sturup Airport: about 1,413 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 10 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Norrbottena län (5), Västerbottens län (5), Dalarnas län (3).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 5 m at Sundsvall-Härnösand Airport to 490 m at Scandinavian Mountains Airport, and the average is about 146 metres above sea level. Of the 38 airports with runway figures, 4 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 13 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Sweden

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with ES: the first letter stands for northern Europe. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is K, which opens 7 of the 52 codes. In 75% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Sweden do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Stockholm, Oslo). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.

From Sweden to the rest of the world

Measured from Stockholm-Arlanda Airport (ARN), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Mariehamn Airport (MHQ, Finland) at 122 km, Turku Airport (TKU, Finland) at 259 km, Kärdla Airport (KDL, Estonia) at 287 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 5,583 km (about 7 h 15 min); Mumbai 6,247 km (about 8 h 00 min); Dubai 4,785 km (about 6 h 15 min); London Heathrow 1,463 km (about 2 h 15 min); New York JFK 6,293 km (about 8 h 05 min); Los Angeles 8,864 km (about 11 h 10 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
