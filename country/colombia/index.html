Airports in Colombia

Airport codes of Colombia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Colombia has 162 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 50 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are San Andrés (ADZ), Barranquilla (BAQ), Bogota (BOG), Cali (CLO), Cartagena (CTG), Medellín (MDE). The longest runway is at El Dorado International Airport: 3,800 metres. The highest is San Luis Airport, at 2,976 metres above sea level.
