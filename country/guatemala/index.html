Airport codes of Guatemala: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Guatemala has 21 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 6 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Guatemala City (GUA). The longest runway is at Mundo Maya International Airport: 3,000 metres. The highest is Quezaltenango Airport, at 2,371 metres above sea level.
