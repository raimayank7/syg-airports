The longest runway is at Mundo Maya International Airport: 3,000 metres. The highest is Quezaltenango Airport, at 2,371 metres above sea level.

The airports of Guatemala in numbers

Of the 21 airports in Guatemala that carry an IATA code, 6 have scheduled airline flights and 15 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 3 medium airports, 2 small airports. That places Guatemala at number 90 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Mundo Maya International Airport (FRS) and the most southerly is Retalhuleu Airport (RER). The two that are farthest apart are Puerto Barrios Airport and Retalhuleu Airport: about 360 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Quetzaltenango Department (1), Petén Department (1), Guatemala Department (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 10 m at Puerto Barrios Airport to 2,371 m at Quezaltenango Airport, and the average is about 1,015 metres above sea level. 3 of them stand above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 5 airports with runway figures, 3 reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Guatemala

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with MG: the first letter stands for Mexico and Central America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is P, which opens 4 of the 21 codes. In 76% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Guatemala with flights keeps the same clock time (zone America/Guatemala), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
