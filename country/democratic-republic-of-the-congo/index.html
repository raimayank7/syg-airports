Airports in Democratic Republic of the Congo

Airport codes of Democratic Republic of the Congo: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Democratic Republic of the Congo has 60 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 20 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Lubumbashi (FBM), Kinshasa (FIH), Kisangani (FKI), Goma (GOM). The longest runway is at Ndjili International Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is Goma International Airport, at 1,551 metres above sea level.

The airports of Democratic Republic of the Congo in numbers

Of the 60 airports in Democratic Republic of the Congo that carry an IATA code, 20 have scheduled airline flights and 40 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports, 12 medium airports, 4 small airports. That places Democratic Republic of the Congo at number 43 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Gbadolite Airport (BDT) and the most southerly is Lubumbashi International Airport (FBM). The two that are farthest apart are Gbadolite Airport and Lubumbashi International Airport: about 1,905 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 50 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Kwilu Province (2), Nord-Ubangi Province (1), Tshuapa Province (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 313 m at Ndjili International Airport to 1,551 m at Goma International Airport, and the average is about 695 metres above sea level. 2 of them stand above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 19 airports with runway figures, 4 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 3 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 4 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft. The main runway is hard (asphalt or concrete) at 16, unpaved (gravel, grass, earth, coral or similar) at 3.

The codes used in Democratic Republic of the Congo

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with FZ: the first letter stands for central and southern Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is K, which opens 16 of the 60 codes. In 83% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Democratic Republic of the Congo do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Lubumbashi, Kinshasa). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.
