Airports in Botswana

Airport codes of Botswana: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Botswana has 15 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 5 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Kasane (BBK), Francistown (FRW), Gaborone (GBE), Maun (MUB). The longest runway is at Sir Seretse Khama International Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is Shakawe Airport, at 1,030 metres above sea level.

The airports of Botswana in numbers

Of the 15 airports in Botswana that carry an IATA code, 5 have scheduled airline flights and 10 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports, 1 small airport. That places Botswana at number 107 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Kasane International Airport (BBK) and the most southerly is Sir Seretse Khama International Airport (GBE). The two that are farthest apart are Sir Seretse Khama International Airport and Shakawe Airport: about 807 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 30 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in North West District (2), Chobe District (1), Francistown (city) (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 943 m at Maun International Airport to 1,030 m at Shakawe Airport, and the average is about 996 metres above sea level. Of the 5 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 1 more reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Botswana

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with FB: the first letter stands for central and southern Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is S, which opens 3 of the 15 codes. In 87% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name.
