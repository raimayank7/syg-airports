Airports in Venezuela

Airport codes of Venezuela: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Venezuela has 61 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 22 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Barcelona (BLA), Barquisimeto (BRM), Maiquetía (CCS), Maracaibo (MAR), Isla Margarita (PMV), Guyana City (PZO), Valencia (VLN). The longest runway is at Maiquetía Simón Bolívar International Airport: 3,500 metres. The highest is Santa Elena de Uairén Airport, at 896 metres above sea level.

The airports of Venezuela in numbers

Of the 61 airports in Venezuela that carry an IATA code, 22 have scheduled airline flights and 39 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 7 large airports, 13 medium airports, 2 small airports. That places Venezuela at number 40 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Los Roques Airport (LRV) and the most southerly is Santa Elena de Uairén Airport (SNV). The two that are farthest apart are La Chinita International Airport and Santa Elena de Uairén Airport: about 1,344 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 05 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Bolívar State (3), Anzoátegui State (2), Sucre State (2).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Andrés Miguel Salazar Marcano Airport to 896 m at Santa Elena de Uairén Airport, and the average is about 215 metres above sea level. Of the 22 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 8 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 2 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Venezuela

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with SV: the first letter stands for South America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is S, which opens 11 of the 61 codes. In 79% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Venezuela with flights keeps the same clock time (zone America/Caracas), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Venezuela to the rest of the world

Measured from Maiquetía Simón Bolívar International Airport (CCS), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Flamingo International Airport (BON, Caribbean Netherlands) at 220 km, Hato International Airport (CUR, Curaçao) at 278 km, Queen Beatrix International Airport (AUA, Aruba) at 391 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 14,198 km (about 17 h 35 min); Mumbai 14,515 km (about 18 h 00 min); Dubai 12,611 km (about 15 h 40 min); London Heathrow 7,471 km (about 9 h 30 min); New York JFK 3,406 km (about 4 h 35 min); Los Angeles 5,817 km (about 7 h 30 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
