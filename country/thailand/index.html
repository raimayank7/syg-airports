Airports in Thailand

Airport codes of Thailand: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Thailand has 43 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 33 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Bangkok (BKK), Chiang Rai (CEI), Chiang Mai (CNX), Bangkok (DMK), Hat Yai (HDY), Phuket (HKT), Krabi (KBV), Na Thon (Ko Samui Island) (USM), Udon Thani (UTH), Rayong (UTP). The longest runway is at Suvarnabhumi Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is Mae Fah Luang - Chiang Rai International Airport, at 390 metres above sea level.
