Airports in Honduras

Airport codes of Honduras: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Honduras has 29 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 8 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Coxen Hole (RTB), San Pedro Sula (SAP), Palmerola (XPL). The longest runway is at Golosón International Airport: 3,010 metres. The highest is Toncontín Airport, at 1,004 metres above sea level.

The airports of Honduras in numbers

Of the 29 airports in Honduras that carry an IATA code, 8 have scheduled airline flights and 21 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports, 3 medium airports, 2 small airports. That places Honduras at number 82 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is La Laguna Airport (GJA) and the most southerly is Toncontín Airport (TGU). The two that are farthest apart are Puerto Lempira Airport and Ramón Villeda Morales International Airport: about 445 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 00 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Islas de la Bahía Department (3), Atlántida Department (1), Gracias a Dios Department (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 9 m at Utila Airport to 1,004 m at Toncontín Airport, and the average is about 215 metres above sea level. Of the 6 airports with runway figures, 3 reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 1 has nothing longer than 1,500 metres and takes mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Honduras

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with MH: the first letter stands for Mexico and Central America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is L, which opens 5 of the 29 codes. In 83% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name.
