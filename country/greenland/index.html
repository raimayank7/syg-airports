Airports in Greenland

Airport codes of Greenland: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Greenland has 60 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 56 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Nuuk (GOH), Ilulissat (JAV). The longest runway is at Kangerlussuaq International Airport: 2,810 metres. The highest is Qaqortoq Airport, at 154 metres above sea level.
