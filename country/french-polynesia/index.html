The airports of French Polynesia in numbers

Of the 51 airports in French Polynesia that carry an IATA code, 38 have scheduled airline flights and 13 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 24 medium airports, 13 small airports. That places French Polynesia at number 23 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Nuku Hiva Airport (NHV) and the most southerly is Raivavae Airport (RVV). The two that are farthest apart are Rimatara Airport and Ua Huka Airport: about 2,078 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 3 h 00 min in a jet.

Heights and runways

Heights run from 1 m at Fakahina Airport to 451 m at Hiva Oa-Atuona Airport, and the average is about 18 metres above sea level. Of the 31 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 26 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in French Polynesia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with NT: the first letter stands for the South Pacific. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is R, which opens 8 of the 51 codes. In 78% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of French Polynesia with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Pacific/Tahiti), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From French Polynesia to the rest of the world

Measured from Fa'a'ā International Airport (PPT), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Mauke Airport (MUK, Cook Islands) at 863 km, Mitiaro Island Airport (MOI, Cook Islands) at 890 km, Enua Airport (AIU, Cook Islands) at 935 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 15,115 km (about 18 h 45 min); Mumbai 15,539 km (about 19 h 15 min); Dubai 17,297 km (about 21 h 20 min); London Heathrow 15,370 km (about 19 h 00 min); New York JFK 10,130 km (about 12 h 40 min); Los Angeles 6,611 km (about 8 h 30 min).
