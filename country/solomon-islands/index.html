The airports of Solomon Islands in numbers

Of the 37 airports in Solomon Islands that carry an IATA code, 20 have scheduled airline flights and 17 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 1 medium airport, 18 small airports. That places Solomon Islands at number 42 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Choiseul Bay Airport (CHY) and the most southerly is Rennell/Tingoa Airport (RNL). The two that are farthest apart are Mono Airport and Santa Cruz/Graciosa Bay/Luova Airport: about 1,182 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 55 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Western Province (6), Malaita Province (2), Rennell and Bellona Province (2).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 1 m at Santa Ana Airport to 168 m at Rennell/Tingoa Airport, and the average is about 17 metres above sea level. Of the 18 airports with runway figures, none has a runway of 2,300 metres, the length most passenger jets need; 16 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft. The main runway is unpaved (gravel, grass, earth, coral or similar) at 10, not stated at 6, hard (asphalt or concrete) at 2.

The codes used in Solomon Islands

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with AG: the first letter stands for the western South Pacific. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is R, which opens 5 of the 37 codes. In 65% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Solomon Islands do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Guadalcanal, Bougainville). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.

From Solomon Islands to the rest of the world

Measured from Honiara International Airport (HIR), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Aropa Airport (KIE, Papua New Guinea) at 590 km, Buka Airport (BUA, Papua New Guinea) at 742 km, Misima Island Airport (MIS, Papua New Guinea) at 802 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 9,830 km (about 12 h 20 min); Mumbai 10,057 km (about 12 h 35 min); Dubai 11,922 km (about 14 h 50 min); London Heathrow 15,014 km (about 18 h 35 min); New York JFK 13,706 km (about 17 h 00 min); Los Angeles 9,823 km (about 12 h 20 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
