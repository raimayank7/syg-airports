Airports in Israel

Airport codes of Israel: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Israel has 11 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 3 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Eilat (ETM), Tel Aviv (TLV). The longest runway is at Ben Gurion International Airport: 4,062 metres. The highest is Ramon International Airport, at 88 metres above sea level.

The airports of Israel in numbers

Of the 11 airports in Israel that carry an IATA code, 3 have scheduled airline flights and 8 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 2 large airports, 1 medium airport. That places Israel at number 146 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The airports with flights are Eilat (ETM), Haifa (HFA), Tel Aviv (TLV).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 9 m at Uri Michaeli Haifa International Airport to 88 m at Ramon International Airport, and the average is about 46 metres above sea level. Of the 3 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 1 has nothing longer than 1,500 metres and takes mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Israel

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LL: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is E, which opens 2 of the 11 codes. In 73% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of Israel with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Asia/Jerusalem), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Israel to the rest of the world

Measured from Ben Gurion International Airport (TLV), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Marka International (Amman Civil) Airport (ADJ, Jordan) at 104 km, Queen Alia International Airport (AMM, Jordan) at 109 km, El Arish International Airport (AAC, Egypt) at 146 km.
