The airports of Turks and Caicos Islands in numbers

Of the 7 airports in Turks and Caicos Islands that carry an IATA code, 5 have scheduled airline flights and 2 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 3 medium airports, 1 small airport. That places Turks and Caicos Islands at number 116 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is North Caicos Airport (NCA) and the most southerly is Salt Cay Airport (SLX). The two that are farthest apart are JAGS McCartney International Airport and Providenciales International Airport: about 122 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Grand Turk (1), North Caicos (1), Providenciales (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 1 m at Salt Cay Airport to 5 m at Providenciales International Airport, and the average is about 3 metres above sea level. Of the 4 airports with runway figures, 1 reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 1 has nothing longer than 1,500 metres and takes mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Turks and Caicos Islands

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with MB: the first letter stands for Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of Turks and Caicos Islands with flights keeps the same clock time (zone America/Grand Turk), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

Measured from Providenciales International Airport (PLS), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Mayaguana Airport (MYG, Bahamas) at 102 km, Inagua Airport (IGA, Bahamas) at 170 km, Spring Point Airport (AXP, Bahamas) at 190 km.
