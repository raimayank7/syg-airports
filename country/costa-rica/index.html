Airports in Costa Rica

Airport codes of Costa Rica: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Costa Rica has 30 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 9 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Liberia (LIR), San José (Alajuela) (SJO). The longest runway is at Juan Santamaría International Airport: 3,012 metres. The highest is Tobías Bolaños International Airport, at 1,002 metres above sea level.

The airports of Costa Rica in numbers

Of the 30 airports in Costa Rica that carry an IATA code, 9 have scheduled airline flights and 21 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 2 large airports, 7 medium airports. That places Costa Rica at number 76 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Daniel Oduber Quirós International Airport (LIR) and the most southerly is Puerto Jimenez Airport (PJM). The two that are farthest apart are Golfito Airport and Daniel Oduber Quirós International Airport: about 337 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Puntarenas Province (3), Alajuela Province (2), Guanacaste Province (2).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Limón International Airport to 1,002 m at Tobías Bolaños International Airport, and the average is about 240 metres above sea level. Of the 9 airports with runway figures, 2 reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 5 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Costa Rica

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with MR: the first letter stands for Mexico and Central America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is P, which opens 4 of the 30 codes. In 50% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Costa Rica do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Costa Rica, Panama). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.

From Costa Rica to the rest of the world

Measured from Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Changuinola Captain Manuel Niño International Airport (CHX, Panama) at 195 km, Bocas del Toro "Isla Colón" International Airport (BOC, Panama) at 227 km, Bluefields Airport (BEF, Nicaragua) at 227 km.
