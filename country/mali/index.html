Airports in Mali

Airport codes of Mali: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Mali has 11 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 5 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Bamako (BKO), Timbuktu (TOM). The longest runway is at Modibo Keita International Airport: 3,200 metres. The highest is Modibo Keita International Airport, at 380 metres above sea level.

The airports of Mali in numbers

Of the 11 airports in Mali that carry an IATA code, 5 have scheduled airline flights and 6 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 2 large airports, 3 medium airports. That places Mali at number 110 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Tombouktou Airport (TOM) and the most southerly is Modibo Keita International Airport (BKO). The two that are farthest apart are Gao International Airport and Kayes Dag Dag Airport: about 1,237 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 00 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Gao Region (2), Koulikoro Region (1), Kayes Region (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 50 m at Kayes Dag Dag Airport to 380 m at Modibo Keita International Airport, and the average is about 247 metres above sea level. Of the 5 airports with runway figures, 4 reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Mali

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with GA: the first letter stands for West Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is K, which opens 3 of the 11 codes. In 64% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Mali with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Bamako), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Mali to the rest of the world

Measured from Modibo Keita International Airport (BKO), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Bobo Dioulasso Airport (BOY, Burkina Faso) at 422 km, Korhogo Airport (HGO, Côte d'Ivoire) at 437 km, Bouaké Airport (BYK, Côte d'Ivoire) at 619 km.
