Airports in South Korea

Airport codes of South Korea: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

South Korea has 28 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 15 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Cheongju (CJJ), Jeju City (CJU), Seoul (GMP), Seoul (ICN), Muan (Piseo-ri) (MWX), Busan (PUS), Daegu (TAE), Gonghang-ro (YNY). The longest runway is at Incheon International Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is Wonju Airport / Hoengseong Air Base (K-38/K-46), at 100 metres above sea level.
