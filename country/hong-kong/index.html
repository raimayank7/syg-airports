Airports in Hong Kong

Airport codes of Hong Kong: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Hong Kong has 2 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 2 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Hong Kong (HKG). The longest runway is at Hong Kong International Airport: 3,800 metres. The highest is Shun Tak Heliport, at 33 metres above sea level.

The airports of Hong Kong in numbers

Of the 2 airports in Hong Kong that carry an IATA code, 2 have scheduled airline flights. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 1 heliport. That places Hong Kong at number 169 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The two airports with flights, Shun Tak Heliport (HHP) and Hong Kong International Airport (HKG), are about 25 km apart in a straight line.

Heights and runways

The longest runway, at Hong Kong International Airport, measures 3,800 metres: long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft.

The codes used in Hong Kong

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with VH: the first letter stands for South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of Hong Kong with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Asia/Hong Kong), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
