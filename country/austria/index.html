Airports in Austria

Airport codes of Austria: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Austria has 8 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 6 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Feldkirchen bei Graz (GRZ), Innsbruck (INN), Klagenfurt am Wörthersee (KLU), Linz (LNZ), Salzburg (SZG), Vienna (VIE). The longest runway is at Vienna International Airport: 3,600 metres. The highest is Innsbruck Airport, at 581 metres above sea level.

The airports of Austria in numbers

Of the 8 airports in Austria that carry an IATA code, 6 have scheduled airline flights and 2 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 6 large airports. That places Austria at number 101 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Linz-Hörsching Airport (LNZ) and the most southerly is Klagenfurt Airport (KLU). The two that are farthest apart are Innsbruck Airport and Vienna International Airport: about 402 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 00 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Styria (1), Tyrol (1), Carinthia (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 183 m at Vienna International Airport to 581 m at Innsbruck Airport, and the average is about 380 metres above sea level. Of the 6 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 4 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Austria

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LO: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is G, which opens 1 of the 8 codes. In 75% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Austria with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Europe/Vienna), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Austria to the rest of the world
