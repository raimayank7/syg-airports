Airports in Madagascar

Airport codes of Madagascar: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Madagascar has 67 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 16 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Mahajanga (MJN), Nosy Be (NOS), Toamasina (TMM), Antananarivo (TNR). The longest runway is at Ivato International Airport: 3,100 metres. The highest is Ivato International Airport, at 1,280 metres above sea level.

The airports of Madagascar in numbers

Of the 67 airports in Madagascar that carry an IATA code, 16 have scheduled airline flights and 51 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports, 9 medium airports, 3 small airports. That places Madagascar at number 55 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Arrachart Airport (DIE) and the most southerly is Tôlanaro Airport (FTU). The two that are farthest apart are Arrachart Airport and Tôlanaro Airport: about 1,432 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 15 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Mahajanga (5), Antsiranana (3), Toliara (3).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Sainte Marie Airport to 1,280 m at Ivato International Airport, and the average is about 119 metres above sea level. Of the 13 airports with runway figures, 1 reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 7 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Madagascar

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with FM: the first letter stands for central and southern Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is W, which opens 19 of the 67 codes. In 43% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Madagascar with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Indian/Antananarivo), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
