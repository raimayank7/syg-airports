Airports in Nepal

Airport codes of Nepal: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Nepal has 43 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 19 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Siddharthanagar (Bhairahawa) (BWA), Kathmandu (KTM), Pokhara (PHH). The longest runway is at Tribhuvan International Airport: 3,350 metres. The highest is Tenzing-Hillary Airport, at 2,859 metres above sea level.

The airports of Nepal in numbers

Of the 43 airports in Nepal that carry an IATA code, 19 have scheduled airline flights and 24 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports, 6 medium airports, 10 small airports. That places Nepal at number 46 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Simikot Airport (IMK) and the most southerly is Biratnagar Airport (BIR). The two that are farthest apart are Bhadrapur Airport and Silgadi Doti Airport: about 763 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 25 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Koshi Province (5), Karnali Province (4), Gandaki Province (3).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 72 m at Biratnagar Airport to 2,859 m at Tenzing-Hillary Airport, and the average is about 1,125 metres above sea level. 6 of them stand above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 16 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 2 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 11 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Nepal

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with VN: the first letter stands for South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is B, which opens 9 of the 43 codes. In 86% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Nepal with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Asia/Kathmandu), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Nepal to the rest of the world

Measured from Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Shigatse Tingri Airport (DDR, China) at 173 km, Darbhanga Airport (DBR, India) at 176 km, Gorakhpur Airport (GOP, India) at 217 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 816 km (about 1 h 30 min); Mumbai 1,592 km (about 2 h 25 min); Dubai 2,990 km (about 4 h 05 min); London Heathrow 7,359 km (about 9 h 20 min); New York JFK 12,117 km (about 15 h 05 min); Los Angeles 12,718 km (about 15 h 50 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
