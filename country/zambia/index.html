Airports in Zambia

Airport codes of Zambia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Zambia has 24 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 6 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Lusaka (LUN), Livingstone (LVI), Mfuwe (MFU), Ndola (NLA). The longest runway is at Kenneth Kaunda International Airport: 3,962 metres. The highest is Solwesi Airport, at 1,387 metres above sea level.

The airports of Zambia in numbers

Of the 24 airports in Zambia that carry an IATA code, 6 have scheduled airline flights and 18 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports, 2 small airports. That places Zambia at number 102 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Kasama Airport (KAA) and the most southerly is Harry Mwanga Nkumbula International Airport (LVI). The two that are farthest apart are Kasama Airport and Harry Mwanga Nkumbula International Airport: about 1,021 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 45 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Northern Province (1), Lusaka Province (1), Southern Province (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 565 m at Mfuwe International Airport to 1,387 m at Solwesi Airport, and the average is about 1,135 metres above sea level. Of the 6 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 2 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets. The main runway is hard (asphalt or concrete) at 5, unpaved (gravel, grass, earth, coral or similar) at 1.

The codes used in Zambia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with FL: the first letter stands for central and southern Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is K, which opens 4 of the 24 codes. In 79% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Zambia with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Lusaka), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
