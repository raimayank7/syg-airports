The airports of Central African Republic in numbers

Of the 22 airports in Central African Republic that carry an IATA code, 1 has scheduled airline flights and 21 do not. That places Central African Republic at number 194 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Heights and runways

Bangui M'Poko International Airport stands 368 metres above sea level. The longest runway, at Bangui M'Poko International Airport, measures 2,600 metres: enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Central African Republic

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with FE: the first letter stands for central and southern Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is B, which opens 11 of the 22 codes. In 82% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name.
