Airports in Switzerland

Airport codes of Switzerland: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Switzerland has 10 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 5 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Geneva (GVA), Zurich (ZRH). The longest runway is at Geneva International Airport: 3,900 metres. The highest is Bern Airport, at 509 metres above sea level.

The airports of Switzerland in numbers

Of the 10 airports in Switzerland that carry an IATA code, 5 have scheduled airline flights and 5 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 2 large airports, 3 medium airports. That places Switzerland at number 104 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Sankt Gallen Altenrhein Airport (ACH) and the most southerly is Lugano Airport (LUG). The two that are farthest apart are Sankt Gallen Altenrhein Airport and Geneva International Airport: about 297 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in St. Gallen (1), Bern (1), Geneva (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 279 m at Lugano Airport to 509 m at Bern Airport, and the average is about 410 metres above sea level. Of the 5 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 2 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Switzerland

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LS: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is B, which opens 2 of the 10 codes. In 70% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name.
