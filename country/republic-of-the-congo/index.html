Airports in Republic of the Congo

Airport codes of Republic of the Congo: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Republic of the Congo has 25 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 2 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Brazzaville (BZV), Pointe Noire (PNR). The longest runway is at Maya-Maya International Airport: 3,300 metres. The highest is Maya-Maya International Airport, at 319 metres above sea level.

The airports of Republic of the Congo in numbers

Of the 25 airports in Republic of the Congo that carry an IATA code, 2 have scheduled airline flights and 23 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 2 large airports. That places Republic of the Congo at number 160 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The two airports with flights, Maya-Maya International Airport (BZV) and Antonio Agostinho-Neto International Airport (PNR), are about 378 km apart in a straight line.

Heights and runways

The longest runway, at Maya-Maya International Airport, measures 3,300 metres: long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft.

The codes used in Republic of the Congo

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with FC: the first letter stands for central and southern Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is B, which opens 3 of the 25 codes. In 88% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Republic of the Congo with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Brazzaville), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
