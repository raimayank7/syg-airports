Airports in Argentina

Airport codes of Argentina: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Argentina has 103 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 45 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Buenos Aires (AEP), San Carlos de Bariloche (BRC), Cordoba (COR), Comodoro Rivadavia (CRD), Buenos Aires (Ezeiza) (EZE), San Salvador de Jujuy (JUJ), Mendoza (MDZ), Neuquén (NQN), Resistencia (RES), Rio Gallegos (RGL), Rosario (ROS), Salta (SLA) and 1 more. The longest runway is at Teniente Benjamín Matienzo International Airport: 3,500 metres. The highest is Martín Miguel de Güemes International Airport, at 1,246 metres above sea level.
