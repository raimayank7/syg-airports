Airports in Iran

Airport codes of Iran: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Iran has 77 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 45 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Abadan (ABD), Ahvaz (AWZ), Bandar Abbas (BND), Qeshm(Dayrestan) (GSM), Isfahan (IFN), Tehran (IKA), Kerman (KER), Kish Island (KIH), Mashhad (MHD), Karaj (PYK), Shiraz (SYZ), Tabriz (TBZ) and 3 more. The longest runway is at Bushehr Airport: 4,470 metres. The highest is Hamadan Airport, at 1,754 metres above sea level.
