Airports in Rwanda

Airport codes of Rwanda: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Rwanda has 5 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 2 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Kigali (KGL). The longest runway is at Kigali International Airport: 3,500 metres. The highest is Kamembe Airport, at 1,583 metres above sea level.

The airports of Rwanda in numbers

Of the 5 airports in Rwanda that carry an IATA code, 2 have scheduled airline flights and 3 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 1 medium airport. That places Rwanda at number 171 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The two airports with flights, Kigali International Airport (KGL) and Kamembe Airport (KME), are about 147 km apart in a straight line.

Heights and runways

The longest runway, at Kigali International Airport, measures 3,500 metres: long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft.

The codes used in Rwanda

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with HR: the first letter stands for east and north-east Africa. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of Rwanda with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Kigali), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
