Airports in Serbia

Airport codes of Serbia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Serbia has 5 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 3 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Belgrade (BEG), Niš (INI). The longest runway is at Belgrade Nikola Tesla Airport: 3,500 metres. The highest is Morava Airport, at 209 metres above sea level.

The airports of Serbia in numbers

Of the 5 airports in Serbia that carry an IATA code, 3 have scheduled airline flights and 2 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 2 large airports, 1 medium airport. That places Serbia at number 139 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The airports with flights are Belgrade (BEG), Niš (INI), Kraljevo (KVO).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 102 m at Belgrade Nikola Tesla Airport to 209 m at Morava Airport, and the average is about 170 metres above sea level. Of the 3 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 1 more reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Serbia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LY: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of Serbia with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Europe/Belgrade), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
