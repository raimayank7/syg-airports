Airports in Slovakia

Airport codes of Slovakia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

R. Štefánik Airport: 3,190 metres. The highest is Poprad-Tatry Airport, at 718 metres above sea level.

The airports of Slovakia in numbers

Of the 8 airports in Slovakia that carry an IATA code, 4 have scheduled airline flights and 4 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 3 medium airports. That places Slovakia at number 126 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Poprad-Tatry Airport (TAT) and the most southerly is M. R. Štefánik Airport (BTS). The two that are farthest apart are M. R. Štefánik Airport and Košice International Airport: about 302 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Bratislava Region (1), Košice Region (1), Banská Bystrica Region (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 133 m at M. R. Štefánik Airport to 718 m at Poprad-Tatry Airport, and the average is about 350 metres above sea level. Of the 4 airports with runway figures, 4 reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Slovakia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LZ: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is P, which opens 2 of the 8 codes. In 75% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Slovakia with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Europe/Bratislava), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
