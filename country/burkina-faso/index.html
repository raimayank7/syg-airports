Airports in Burkina Faso

Airport codes of Burkina Faso: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Burkina Faso has 27 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 2 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Bobo Dioulasso (BOY), Ouagadougou (OUA). The longest runway is at Bobo Dioulasso Airport: 3,300 metres. The highest is Bobo Dioulasso Airport, at 461 metres above sea level.
