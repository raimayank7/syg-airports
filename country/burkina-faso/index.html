Airports in Burkina Faso

Airport codes of Burkina Faso: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Burkina Faso has 27 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 2 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Bobo Dioulasso (BOY), Ouagadougou (OUA). The longest runway is at Bobo Dioulasso Airport: 3,300 metres. The highest is Bobo Dioulasso Airport, at 461 metres above sea level.

The airports of Burkina Faso in numbers

Of the 27 airports in Burkina Faso that carry an IATA code, 2 have scheduled airline flights and 25 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 2 large airports. That places Burkina Faso at number 159 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The two airports with flights, Bobo Dioulasso Airport (BOY) and Ouagadougou Thomas Sankara International Airport (OUA), are about 334 km apart in a straight line.

Heights and runways

The longest runway, at Bobo Dioulasso Airport, measures 3,300 metres: long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft.

The codes used in Burkina Faso

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with DF: the first letter stands for West Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is X, which opens 14 of the 27 codes. In 48% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of Burkina Faso with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Ouagadougou), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
