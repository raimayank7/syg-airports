Airports in Italy

Airport codes of Italy: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Italy has 51 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 42 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Brindisi (BDS), Orio al Serio (BG) (BGY), Bologna (BLQ), Bari (BRI), Cagliari (CAG), Rome (CIA), Catania (CTA), Rome (FCO), Firenze (FI) (FLR), Genova (GE) (GOA), Segrate (MI) (LIN), Ferno (VA) (MXP) and 13 more. The longest runway is at Milan Malpensa International Airport: 3,920 metres. The highest is Cuneo International Airport, at 386 metres above sea level.

The airports of Italy in numbers

Of the 51 airports in Italy that carry an IATA code, 42 have scheduled airline flights and 9 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 25 large airports, 16 medium airports, 1 heliport. That places Italy at number 21 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Bolzano Airport (BZO) and the most southerly is Lampedusa Airport (LMP). The two that are farthest apart are Bolzano Airport and Lampedusa Airport: about 1,224 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 00 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Sicily (6), Apulia (4), Lombardy (4).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Pisa International Airport to 386 m at Cuneo International Airport, and the average is about 79 metres above sea level. Of the 41 airports with runway figures, 5 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 23 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 1 has nothing longer than 1,500 metres and takes mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Italy

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LI: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is B, which opens 6 of the 51 codes. In 63% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Italy do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Rome, Tunis). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.

From Italy to the rest of the world

Measured from Rome–Fiumicino Leonardo da Vinci International Airport (FCO), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Bastia-Poretta International airport (BIA, France) at 243 km, Figari Sud-Corse Airport (FSC, France) at 264 km, Ajaccio Napoléon Bonaparte airport (AJA, France) at 286 km.
