Airports in El Salvador

Airport codes of El Salvador: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

El Salvador has 2 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 2 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are San Salvador (San Luis Talpa) (SAL). The longest runway is at El Salvador International Airport Saint Óscar Arnulfo Romero y Galdámez: 3,200 metres. The highest is Ilopango International Airport, at 618 metres above sea level.
