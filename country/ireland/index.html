Airports in Ireland

Airport codes of Ireland: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Ireland has 15 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 10 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Dublin (DUB), Charlestown (NOC), Cork (ORK), Shannon (SNN). The longest runway is at Shannon Airport: 3,199 metres. The highest is Ireland West Airport Knock, at 203 metres above sea level.

The airports of Ireland in numbers

Of the 15 airports in Ireland that carry an IATA code, 10 have scheduled airline flights and 5 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports, 2 medium airports, 4 small airports. That places Ireland at number 73 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Donegal Airport (CFN) and the most southerly is Cork International Airport (ORK). The two that are farthest apart are Donegal Airport and Cork International Airport: about 356 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in County Galway (4), County Donegal (1), County Dublin (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 5 m at Inishmaan Aerodrome to 203 m at Ireland West Airport Knock, and the average is about 53 metres above sea level. Of the 10 airports with runway figures, 3 reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 5 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Ireland

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with EI: the first letter stands for northern Europe. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is I, which opens 3 of the 15 codes. In 67% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Ireland with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Europe/Dublin), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Ireland to the rest of the world

Measured from Dublin Airport (DUB), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Isle of Man Airport (IOM, Isle of Man) at 130 km, George Best Belfast City Airport (BHD, United Kingdom) at 135 km, Belfast International Airport (BFS, United Kingdom) at 137 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 7,073 km (about 9 h 00 min); Mumbai 7,601 km (about 9 h 40 min); Dubai 5,919 km (about 7 h 40 min); London Heathrow 449 km (about 1 h 00 min); New York JFK 5,103 km (about 6 h 40 min); Los Angeles 8,318 km (about 10 h 30 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
