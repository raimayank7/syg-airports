The airports of Guyana in numbers

Of the 29 airports in Guyana that carry an IATA code, 4 have scheduled airline flights and 25 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 3 medium airports. That places Guyana at number 133 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Eugene F. Correia International Airport (OGL) and the most southerly is Lethem Airport (LTM). The two that are farthest apart are Lethem Airport and Eugene F. Correia International Airport: about 425 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 00 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Demerara-Mahaica (2), Potaro-Siparuni (1), Upper Takutu-Upper Essequibo (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Eugene F. Correia International Airport to 463 m at Kaieteur Airport, and the average is about 151 metres above sea level. Of the 4 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 2 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Guyana

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with SY: the first letter stands for South America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is K, which opens 6 of the 29 codes. In 79% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name.
