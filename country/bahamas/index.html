Airports in Bahamas

Airport codes of Bahamas: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Bahamas has 33 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 23 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Freeport (FPO), Nassau (NAS), San Salvador (ZSA). The longest runway is at Lynden Pindling International Airport: 3,460 metres. The highest is Governor's Harbour Airport, at 8 metres above sea level.

The airports of Bahamas in numbers

Of the 33 airports in Bahamas that carry an IATA code, 23 have scheduled airline flights and 10 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports, 19 medium airports, 1 seaplane base. That places Bahamas at number 37 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Treasure Cay Airport (TCB) and the most southerly is Inagua Airport (IGA). The two that are farthest apart are Grand Bahama International Airport and Inagua Airport: about 804 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 30 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in North Andros (3), Cat Island (2), Bimini (2).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Andros Town Airport to 8 m at Governor's Harbour Airport, and the average is about 3 metres above sea level. Of the 22 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 2 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 4 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Bahamas

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with MY: the first letter stands for Mexico and Central America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is T, which opens 4 of the 33 codes. In 52% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Bahamas do not all keep the same time: they fall in 3 time zone areas (Nassau, Grand Turk, New York). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.

From Bahamas to the rest of the world

Measured from Lynden Pindling International Airport (NAS), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Fort Lauderdale Hollywood International Airport (FLL, United States) at 293 km, Miami International Airport (MIA, United States) at 296 km, Miami-Opa Locka Executive Airport (OPF, United States) at 298 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 13,464 km (about 16 h 45 min); Mumbai 14,152 km (about 17 h 35 min); Dubai 12,468 km (about 15 h 30 min); London Heathrow 6,977 km (about 8 h 55 min); New York JFK 1,768 km (about 2 h 40 min); Los Angeles 4,056 km (about 5 h 25 min).
