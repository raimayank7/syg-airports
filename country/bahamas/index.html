Airports in Bahamas

Airport codes of Bahamas: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Bahamas has 33 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 23 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Freeport (FPO), Nassau (NAS), San Salvador (ZSA). The longest runway is at Lynden Pindling International Airport: 3,460 metres. The highest is Governor's Harbour Airport, at 8 metres above sea level.
