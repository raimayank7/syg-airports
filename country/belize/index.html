The airports of Belize in numbers

Of the 20 airports in Belize that carry an IATA code, 12 have scheduled airline flights and 8 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 7 medium airports, 4 small airports. That places Belize at number 69 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Corozal Airport (CZH) and the most southerly is Punta Gorda Airport (PND). The two that are farthest apart are Corozal Airport and Punta Gorda Airport: about 257 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Stann Creek District (4), Belize District (4), Corozal District (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 1 m at Caye Caulker Airport to 21 m at H.E Alfredo Martinez (Tower Hill) Airstrip, and the average is about 5 metres above sea level. Of the 4 airports with runway figures, 1 reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 3 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Belize

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with MZ: the first letter stands for Mexico and Central America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is S, which opens 5 of the 20 codes. In 80% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Belize do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Belize, Guatemala). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.
