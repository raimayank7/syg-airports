Airports in Belgium

Airport codes of Belgium: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Belgium has 8 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 5 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Zaventem (BRU), Charleroi (CRL), Oostende (OST). The longest runway is at Liège Airport: 3,690 metres. The highest is Liège Airport, at 201 metres above sea level.

The airports of Belgium in numbers

Of the 8 airports in Belgium that carry an IATA code, 5 have scheduled airline flights and 3 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports, 2 medium airports. That places Belgium at number 106 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Ostend-Bruges International Airport (OST) and the most southerly is Brussels South Charleroi Airport (CRL). The two that are farthest apart are Liège Airport and Ostend-Bruges International Airport: about 191 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Antwerp Province (1), Flemish Brabant Province (1), Hainaut Province (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 4 m at Ostend-Bruges International Airport to 201 m at Liège Airport, and the average is about 91 metres above sea level. Of the 5 airports with runway figures, 3 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 1 more reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Belgium

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with EB: the first letter stands for northern Europe. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is O, which opens 2 of the 8 codes. In 50% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Belgium with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Europe/Brussels), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Belgium to the rest of the world

Measured from Brussels Airport (BRU), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Eindhoven Airport (EIN, Netherlands) at 87 km, Maastricht Aachen Airport (MST, Netherlands) at 90 km, Lille Airport (LIL, France) at 104 km.
