Airports in Bosnia and Herzegovina

Airport codes of Bosnia and Herzegovina: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Bosnia and Herzegovina has 4 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 4 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Mahovljani (BNX), Mostar (OMO), Sarajevo (SJJ), Dubrave Gornje (TZL). The longest runway is at Sarajevo International Airport: 2,641 metres. The highest is Sarajevo International Airport, at 521 metres above sea level.

The airports of Bosnia and Herzegovina in numbers

Of the 4 airports in Bosnia and Herzegovina that carry an IATA code, 4 have scheduled airline flights. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports. That places Bosnia and Herzegovina at number 122 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Banja Luka International Airport (BNX) and the most southerly is Mostar International Airport (OMO). The two that are farthest apart are Banja Luka International Airport and Mostar International Airport: about 190 km in a straight line.

Heights and runways

Heights run from 48 m at Mostar International Airport to 521 m at Sarajevo International Airport, and the average is about 232 metres above sea level. Of the 4 airports with runway figures, 4 reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Bosnia and Herzegovina

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LQ: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of Bosnia and Herzegovina with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Europe/Sarajevo), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Bosnia and Herzegovina to the rest of the world

Measured from Sarajevo International Airport (SJJ), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Dubrovnik Ruđer Bošković Airport (DBV, Croatia) at 140 km, Brač Airport (BWK, Croatia) at 146 km, Tivat Airport (TIV, Montenegro) at 161 km.
