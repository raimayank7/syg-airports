Airports in Iraq

Airport codes of Iraq: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Iraq has 12 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 6 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Baghdad (BGW), Basra (BSR), Arbil (EBL), Kirkuk (KIK), Najaf (NJF). The longest runway is at Erbil International Airport: 4,800 metres. The highest is Jalal Talabani International Airport, at 760 metres above sea level.

The airports of Iraq in numbers

Of the 12 airports in Iraq that carry an IATA code, 6 have scheduled airline flights and 6 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 5 large airports, 1 medium airport. That places Iraq at number 94 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Erbil International Airport (EBL) and the most southerly is Basra International Airport (BSR). The two that are farthest apart are Basra International Airport and Erbil International Airport: about 720 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 20 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Baghdad Governorate (1), Basra Governorate (1), Erbil Governorate (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Basra International Airport to 760 m at Jalal Talabani International Airport, and the average is about 260 metres above sea level. Of the 6 airports with runway figures, 4 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 2 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Iraq

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with OR: the first letter stands for the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is B, which opens 3 of the 12 codes. In 42% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Iraq with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Asia/Baghdad), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
