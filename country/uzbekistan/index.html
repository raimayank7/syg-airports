Airports in Uzbekistan

Airport codes of Uzbekistan: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Uzbekistan has 17 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 13 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Bukhara (BHK), Nukus (NCU), Namangan (NMA), Samarkand (SKD), Tashkent (TAS), Urgench (UGC). The longest runway is at Navoi International Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is Samarkand International Airport, at 678 metres above sea level.
