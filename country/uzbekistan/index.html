Airports in Uzbekistan

Airport codes of Uzbekistan: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Uzbekistan has 17 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 13 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Bukhara (BHK), Nukus (NCU), Namangan (NMA), Samarkand (SKD), Tashkent (TAS), Urgench (UGC). The longest runway is at Navoi International Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is Samarkand International Airport, at 678 metres above sea level.

The airports of Uzbekistan in numbers

Of the 17 airports in Uzbekistan that carry an IATA code, 13 have scheduled airline flights and 4 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 6 large airports, 6 medium airports, 1 small airport. That places Uzbekistan at number 63 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Nukus International Airport (NCU) and the most southerly is Termez Airport (TMJ). The two that are farthest apart are Andijan International Airport and Nukus International Airport: about 1,070 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 45 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Tashkent Region (2), Andijan Region (1), Bukhara Region (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 75 m at Nukus International Airport to 678 m at Samarkand International Airport, and the average is about 392 metres above sea level. Of the 13 airports with runway figures, 5 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 7 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 1 has nothing longer than 1,500 metres and takes mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Uzbekistan

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with UZ: the first letter stands for Russia and neighbouring states. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is N, which opens 3 of the 17 codes. In 76% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Uzbekistan do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Samarkand, Tashkent). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.

From Uzbekistan to the rest of the world

Measured from Tashkent International Airport (TAS), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Khujand International Airport (LBD, Tajikistan) at 121 km, Shymkent International Airport (CIT, Kazakhstan) at 124 km, Hazrat Sultan International Airport (HSA, Kazakhstan) at 236 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 1,580 km (about 2 h 25 min); Mumbai 2,488 km (about 3 h 30 min); Dubai 2,194 km (about 3 h 10 min); London Heathrow 5,261 km (about 6 h 50 min); New York JFK 10,176 km (about 12 h 45 min); Los Angeles 11,616 km (about 14 h 30 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
