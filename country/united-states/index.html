The airports of United States in numbers

Of the 2,036 airports in United States that carry an IATA code, 679 have scheduled airline flights and 1357 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 95 large airports, 407 medium airports, 132 small airports, 42 seaplane bases, 3 heliports. That places United States at number 1 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Wiley Post Will Rogers Memorial Airport (BRW) and the most southerly is Hilo International Airport (ITO). The two that are farthest apart are Hancock County-Bar Harbor Airport and Lihue Airport: about 8,393 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 10 h 35 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Alaska (216), California (32), Washington (28).

Heights and runways

Heights run from -16 m at Imperial County Airport to 2,765 m at Telluride Regional Airport, and the average is about 315 metres above sea level. 37 of them stand above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 675 airports with runway figures, 74 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 204 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 171 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft. The main runway is hard (asphalt or concrete) at 495, unpaved (gravel, grass, earth, coral or similar) at 179, not stated at 1.

The codes used in United States

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here do not share one opening: they begin with K (437), P (178). Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is M, which opens 175 of the 2036 codes. In 72% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of United States do not all keep the same time: they fall in 4 time zone areas (Chicago, New York, Los Angeles, Denver). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.
