Airports in Ethiopia

Airport codes of Ethiopia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Ethiopia has 47 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 24 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Addis Ababa (ADD), Hawassa (AWA), Dire Dawa (DIR), Jijiga (JIJ). The longest runway is at Addis Ababa Bole International Airport: 3,800 metres. The highest is Debre Markos Airport, at 2,480 metres above sea level.
