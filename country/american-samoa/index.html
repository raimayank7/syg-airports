Airports in American Samoa

Airport codes of American Samoa: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

American Samoa has 3 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 3 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Pago Pago (PPG). The longest runway is at Pago Pago International Airport: 3,048 metres. The highest is Fitiuta Airport, at 34 metres above sea level.

The airports of American Samoa in numbers

Of the 3 airports in American Samoa that carry an IATA code, 3 have scheduled airline flights. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 2 small airports. That places American Samoa at number 148 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The airports with flights are Fitiuta Village (FTI), Ofu (OFU), Pago Pago (PPG).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Ofu Airport to 34 m at Fitiuta Airport, and the average is about 15 metres above sea level. Of the 3 airports with runway figures, 1 reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 2 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in American Samoa

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with NS: the first letter stands for the South Pacific. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of American Samoa with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Pacific/Pago Pago), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
