The airports of Uganda in numbers

Of the 14 airports in Uganda that carry an IATA code, 6 have scheduled airline flights and 8 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 4 medium airports, 1 small airport. That places Uganda at number 97 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Arua Airport (RUA) and the most southerly is Kisoro Airport (KXO). The two that are farthest apart are Kisoro Airport and Soroti Airport: about 548 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 10 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Western Region (2), Northern Region (2), Central Region (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 1,070 m at Gulu Airport to 1,890 m at Kisoro Airport, and the average is about 1,257 metres above sea level. 1 of them stands above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 6 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 1 more reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 1 has nothing longer than 1,500 metres and takes mainly propeller aircraft. The main runway is hard (asphalt or concrete) at 5, unpaved (gravel, grass, earth, coral or similar) at 1.

The codes used in Uganda

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with HU: the first letter stands for east and north-east Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is K, which opens 5 of the 14 codes. In 71% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Uganda with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Kampala), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Uganda to the rest of the world

Measured from Entebbe International Airport (EBB), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Bukoba Airport (BKZ, Tanzania) at 168 km, Musoma Airport (MUZ, Tanzania) at 229 km, Kisumu International Airport (KIS, Kenya) at 255 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 5,705 km (about 7 h 20 min); Mumbai 4,890 km (about 6 h 25 min); Dubai 3,732 km (about 5 h 00 min); London Heathrow 6,497 km (about 8 h 20 min); New York JFK 11,365 km (about 14 h 10 min); Los Angeles 15,167 km (about 18 h 45 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
