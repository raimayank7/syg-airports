Airports in Romania

Airport codes of Romania: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Romania has 18 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 16 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Bucharest (BBU), Bacău (BCM), Cluj-Napoca (CLJ), Constanța (CND), Craiova (CRA), Brașov (Ghimbav) (GHV), Iaşi (IAS), Oradea (OMR), Otopeni (OTP), Sibiu (SBZ), Suceava (SCV), Timişoara (TSR). The longest runway is at Bucharest Henri Coandă International Airport: 3,500 metres. The highest is Brașov-Ghimbav International Airport, at 530 metres above sea level.

The airports of Romania in numbers

Of the 18 airports in Romania that carry an IATA code, 16 have scheduled airline flights and 2 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 12 large airports, 4 medium airports. That places Romania at number 53 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Satu Mare International Airport (SUJ) and the most southerly is Craiova International Airport (CRA). The two that are farthest apart are Arad International Airport and Mihail Kogălniceanu International Airport: about 600 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 15 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Arad County (1), Maramureş County (1), Bucharest (municipality) (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 91 m at Bucharest Băneasa Aurel Vlaicu International Airport to 530 m at Brașov-Ghimbav International Airport, and the average is about 217 metres above sea level. Of the 16 airports with runway figures, 4 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 8 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Romania

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LR: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is C, which opens 4 of the 18 codes. In 78% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Romania with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Europe/Bucharest), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Romania to the rest of the world

Measured from Bucharest Henri Coandă International Airport (OTP), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Varna Airport (VAR, Bulgaria) at 203 km, Burgas Airport (BOJ, Bulgaria) at 250 km, Plovdiv International Airport (PDV, Bulgaria) at 296 km.
