The airports of Mongolia in numbers

Of the 24 airports in Mongolia that carry an IATA code, 13 have scheduled airline flights and 11 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 10 medium airports, 2 small airports. That places Mongolia at number 62 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Ulaangom Airport (ULO) and the most southerly is Dalanzadgad Airport (DLZ). The two that are farthest apart are Choibalsan Airport and Ölgii Mongolei International Airport: about 1,814 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 40 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Övörkhangai Province (1), Bayankhongor Province (1), Dornod Province (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 749 m at Choibalsan Airport to 2,213 m at Altai Airport, and the average is about 1,553 metres above sea level. 6 of them stand above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 11 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 7 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Mongolia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with ZM: the first letter stands for China, Mongolia and North Korea. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is U, which opens 9 of the 24 codes. In 62% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Mongolia do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Ulaanbaatar, Hovd). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.

From Mongolia to the rest of the world

Measured from Ulaanbaatar Chinggis Khaan International Airport (UBN), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Baikal International Airport (UUD, Russia) at 465 km, Irkutsk International Airport (IKT, Russia) at 542 km, Erenhot Saiwusu International Airport (ERL, China) at 623 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 3,322 km (about 4 h 30 min); Mumbai 4,411 km (about 5 h 50 min); Dubai 5,112 km (about 6 h 40 min); London Heathrow 7,009 km (about 8 h 55 min); New York JFK 10,198 km (about 12 h 45 min); Los Angeles 9,886 km (about 12 h 25 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
