Airports in Mongolia

Airport codes of Mongolia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Mongolia has 24 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 13 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Ulaanbaatar (Sergelen) (UBN). The longest runway is at Ulaanbaatar Chinggis Khaan International Airport: 3,600 metres. The highest is Altai Airport, at 2,213 metres above sea level.
