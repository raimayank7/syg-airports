The airports of Côte d'Ivoire in numbers

Of the 23 airports in Côte d'Ivoire that carry an IATA code, 4 have scheduled airline flights and 19 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 3 medium airports. That places Côte d'Ivoire at number 118 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Korhogo Airport (HGO) and the most southerly is San Pedro Airport (SPY). The two that are farthest apart are Korhogo Airport and San Pedro Airport: about 530 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 10 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Abidjan (autonomous district) (1), Vallée du Bandama District (1), Savanes District (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 6 m at Félix-Houphouët-Boigny International Airport to 375 m at Bouaké Airport, and the average is about 190 metres above sea level. Of the 4 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 1 more reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Côte d'Ivoire

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with DI: the first letter stands for West Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is B, which opens 5 of the 23 codes. In 74% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Every airport of Côte d'Ivoire with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Abidjan), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
