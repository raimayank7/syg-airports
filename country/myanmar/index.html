Airports in Myanmar

Airport codes of Myanmar: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Myanmar has 46 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 23 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Mandalay (MDL), Naypyitaw (NYT), Yangon (RGN). The longest runway is at Mandalay International Airport: 4,268 metres. The highest is Kanti Airport, at 1,829 metres above sea level.
