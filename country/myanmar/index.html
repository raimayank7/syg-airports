Airports in Myanmar

Airport codes of Myanmar: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Myanmar has 46 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 23 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Mandalay (MDL), Naypyitaw (NYT), Yangon (RGN). The longest runway is at Mandalay International Airport: 4,268 metres. The highest is Kanti Airport, at 1,829 metres above sea level.

The airports of Myanmar in numbers

Of the 46 airports in Myanmar that carry an IATA code, 23 have scheduled airline flights and 23 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports, 14 medium airports, 6 small airports. That places Myanmar at number 36 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Putao Airport (PBU) and the most southerly is Kawthoung Airport (KAW). The two that are farthest apart are Kawthoung Airport and Putao Airport: about 1,925 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 50 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Shan State (5), Rakhine State (3), Kachin State (3).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 6 m at Pathein Airport to 1,829 m at Kanti Airport, and the average is about 336 metres above sea level. 1 of them stands above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 22 airports with runway figures, 4 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 5 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 2 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Myanmar

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with VY: the first letter stands for South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is M, which opens 9 of the 46 codes. In 74% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. From Myanmar to the rest of the world

Measured from Mandalay International Airport (MDL), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Mae Hong Son Airport (HGN, Thailand) at 338 km, Cangyuan Washan Airport (CWJ, China) at 390 km, Dehong Mangshi International Airport (LUM, China) at 398 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 2,045 km (about 3 h 00 min); Mumbai 2,424 km (about 3 h 25 min); Dubai 4,145 km (about 5 h 30 min); London Heathrow 8,566 km (about 10 h 50 min); New York JFK 13,002 km (about 16 h 10 min); Los Angeles 12,837 km (about 16 h 00 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
