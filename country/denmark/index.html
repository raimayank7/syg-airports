Airports in Denmark

Airport codes of Denmark: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Denmark has 17 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 11 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Aalborg (AAL), Aarhus (AAR), Billund (BLL), Copenhagen (CPH), Odense (ODE). The longest runway is at Copenhagen Kastrup Airport: 3,600 metres. The highest is Billund Airport, at 75 metres above sea level.

The airports of Denmark in numbers

Of the 17 airports in Denmark that carry an IATA code, 11 have scheduled airline flights and 6 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 5 large airports, 5 medium airports, 1 small airport. That places Denmark at number 71 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Læsø Airport (BYR) and the most southerly is Sønderborg Airport (SGD). The two that are farthest apart are Esbjerg Airport and Bornholm Airport: about 396 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in South Denmark (4), North Jutland (2), Central Jutland (2).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Aalborg Airport to 75 m at Billund Airport, and the average is about 26 metres above sea level. Of the 11 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 5 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 1 has nothing longer than 1,500 metres and takes mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Denmark

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with EK: the first letter stands for northern Europe. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is S, which opens 4 of the 17 codes. In 76% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. From Denmark to the rest of the world

Measured from Copenhagen Kastrup Airport (CPH), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Malmö Sturup Airport (MMX, Sweden) at 46 km, Ängelholm-Helsingborg Airport (AGH, Sweden) at 76 km, Halmstad Airport (HAD, Sweden) at 120 km.
