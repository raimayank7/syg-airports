Airports in Cyprus

Airport codes of Cyprus: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Cyprus has 5 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 3 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Tymbou (Kirklar) (ECN), Larnaca (LCA), Paphos (PFO). The longest runway is at Ercan International Airport: 3,200 metres. The highest is Ercan International Airport, at 123 metres above sea level.

The airports of Cyprus in numbers

Of the 5 airports in Cyprus that carry an IATA code, 3 have scheduled airline flights and 2 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports. That places Cyprus at number 145 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The airports with flights are Tymbou (Kirklar) (ECN), Larnaca (LCA), Paphos (PFO).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Larnaca International Airport to 123 m at Ercan International Airport, and the average is about 46 metres above sea level. Of the 3 airports with runway figures, 3 reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Cyprus

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LC: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of Cyprus with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Asia/Nicosia), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
