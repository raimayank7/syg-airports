Airports in Bangladesh

Airport codes of Bangladesh: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Bangladesh has 12 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 8 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Chattogram (Chittagong) (CGP), Dhaka (DAC), Sylhet (ZYL). The longest runway is at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport: 3,200 metres. The highest is Saidpur Airport, at 38 metres above sea level.

The airports of Bangladesh in numbers

Of the 12 airports in Bangladesh that carry an IATA code, 8 have scheduled airline flights and 4 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports, 5 medium airports. That places Bangladesh at number 81 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Saidpur Airport (SPD) and the most southerly is Cox's Bazar Airport (CXB). The two that are farthest apart are Cox's Bazar Airport and Saidpur Airport: about 571 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 10 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Chittagong Division (2), Rajshahi Division (2), Barisal Division (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 4 m at Shah Amanat International Airport to 38 m at Saidpur Airport, and the average is about 13 metres above sea level. Of the 8 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 3 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Bangladesh

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with VG: the first letter stands for South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is C, which opens 3 of the 12 codes. In 83% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Bangladesh with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Asia/Dhaka), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Bangladesh to the rest of the world

Measured from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Agartala - Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport (IXA, India) at 86 km, Lengpui Airport (AJL, India) at 226 km, Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport (CCU, India) at 239 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 1,426 km (about 2 h 15 min); Mumbai 1,888 km (about 2 h 45 min); Dubai 3,536 km (about 4 h 45 min); London Heathrow 8,015 km (about 10 h 10 min); New York JFK 12,661 km (about 15 h 45 min); Los Angeles 12,904 km (about 16 h 05 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
