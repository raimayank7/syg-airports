Airports in Bangladesh

Airport codes of Bangladesh: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Bangladesh has 12 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 8 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Chattogram (Chittagong) (CGP), Dhaka (DAC), Sylhet (ZYL). The longest runway is at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport: 3,200 metres. The highest is Saidpur Airport, at 38 metres above sea level.
