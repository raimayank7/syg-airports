Airports in Ecuador

Airport codes of Ecuador: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Ecuador has 29 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 15 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Tachina (ESM), Guayaquil (GYE), Salinas/La Libertad (SNC), Quito (UIO). The longest runway is at Mariscal Sucre International Airport: 4,098 metres. The highest is Lieutenant Colonel Luis A. Mantilla International Airport, at 2,941 metres above sea level.

The airports of Ecuador in numbers

Of the 29 airports in Ecuador that carry an IATA code, 15 have scheduled airline flights and 14 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports, 8 medium airports, 3 small airports. That places Ecuador at number 60 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Carlos Concha Torres International Airport (ESM) and the most southerly is Ciudad de Catamayo Airport (LOH). The two that are farthest apart are General Villamil Airport and Francisco De Orellana Airport: about 1,554 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 20 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Galapagos Province (3), Azuay Province (1), Esmeraldas Province (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 5 m at General Ulpiano Paez International Airport to 2,941 m at Lieutenant Colonel Luis A. Mantilla International Airport, and the average is about 890 metres above sea level. 4 of them stand above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 15 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 8 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 1 has nothing longer than 1,500 metres and takes mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Ecuador

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with SE: the first letter stands for South America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is T, which opens 5 of the 29 codes. In 62% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Ecuador with flights keeps the same clock time (zone America/Guayaquil), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Ecuador to the rest of the world

Measured from General Ulpiano Paez International Airport (SNC), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Captain Pedro Canga Rodríguez International Airport (TBP, Peru) at 164 km, Captain Victor Montes Arias International Airport (TYL, Peru) at 265 km, PAF Captain Guillermo Concha Iberico International Airport (PIU, Peru) at 336 km.
