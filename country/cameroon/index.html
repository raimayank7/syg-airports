Airports in Cameroon

Airport codes of Cameroon: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Cameroon has 19 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 5 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Douala (DLA), Garoua (GOU), Yaoundé (NSI). The longest runway is at Yaoundé Nsimalen International Airport: 3,400 metres. The highest is N'Gaoundéré Airport, at 1,114 metres above sea level.

The airports of Cameroon in numbers

Of the 19 airports in Cameroon that carry an IATA code, 5 have scheduled airline flights and 14 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports, 2 medium airports. That places Cameroon at number 113 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Salak Airport (MVR) and the most southerly is Yaoundé Nsimalen International Airport (NSI). The two that are farthest apart are Douala International Airport and Salak Airport: about 874 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 35 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Littoral Region (1), North Region (1), Far North Region (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 10 m at Douala International Airport to 1,114 m at N'Gaoundéré Airport, and the average is about 497 metres above sea level. Of the 5 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 2 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Cameroon

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with FK: the first letter stands for central and southern Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is B, which opens 4 of the 19 codes. In 84% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Cameroon with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Douala), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Cameroon to the rest of the world
