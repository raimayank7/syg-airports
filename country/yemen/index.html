Airports in Yemen

Airport codes of Yemen: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Yemen has 19 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 7 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Aden (ADE), Seiyun (GXF), Mukalla(Riyan) (RIY), Sanaa (SAH). The longest runway is at Socotra Airport: 3,300 metres. The highest is Sanaa International Airport, at 2,199 metres above sea level.

The airports of Yemen in numbers

Of the 19 airports in Yemen that carry an IATA code, 7 have scheduled airline flights and 12 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports, 3 medium airports. That places Yemen at number 83 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Al Ghaydah International Airport (AAY) and the most southerly is Socotra Airport (SCT). The two that are farthest apart are Sanaa International Airport and Socotra Airport: about 1,091 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 50 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Hadhramaut Governorate (2), Al Mahrah Governorate (1), Aden Governorate (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Aden International Airport to 2,199 m at Sanaa International Airport, and the average is about 631 metres above sea level. 1 of them stands above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 7 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 5 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Yemen

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with OY: the first letter stands for the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is A, which opens 3 of the 19 codes. In 53% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Yemen with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Asia/Aden), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Yemen to the rest of the world

Measured from Sanaa International Airport (SAH), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Jizan Regional Airport / King Abdullah bin Abdulaziz Airport (GIZ, Saudi Arabia) at 236 km, Najran Domestic Airport (EAM, Saudi Arabia) at 238 km, Abha International Airport (AHB, Saudi Arabia) at 349 km.
