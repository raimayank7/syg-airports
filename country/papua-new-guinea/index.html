Airports in Papua New Guinea

Airport codes of Papua New Guinea: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Papua New Guinea has 345 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 37 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Lae (LAE), Port Moresby (POM). The longest runway is at Port Moresby Jacksons International Airport: 2,750 metres. The highest is Wapenamanda Airport, at 1,795 metres above sea level.
