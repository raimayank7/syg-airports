Airports in Fiji

Airport codes of Fiji: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Fiji has 27 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 17 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Nadi (NAN), Nausori (SUV). The longest runway is at Nadi International Airport: 3,273 metres. The highest is Koro Island Airport, at 109 metres above sea level.

The airports of Fiji in numbers

Of the 27 airports in Fiji that carry an IATA code, 17 have scheduled airline flights and 10 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 2 large airports, 1 medium airport, 14 small airports. That places Fiji at number 51 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Rotuma Airport (RTA) and the most southerly is Vunisea Airport (KDV). The two that are farthest apart are Lakeba Island Airport and Rotuma Airport: about 773 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 25 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Eastern Division (8), Western Division (4), Northern Division (3).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Vunisea Airport to 109 m at Koro Island Airport, and the average is about 20 metres above sea level. Of the 7 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 5 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft. The main runway is hard (asphalt or concrete) at 3, not stated at 3, unpaved (gravel, grass, earth, coral or similar) at 1.

The codes used in Fiji

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with NF: the first letter stands for the South Pacific. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is L, which opens 4 of the 27 codes. In 63% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Fiji with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Pacific/Fiji), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Fiji to the rest of the world

Measured from Nadi International Airport (NAN), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Pointe Vele Airport (FUT, Wallis and Futuna) at 615 km, Futuna Airport (FTA, Vanuatu) at 784 km, Hihifo Airport (WLS, Wallis and Futuna) at 847 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 11,922 km (about 14 h 50 min); Mumbai 12,124 km (about 15 h 05 min); Dubai 14,008 km (about 17 h 25 min); London Heathrow 16,262 km (about 20 h 05 min); New York JFK 12,849 km (about 16 h 00 min); Los Angeles 8,893 km (about 11 h 15 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
