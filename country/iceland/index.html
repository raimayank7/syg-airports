Airports in Iceland

Airport codes of Iceland: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Iceland has 30 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 9 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Akureyri (AEY), Reykjavík (KEF). The longest runway is at Keflavik International Airport: 3,065 metres. The highest is Keflavik International Airport, at 52 metres above sea level.
