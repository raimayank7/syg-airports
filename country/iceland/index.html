Airports in Iceland

Airport codes of Iceland: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Iceland has 30 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 9 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Akureyri (AEY), Reykjavík (KEF). The longest runway is at Keflavik International Airport: 3,065 metres. The highest is Keflavik International Airport, at 52 metres above sea level.

The airports of Iceland in numbers

Of the 30 airports in Iceland that carry an IATA code, 9 have scheduled airline flights and 21 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 2 large airports, 6 medium airports, 1 small airport. That places Iceland at number 74 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Grímsey Airport (GRY) and the most southerly is Keflavik International Airport (KEF). The two that are farthest apart are Keflavik International Airport and Þórshöfn Airport: about 421 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 00 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Northeastern Region (3), Eastern Region (2), Southern Region (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Akureyri International Airport to 52 m at Keflavik International Airport, and the average is about 16 metres above sea level. Of the 9 airports with runway figures, 2 reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 5 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Iceland

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with BI: the first letter stands for Greenland, Iceland and Kosovo. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is B, which opens 4 of the 30 codes. In 80% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Iceland with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Atlantic/Reykjavik), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Iceland to the rest of the world

Measured from Keflavik International Airport (KEF), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Sermiligaaq Heliport (SGG, Greenland) at 681 km, Kuummiut Heliport (KUZ, Greenland) at 708 km, Kulusuk Airport (KUS, Greenland) at 708 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 7,629 km (about 9 h 40 min); Mumbai 8,369 km (about 10 h 35 min); Dubai 6,919 km (about 8 h 50 min); London Heathrow 1,895 km (about 2 h 45 min); New York JFK 4,163 km (about 5 h 30 min); Los Angeles 6,926 km (about 8 h 50 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
