Airports in Brazil

Airport codes of Brazil: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Brazil has 346 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 129 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Belém (BEL), Porto Seguro (BPS), Brasília (BSB), Boa Vista (BVB), Cuiabá (CGB), São Paulo (CGH), Belo Horizonte (CNF), Curitiba (CWB), Florianópolis (FLN), Fortaleza (FOR), Rio De Janeiro (GIG), São Paulo (GRU) and 16 more. The longest runway is at Rio de Janeiro Galeão – Tom Jobim International Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is Presidente Juscelino Kubitschek International Airport, at 1,066 metres above sea level.
