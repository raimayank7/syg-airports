Airports in South Africa

Airport codes of South Africa: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

South Africa has 90 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 22 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Bloemfontein (BFN), Cape Town (CPT), Durban (DUR), East London (ELS), George (GRJ), Johannesburg (HLA), Johannesburg (JNB), Kimberley (KIM), Mbombela (MQP), Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth) (PLZ), Polokwane (PTG). The longest runway is at Upington Airport: 4,900 metres. The highest is O.R. Tambo International Airport, at 1,694 metres above sea level.
