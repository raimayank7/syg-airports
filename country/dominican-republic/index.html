Airports in Dominican Republic

Airport codes of Dominican Republic: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Dominican Republic has 10 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 8 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are La Romana (LRM), Punta Cana (PUJ), Santo Domingo (SDQ), Santiago (STI). The longest runway is at Las Américas International Airport: 3,353 metres. The highest is Cibao International Airport, at 172 metres above sea level.

The airports of Dominican Republic in numbers

Of the 10 airports in Dominican Republic that carry an IATA code, 8 have scheduled airline flights and 2 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports, 4 medium airports. That places Dominican Republic at number 78 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Gregorio Luperon International Airport (POP) and the most southerly is Maria Montez International Airport (BRX). The two that are farthest apart are Maria Montez International Airport and Punta Cana International Airport: about 293 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Distrito Nacional (2), Samaná Province (1), Barahona Province (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Maria Montez International Airport to 172 m at Cibao International Airport, and the average is about 40 metres above sea level. Of the 8 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 6 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Dominican Republic

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with MD: the first letter stands for Mexico and Central America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is P, which opens 2 of the 10 codes. In 80% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Dominican Republic with flights keeps the same clock time (zone America/Santo Domingo), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
