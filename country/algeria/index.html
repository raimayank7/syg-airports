Airports in Algeria

Airport codes of Algeria: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Algeria has 39 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 31 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Annaba (AAE), Algiers (ALG), Béjaïa (BJA), Batna (BLJ), Biskra (BSK), Chlef (CFK), Constantine (CZL), Djanet (DJG), Tahir (GJL), Es-Sénia (ORN), Zenata (TLM), Tamanrasset (TMR). The longest runway is at Laghouat - Molay Ahmed Medeghri Airport: 3,806 metres. The highest is Aguenar – Hadj Bey Akhamok Airport, at 1,377 metres above sea level.
