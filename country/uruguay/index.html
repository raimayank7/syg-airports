Airports in Uruguay

Airport codes of Uruguay: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Uruguay has 13 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 3 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Ciudad de la Costa (MVD). The longest runway is at Carrasco General Cesáreo L. Berisso International Airport: 3,200 metres. The highest is Pres. Gral. Óscar D. Gestido Binational Airport, at 217 metres above sea level.

The airports of Uruguay in numbers

Of the 13 airports in Uruguay that carry an IATA code, 3 have scheduled airline flights and 10 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 2 medium airports. That places Uruguay at number 151 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The airports with flights are Ciudad de la Costa (MVD), Punta del Este (PDP), Rivera/Santana do Livramento (RVY).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 29 m at Capitan Corbeta CA Curbelo International Airport to 217 m at Pres. Gral. Óscar D. Gestido Binational Airport, and the average is about 93 metres above sea level. Of the 3 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft.

The codes used in Uruguay

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with SU: the first letter stands for South America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is M, which opens 2 of the 13 codes. In 92% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Uruguay with flights keeps the same clock time (zone America/Montevideo), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
