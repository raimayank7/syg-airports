The airports of Vanuatu in numbers

Of the 29 airports in Vanuatu that carry an IATA code, 17 have scheduled airline flights and 12 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 2 medium airports, 14 small airports. That places Vanuatu at number 50 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Sola Airport (SLH) and the most southerly is Futuna Airport (FTA). The two that are farthest apart are Futuna Airport and Sola Airport: about 692 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 20 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Malampa (4), Shefa (4), Penama (4).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Lamen Bay Airport to 150 m at Sara Airport, and the average is about 34 metres above sea level. Of the 16 airports with runway figures, 1 reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 14 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft. The main runway is unpaved (gravel, grass, earth, coral or similar) at 11, hard (asphalt or concrete) at 5.

The codes used in Vanuatu

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with NV: the first letter stands for the South Pacific. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is L, which opens 4 of the 29 codes. In 69% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Vanuatu with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Pacific/Efate), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
