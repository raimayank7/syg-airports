Airports in Germany

Airport codes of Germany: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Germany has 85 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 38 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Berlin (BER), Bremen (BRE), Köln (Cologne) (CGN), Dresden (DRS), Dortmund (DTM), Düsseldorf (DUS), Erfurt (ERF), Friedrichshafen (FDH), Rheinmünster (FKB), Memmingen (FMM), Greven (FMO), Frankfurt am Main (FRA) and 10 more. The longest runway is at Berlin Brandenburg Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is Memmingen Allgau Airport, at 633 metres above sea level.
