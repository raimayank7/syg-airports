The airports of Suriname in numbers

Of the 17 airports in Suriname that carry an IATA code, 6 have scheduled airline flights and 11 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 5 small airports. That places Suriname at number 91 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Eduard Alexander Gummels Airport (EAX) and the most southerly is Drietabbetje Airport (DRJ). The two that are farthest apart are Wageningen Airstrip and Drietabbetje Airport: about 293 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Paramaribo (2), Nickerie (1), Sipaliwini (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Wageningen Airstrip to 72 m at Drietabbetje Airport, and the average is about 21 metres above sea level. Of the 3 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 2 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Suriname

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with SM: the first letter stands for South America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is A, which opens 3 of the 17 codes. In 65% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Suriname do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Paramaribo, Cayenne). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.
