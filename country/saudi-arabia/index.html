Airports in Saudi Arabia

Airport codes of Saudi Arabia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Saudi Arabia has 34 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 29 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Abha (AHB), Al-Jawf (AJF), Qaisumah (AQI), Ad Dammam (DMM), Qassim (ELQ), Hail (HAS), Hofuf (HOF), Jeddah (JED), Medina (MED), Sharma (NUM), Hanak (RSI), Riyadh (RUH) and 4 more. The longest runway is at Prince Mohammad Bin Abdulaziz Airport: 4,335 metres. The highest is Abha International Airport, at 2,090 metres above sea level.

The airports of Saudi Arabia in numbers

Of the 34 airports in Saudi Arabia that carry an IATA code, 29 have scheduled airline flights and 5 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 16 large airports, 13 medium airports. That places Saudi Arabia at number 30 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Turaif Domestic Airport (TUI) and the most southerly is Jizan Regional Airport / King Abdullah bin Abdulaziz Airport (GIZ). The two that are farthest apart are Sharurah Domestic Airport and Gurayat Domestic Airport: about 1,841 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 45 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Ash Sharqiyah Region (4), Tabuk Region (4), Riyadh Region (3).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 6 m at Jizan Regional Airport / King Abdullah bin Abdulaziz Airport to 2,090 m at Abha International Airport, and the average is about 634 metres above sea level. 2 of them stand above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 29 airports with runway figures, 15 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 14 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Saudi Arabia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with OE: the first letter stands for the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is A, which opens 5 of the 34 codes. In 65% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Saudi Arabia with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Asia/Riyadh), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Saudi Arabia to the rest of the world

Measured from King Abdulaziz International Airport (JED), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Port Sudan New International Airport (PZU, Sudan) at 320 km, Marsa Alam International Airport (RMF, Egypt) at 634 km, Aswan International Airport (ASW, Egypt) at 697 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 3,879 km (about 5 h 10 min); Mumbai 3,519 km (about 4 h 45 min); Dubai 1,699 km (about 2 h 35 min); London Heathrow 4,748 km (about 6 h 15 min); New York JFK 10,225 km (about 12 h 50 min); Los Angeles 13,390 km (about 16 h 40 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
