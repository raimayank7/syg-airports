Airports in Kazakhstan

Airport codes of Kazakhstan: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Kazakhstan has 29 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 24 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Aktobe (AKX), Almaty (ALA), Baikonur (BXY), Shymkent (CIT), Taraz (DMB), Zhezkazgan (DZN), Atyrau (GUW), Turkıstan (HSA), Karaganda (KGF), Kokshetau (KOV), Kostanay (KSN), Kyzylorda (KZO) and 7 more. The longest runway is at Almaty International Airport: 4,500 metres. The highest is Almaty International Airport, at 681 metres above sea level.

The airports of Kazakhstan in numbers

Of the 29 airports in Kazakhstan that carry an IATA code, 24 have scheduled airline flights and 5 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 19 large airports, 2 medium airports, 3 small airports. That places Kazakhstan at number 35 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Petropavl International Airport (PPK) and the most southerly is Shymkent International Airport (CIT). The two that are farthest apart are Aktau International Airport and Zaysan Airport: about 2,635 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 3 h 40 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Karaganda Region (2), Turkistan Region (2), Abai Region (2).

Heights and runways

Heights run from -22 m at Atyrau International Airport to 681 m at Almaty International Airport, and the average is about 307 metres above sea level. Of the 23 airports with runway figures, 6 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 16 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Kazakhstan

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with UA: the first letter stands for Russia and neighbouring states. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is A, which opens 4 of the 29 codes. In 66% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Kazakhstan do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Almaty, Qostanay). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.

From Kazakhstan to the rest of the world

Measured from Almaty International Airport (ALA), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Issyk-Kul International Airport (IKU, Kyrgyzstan) at 90 km, Karakol International Airport (IKG, Kyrgyzstan) at 146 km, Manas International Airport (BSZ, Kyrgyzstan) at 210 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 1,646 km (about 2 h 30 min); Mumbai 2,726 km (about 3 h 45 min); Dubai 2,814 km (about 3 h 55 min); London Heathrow 5,624 km (about 7 h 15 min); New York JFK 10,228 km (about 12 h 50 min); Los Angeles 11,278 km (about 14 h 05 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
