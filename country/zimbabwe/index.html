Airports in Zimbabwe

Airport codes of Zimbabwe: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Zimbabwe has 13 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 4 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Bulawayo (BUQ), Harare (HRE), Victoria Falls (VFA). The longest runway is at Robert Gabriel Mugabe International Airport: 4,725 metres. The highest is Robert Gabriel Mugabe International Airport, at 1,490 metres above sea level.

The airports of Zimbabwe in numbers

Of the 13 airports in Zimbabwe that carry an IATA code, 4 have scheduled airline flights and 9 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports, 1 medium airport. That places Zimbabwe at number 128 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Kariba Airport (KAB) and the most southerly is Joshua Mqabuko Nkomo International Airport (BUQ). The two that are farthest apart are Robert Gabriel Mugabe International Airport and Victoria Falls International Airport: about 556 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 10 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Bulawayo Metropolitan Province (1), Harare Metropolitan Province (1), Mashonaland West Province (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 520 m at Kariba Airport to 1,490 m at Robert Gabriel Mugabe International Airport, and the average is about 1,100 metres above sea level. Of the 4 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 1 more reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Zimbabwe

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with FV: the first letter stands for central and southern Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is B, which opens 3 of the 13 codes. In 62% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Zimbabwe with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Harare), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
