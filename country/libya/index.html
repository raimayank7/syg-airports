Airports in Libya

Airport codes of Libya: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Libya has 18 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 7 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Benina (BEN), Al Albraq (LAQ), Tripoli (MJI). The longest runway is at Kufra Airport: 3,660 metres. The highest is Al Abraq International Airport, at 657 metres above sea level.

The airports of Libya in numbers

Of the 18 airports in Libya that carry an IATA code, 7 have scheduled airline flights and 11 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports, 3 medium airports, 1 small airport. That places Libya at number 84 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Mitiga International Airport (MJI) and the most southerly is Kufra Airport (AKF). The two that are farthest apart are Kufra Airport and Ghadames Airport: about 1,500 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 20 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Al Kufrah District (1), Benghazi District (1), Al Jabal al Akhdar District (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 11 m at Mitiga International Airport to 657 m at Al Abraq International Airport, and the average is about 287 metres above sea level. Of the 7 airports with runway figures, 7 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft.

The codes used in Libya

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with HL: the first letter stands for east and north-east Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is L, which opens 3 of the 18 codes. In 50% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Libya with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Tripoli), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Libya to the rest of the world

Measured from Al Abraq International Airport (LAQ), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Chania International Airport (CHQ, Greece) at 366 km, Kithira Airport (KIT, Greece) at 400 km, Heraklion International Nikos Kazantzakis Airport (HER, Greece) at 411 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 5,236 km (about 6 h 50 min); Mumbai 5,261 km (about 6 h 50 min); Dubai 3,340 km (about 4 h 30 min); London Heathrow 2,758 km (about 3 h 50 min); New York JFK 8,140 km (about 10 h 20 min); Los Angeles 11,517 km (about 14 h 25 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
