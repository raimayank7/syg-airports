Airports in Cambodia

Airport codes of Cambodia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Cambodia has 11 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 5 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Ta Noun (DSY), Preah Sihanouk (KOS), Phnom Penh (Boeng Khyang) (KTI), Siem Reap (SAI). The longest runway is at Techo International Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport, at 58 metres above sea level.

The airports of Cambodia in numbers

Of the 11 airports in Cambodia that carry an IATA code, 5 have scheduled airline flights and 6 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports, 1 medium airport. That places Cambodia at number 108 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport (SAI) and the most southerly is Sihanouk International Airport (KOS). The two that are farthest apart are Sihanouk International Airport and Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport: about 318 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Battambang province (1), Koh Kong province (1), Preah Sihanouk province (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 6 m at Techo International Airport to 58 m at Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport, and the average is about 22 metres above sea level. Of the 5 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 2 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Cambodia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with VD: the first letter stands for South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is K, which opens 4 of the 11 codes. In 55% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Cambodia with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Asia/Phnom Penh), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Cambodia to the rest of the world

Measured from Techo International Airport (KTI), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Rach Gia Airport (VKG, Vietnam) at 158 km, Can Tho International Airport (VCA, Vietnam) at 166 km, Phú Quốc International Airport (PQC, Vietnam) at 167 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 3,465 km (about 4 h 40 min); Mumbai 3,539 km (about 4 h 45 min); Dubai 5,421 km (about 7 h 00 min); London Heathrow 10,058 km (about 12 h 35 min); New York JFK 14,231 km (about 17 h 40 min); Los Angeles 13,209 km (about 16 h 25 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
