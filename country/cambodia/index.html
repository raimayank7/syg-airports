Airports in Cambodia

Airport codes of Cambodia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Cambodia has 11 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 5 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Ta Noun (DSY), Preah Sihanouk (KOS), Phnom Penh (Boeng Khyang) (KTI), Siem Reap (SAI). The longest runway is at Techo International Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport, at 58 metres above sea level.
