Airports in Turkey

Airport codes of Turkey: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Turkey has 66 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 51 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Gaziemir (ADB), Eskişehir (AOE), Kayseri (ASR), Antalya (AYT), Bodrum (BJV), Tarsus (COV), Dalaman (DLM), Edremit (EDO), Ankara (ESB), Şanlıurfa (GNY), Gaziantep (GZT), Istanbul (IST) and 3 more. The longest runway is at İstanbul Airport: 4,100 metres. The highest is Hakkari Yüksekova Airport, at 1,951 metres above sea level.

The airports of Turkey in numbers

Of the 66 airports in Turkey that carry an IATA code, 51 have scheduled airline flights and 15 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 15 large airports, 36 medium airports. That places Turkey at number 13 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Sinop Airport (NOP) and the most southerly is Gazipaşa-Alanya Airport (GZP). The two that are farthest apart are Çanakkale Airport and Hakkari Yüksekova Airport: about 1,566 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 25 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Antalya Province (2), Muğla Province (2), Balıkesir Province (2).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Ordu–Giresun Airport to 1,951 m at Hakkari Yüksekova Airport, and the average is about 638 metres above sea level. 6 of them stand above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 51 airports with runway figures, 15 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 32 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Turkey

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LT: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is A, which opens 9 of the 66 codes. In 67% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Turkey with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Europe/Istanbul), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Turkey to the rest of the world

Measured from İstanbul Airport (IST), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Burgas Airport (BOJ, Bulgaria) at 176 km, Varna Airport (VAR, Bulgaria) at 230 km, Alexandroupoli Democritus Airport (AXD, Greece) at 237 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 4,567 km (about 6 h 00 min); Mumbai 4,836 km (about 6 h 20 min); Dubai 3,030 km (about 4 h 10 min); London Heathrow 2,487 km (about 3 h 30 min); New York JFK 8,026 km (about 10 h 10 min); Los Angeles 11,002 km (about 13 h 45 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
