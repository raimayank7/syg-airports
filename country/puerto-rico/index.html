Airports in Puerto Rico

Airport codes of Puerto Rico: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Puerto Rico has 10 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 8 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are San Juan (SJU). The longest runway is at Rafael Hernández International Airport: 3,567 metres. The highest is Rafael Hernández International Airport, at 72 metres above sea level.

The airports of Puerto Rico in numbers

Of the 10 airports in Puerto Rico that carry an IATA code, 8 have scheduled airline flights and 2 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 7 medium airports. That places Puerto Rico at number 79 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Rafael Hernández International Airport (BQN) and the most southerly is Mercedita International Airport (PSE). The two that are farthest apart are Benjamin Rivera Noriega Airport and Eugenio Maria De Hostos Airport: about 195 km in a straight line.

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Luis Munoz Marin International Airport to 72 m at Rafael Hernández International Airport, and the average is about 17 metres above sea level. Of the 8 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 1 more reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 2 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Puerto Rico

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with TJ: the first letter stands for the Caribbean. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is S, which opens 2 of the 10 codes. In 80% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Puerto Rico with flights keeps the same clock time (zone America/Puerto Rico), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
