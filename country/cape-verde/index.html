Airports in Cape Verde

Airport codes of Cape Verde: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Cape Verde has 8 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 7 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Rabil (BVC), Praia (RAI), Espargos (SID), São Pedro (VXE). The longest runway is at Amílcar Cabral International Airport: 3,280 metres. The highest is Preguiça Airport, at 204 metres above sea level.
