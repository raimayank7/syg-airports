Airports in Cape Verde

Airport codes of Cape Verde: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Cape Verde has 8 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 7 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Rabil (BVC), Praia (RAI), Espargos (SID), São Pedro (VXE). The longest runway is at Amílcar Cabral International Airport: 3,280 metres. The highest is Preguiça Airport, at 204 metres above sea level.

The airports of Cape Verde in numbers

Of the 8 airports in Cape Verde that carry an IATA code, 7 have scheduled airline flights and 1 does not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports, 2 medium airports, 1 small airport. That places Cape Verde at number 87 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Cesaria Evora International Airport (VXE) and the most southerly is São Filipe Airport (SFL). The two that are farthest apart are Maio Airport and Cesaria Evora International Airport: about 271 km in a straight line.

Heights and runways

Heights run from 11 m at Maio Airport to 204 m at Preguiça Airport, and the average is about 81 metres above sea level. Of the 7 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 3 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Cape Verde

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with GV: the first letter stands for West Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is S, which opens 3 of the 8 codes. In 12% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Cape Verde with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Atlantic/Cape Verde), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
