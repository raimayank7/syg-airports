Airport codes of Angola: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Angola has 42 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 13 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Luanda (Ícolo e Bengo) (NBJ). The longest runway is at Dr. Antonio Agostinho Neto International Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is Lubango Mukanka International Airport, at 1,761 metres above sea level.
