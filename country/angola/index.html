The airports of Angola in numbers

Of the 42 airports in Angola that carry an IATA code, 13 have scheduled airline flights and 29 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 12 medium airports. That places Angola at number 64 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Cabinda Airport (CAB) and the most southerly is Ngjiva Pereira Airport (VPE). The two that are farthest apart are Cabinda Airport and Ngjiva Pereira Airport: about 1,328 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 05 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Zaire Province (2), Cabinda Province (1), Benguela Province (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 5 m at Soyo Airport to 1,761 m at Lubango Mukanka International Airport, and the average is about 763 metres above sea level. 2 of them stand above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 13 airports with runway figures, 4 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 5 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Angola

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with FN: the first letter stands for central and southern Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is C, which opens 8 of the 42 codes. In 57% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Angola with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Luanda), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
