Airports in Vietnam

Airport codes of Vietnam: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Vietnam has 28 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 22 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Nha Trang/nha Trang aiurportCam Ranh (CXR), Da Nang (DAD), Hanoi (Soc Son) (HAN), Haiphong (Hai An) (HPH), Huế (HUI), Phu Quoc Island (PQC), Ho Chi Minh City (SGN), Can Tho (VCA). The longest runway is at Tan Son Nhat International Airport: 3,800 metres. The highest is Lien Khuong Airport, at 962 metres above sea level.

The airports of Vietnam in numbers

Of the 28 airports in Vietnam that carry an IATA code, 22 have scheduled airline flights and 6 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 8 large airports, 12 medium airports, 2 small airports. That places Vietnam at number 41 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Dien Bien Phu Airport (DIN) and the most southerly is Con Dao Airport (VCS). The two that are farthest apart are Dien Bien Phu Airport and Con Dao Airport: about 1,461 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 15 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in South Central Coast Region (5), Southwest Region (4), North Central Region (4).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Cà Mau Airport to 962 m at Lien Khuong Airport, and the average is about 131 metres above sea level. Of the 22 airports with runway figures, 7 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 12 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 2 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Vietnam

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with VV: the first letter stands for South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is V, which opens 8 of the 28 codes. In 61% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Vietnam do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Ho Chi Minh, Bangkok). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.

From Vietnam to the rest of the world

Measured from Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Techo International Airport (KTI, Cambodia) at 198 km, Sihanouk International Airport (KOS, Cambodia) at 331 km, Dara Sakor International Airport (DSY, Cambodia) at 374 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 3,652 km (about 4 h 55 min); Mumbai 3,737 km (about 5 h 00 min); Dubai 5,618 km (about 7 h 15 min); London Heathrow 10,219 km (about 12 h 50 min); New York JFK 14,293 km (about 17 h 45 min); Los Angeles 13,130 km (about 16 h 20 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
