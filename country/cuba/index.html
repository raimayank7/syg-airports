Airports in Cuba

Airport codes of Cuba: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Cuba has 27 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 15 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Camaguey (CMW), Havana (HAV), Holguin (HOG), Santiago (SCU), Santa Clara (SNU), Matanzas (VRA). The longest runway is at Antonio Maceo International Airport: 4,002 metres. The highest is Ignacio Agramonte International Airport, at 126 metres above sea level.
