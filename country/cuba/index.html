Airports in Cuba

Airport codes of Cuba: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Cuba has 27 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 15 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Camaguey (CMW), Havana (HAV), Holguin (HOG), Santiago (SCU), Santa Clara (SNU), Matanzas (VRA). The longest runway is at Antonio Maceo International Airport: 4,002 metres. The highest is Ignacio Agramonte International Airport, at 126 metres above sea level.

The airports of Cuba in numbers

Of the 27 airports in Cuba that carry an IATA code, 15 have scheduled airline flights and 12 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 6 large airports, 9 medium airports. That places Cuba at number 57 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Juan Gualberto Gomez International Airport (VRA) and the most southerly is Antonio Maceo International Airport (SCU). The two that are farthest apart are Gustavo Rizo Airport and Rafael Cabrera Airport: about 874 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 35 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Granma Province (2), Isla de la Juventud (special municipality) (2), Guantánamo Province (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Vilo Acuña International Airport to 126 m at Ignacio Agramonte International Airport, and the average is about 56 metres above sea level. Of the 15 airports with runway figures, 4 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 7 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Cuba

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with MU: the first letter stands for Mexico and Central America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is C, which opens 4 of the 27 codes. In 59% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Cuba with flights keeps the same clock time (zone America/Havana), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Cuba to the rest of the world

Measured from Antonio Maceo International Airport (SCU), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Ian Fleming International Airport (OCJ, Jamaica) at 211 km, Jérémie Airport (JEE, Haiti) at 228 km, Tinson Pen Airport (KTP, Jamaica) at 244 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 13,886 km (about 17 h 15 min); Mumbai 14,492 km (about 18 h 00 min); Dubai 12,729 km (about 15 h 50 min); London Heathrow 7,281 km (about 9 h 15 min); New York JFK 2,306 km (about 3 h 15 min); Los Angeles 4,459 km (about 5 h 50 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
