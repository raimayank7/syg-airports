The airports of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines in numbers

Of the 5 airports in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines that carry an IATA code, 5 have scheduled airline flights. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 4 medium airports. That places Saint Vincent and the Grenadines at number 111 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Argyle International Airport (SVD) and the most southerly is Union Island International Airport (UNI). The two that are farthest apart are Argyle International Airport and Union Island International Airport: about 68 km in a straight line.

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Mustique Airport to 41 m at Argyle International Airport, and the average is about 11 metres above sea level. Of the 5 airports with runway figures, 1 reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 3 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

Clock times

Every airport of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines with flights keeps the same clock time (zone America/St Vincent), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
