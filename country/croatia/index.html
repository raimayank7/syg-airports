Airports in Croatia

Airport codes of Croatia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Croatia has 9 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 8 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Dubrovnik (DBV), Pula (PUY), Rijeka(Omišalj) (RJK), Split (SPU), Zadar (ZAD), Velika Gorica (ZAG). The longest runway is at Zagreb Franjo Tuđman International Airport: 3,252 metres. The highest is Brač Airport, at 541 metres above sea level.

The airports of Croatia in numbers

Of the 9 airports in Croatia that carry an IATA code, 8 have scheduled airline flights and 1 does not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 6 large airports, 2 medium airports. That places Croatia at number 80 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Zagreb Franjo Tuđman International Airport (ZAG) and the most southerly is Dubrovnik Ruđer Bošković Airport (DBV). The two that are farthest apart are Dubrovnik Ruđer Bošković Airport and Pula Airport: about 435 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 00 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Split-Dalmatia County (2), Dubrovnik-Neretva County (1), Osijek-Baranja County (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 24 m at Split Saint Jerome Airport to 541 m at Brač Airport, and the average is about 147 metres above sea level. Of the 8 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 5 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Croatia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LD: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is Z, which opens 2 of the 9 codes. In 67% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Croatia with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Europe/Zagreb), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Croatia to the rest of the world

Measured from Zadar Airport (ZAD), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Marche Airport (AOI, Italy) at 168 km, Banja Luka International Airport (BNX, Bosnia and Herzegovina) at 180 km, Abruzzo Airport (PSR, Italy) at 208 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 5,655 km (about 7 h 20 min); Mumbai 5,971 km (about 7 h 40 min); Dubai 4,160 km (about 5 h 30 min); London Heathrow 1,433 km (about 2 h 15 min); New York JFK 6,952 km (about 8 h 55 min); Los Angeles 10,157 km (about 12 h 45 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
