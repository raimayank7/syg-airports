Airports in Finland

Airport codes of Finland: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Finland has 32 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 20 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Helsinki (Vantaa) (HEL), Ivalo (IVL), Kittilä (KTT), Kuopio / Siilinjärvi (KUO), Lappeenranta (LPP), Oulu / Oulunsalo (OUL), Rovaniemi (RVN), Turku (TKU), Tampere / Pirkkala (TMP), Vaasa (VAA). The longest runway is at Helsinki Vantaa Airport: 3,500 metres. The highest is Enontekio Airport, at 306 metres above sea level.

The airports of Finland in numbers

Of the 32 airports in Finland that carry an IATA code, 20 have scheduled airline flights and 12 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 10 large airports, 10 medium airports. That places Finland at number 44 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Ivalo Airport (IVL) and the most southerly is Mariehamn Airport (MHQ). The two that are farthest apart are Ivalo Airport and Mariehamn Airport: about 1,009 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 45 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Lapland Region (5), North Ostrobothnia Region (2), Uusimaa Region (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 5 m at Mariehamn Airport to 306 m at Enontekio Airport, and the average is about 106 metres above sea level. Of the 20 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 17 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Finland

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with EF: the first letter stands for northern Europe. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is K, which opens 10 of the 32 codes. In 97% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. From Finland to the rest of the world

Measured from Helsinki Vantaa Airport (HEL), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Tallinn Linnahall Heliport (CHE, Estonia) at 97 km, Lennart Meri Tallinn Airport (TLL, Estonia) at 101 km, Kärdla Airport (KDL, Estonia) at 190 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 5,224 km (about 6 h 50 min); Mumbai 5,925 km (about 7 h 40 min); Dubai 4,538 km (about 6 h 00 min); London Heathrow 1,848 km (about 2 h 45 min); New York JFK 6,607 km (about 8 h 30 min); Los Angeles 9,013 km (about 11 h 20 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
