Airports in Bolivia

Airport codes of Bolivia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Bolivia has 41 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 16 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Cochabamba (CBB), La Paz / El Alto (LPB), Oruro (ORU), Sucre (SRE), Quijarro (UYU), Santa Cruz (VVI). The longest runway is at El Alto International Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is El Alto International Airport, at 4,071 metres above sea level.

The airports of Bolivia in numbers

Of the 41 airports in Bolivia that carry an IATA code, 16 have scheduled airline flights and 25 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 6 large airports, 7 medium airports, 3 small airports. That places Bolivia at number 56 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Guayaramerín Airport (GYA) and the most southerly is Capitan Oriel Lea Plaza Airport (TJA). The two that are farthest apart are Capitán Aníbal Arab Airport and Capitán Av. Salvador Ogaya G. airport: about 1,470 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 15 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in El Beni Department (4), Santa Cruz Department (3), Cochabamba Department (2).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 134 m at Capitán Av. Salvador Ogaya G. airport to 4,071 m at El Alto International Airport, and the average is about 1,310 metres above sea level. 6 of them stand above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 15 airports with runway figures, 5 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 4 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 2 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Bolivia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with SL: the first letter stands for South America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is S, which opens 11 of the 41 codes. In 85% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name.
