Airports in Azerbaijan

Airport codes of Azerbaijan: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Azerbaijan has 11 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 6 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Ganja (GNJ), Baku (GYD), Nakhchivan (NAJ). The longest runway is at Heydar Aliyev International Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is Lachin International Airport, at 1,700 metres above sea level.

The airports of Azerbaijan in numbers

Of the 11 airports in Azerbaijan that carry an IATA code, 6 have scheduled airline flights and 5 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports, 2 medium airports, 1 small airport. That places Azerbaijan at number 100 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Gabala International Airport (GBB) and the most southerly is Lankaran International Airport (LLK). The two that are farthest apart are Heydar Aliyev International Airport and Nakhchivan International Airport: about 417 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 00 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Qabala District (1), Ganja (city) (1), Baku (city) (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Heydar Aliyev International Airport to 1,700 m at Lachin International Airport, and the average is about 533 metres above sea level. 1 of them stands above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 5 airports with runway figures, 5 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft.

The codes used in Azerbaijan

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with UB: the first letter stands for Russia and neighbouring states. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is G, which opens 3 of the 11 codes. In 91% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Azerbaijan with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Asia/Baku), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Azerbaijan to the rest of the world

Measured from Heydar Aliyev International Airport (GYD), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Parsabad-Moghan Airport (PFQ, Iran) at 208 km, Turkmenbaşy International Airport (KRW, Turkmenistan) at 255 km, Ardabil Airport (ADU, Iran) at 277 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 2,794 km (about 3 h 50 min); Mumbai 3,221 km (about 4 h 25 min); Dubai 1,763 km (about 2 h 35 min); London Heathrow 4,002 km (about 5 h 20 min); New York JFK 9,361 km (about 11 h 45 min); Los Angeles 11,656 km (about 14 h 35 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
