Airports in Norway

Airport codes of Norway: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Norway has 50 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 47 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Ålesund (AES), Bergen (BGO), Bodø (BOO), Evenes (EVE), Kristiansand(Kjevik) (KRS), Oslo (Gardermoen) (OSL), Stavanger (SVG), Tromsø (TOS), Trondheim (TRD), Sandefjord(Torp) (TRF). The longest runway is at Oslo-Gardermoen International Airport: 3,600 metres. The highest is Røros Airport, at 626 metres above sea level.

The airports of Norway in numbers

Of the 50 airports in Norway that carry an IATA code, 47 have scheduled airline flights and 3 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 10 large airports, 30 medium airports, 6 small airports, 1 heliport. That places Norway at number 16 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Svalbard Airport, Longyear (LYR) and the most southerly is Kristiansand Airport (KRS). The two that are farthest apart are Kristiansand Airport and Svalbard Airport, Longyear: about 2,245 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 3 h 10 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Nordland (12), Finnmark (11), Vestland (6).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Namsos Airport to 626 m at Røros Airport, and the average is about 63 metres above sea level. Of the 47 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 13 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 26 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Norway

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with EN: the first letter stands for northern Europe. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is S, which opens 8 of the 50 codes. In 76% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Norway do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Oslo, Moscow). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.

From Norway to the rest of the world

Measured from Oslo-Gardermoen International Airport (OSL), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Torsby Airport (TYF, Sweden) at 105 km, Hagfors Airport (HFS, Sweden) at 139 km, Scandinavian Mountains Airport (SCR, Sweden) at 143 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 5,968 km (about 7 h 40 min); Mumbai 6,628 km (about 8 h 30 min); Dubai 5,138 km (about 6 h 40 min); London Heathrow 1,206 km (about 1 h 55 min); New York JFK 5,917 km (about 7 h 40 min); Los Angeles 8,580 km (about 10 h 50 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
