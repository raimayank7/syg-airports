Airports in Micronesia

Airport codes of Micronesia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Micronesia has 5 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 4 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Okat (KSA), Weno Island (TKK), Yap Island (YAP). The longest runway is at Pohnpei International Airport: 2,012 metres. The highest is Yap International Airport, at 28 metres above sea level.

The airports of Micronesia in numbers

Of the 5 airports in Micronesia that carry an IATA code, 4 have scheduled airline flights and 1 does not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports, 1 medium airport. That places Micronesia at number 135 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Yap International Airport (YAP) and the most southerly is Kosrae International Airport (KSA). The two that are farthest apart are Kosrae International Airport and Yap International Airport: about 2,780 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 3 h 50 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Kosrae State (1), Pohnpei State (1), Chuuk State (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Pohnpei International Airport to 28 m at Yap International Airport, and the average is about 9 metres above sea level. Of the 4 airports with runway figures, none has a runway of 2,300 metres, the length most passenger jets need.

The codes used in Micronesia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with PT: the first letter stands for the North Pacific, including Alaska and Hawaii. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of Micronesia with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Pacific/Pohnpei), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
