Airports in Czech Republic

Airport codes of Czech Republic: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Czech Republic has 12 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 6 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are České Budějovice (JCL), Karlovy Vary (KLV), Mošnov (OSR), Pardubice (PED), Prague (PRG). The longest runway is at Václav Havel Airport Prague: 3,715 metres. The highest is Karlovy Vary Airport, at 606 metres above sea level.

The airports of Czech Republic in numbers

Of the 12 airports in Czech Republic that carry an IATA code, 6 have scheduled airline flights and 6 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 5 large airports, 1 medium airport. That places Czech Republic at number 95 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Karlovy Vary Airport (KLV) and the most southerly is České Budějovice South Bohemian Airport (JCL). The two that are farthest apart are Karlovy Vary Airport and Leoš Janáček Airport Ostrava: about 376 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in South Moravian Region (1), South Bohemian Region (1), Karlovy Vary Region (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 226 m at Pardubice Airport to 606 m at Karlovy Vary Airport, and the average is about 356 metres above sea level. Of the 6 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 3 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Czech Republic

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LK: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is P, which opens 3 of the 12 codes. In 67% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Czech Republic with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Europe/Prague), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
