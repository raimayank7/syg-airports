Airports in Haiti

Airport codes of Haiti: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Haiti has 6 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 4 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Cap Haitien (CAP), Port-au-Prince (PAP). The longest runway is at Toussaint Louverture International Airport: 3,040 metres. The highest is Antoine-Simon International Airport, at 62 metres above sea level.

The airports of Haiti in numbers

Of the 6 airports in Haiti that carry an IATA code, 4 have scheduled airline flights and 2 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 2 large airports, 2 medium airports. That places Haiti at number 130 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Cap Haitien International Airport (CAP) and the most southerly is Antoine-Simon International Airport (CYA). The two that are farthest apart are Cap Haitien International Airport and Jérémie Airport: about 238 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Nord Department (1), Sud Department (1), Grande'Anse Department (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Cap Haitien International Airport to 62 m at Antoine-Simon International Airport, and the average is about 37 metres above sea level. Of the 3 airports with runway figures, 2 reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 1 has nothing longer than 1,500 metres and takes mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Haiti

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with MT: the first letter stands for Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of Haiti with flights keeps the same clock time (zone America/Port-au-Prince), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
