Airports in Namibia

Airport codes of Namibia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Namibia has 31 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 9 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Windhoek (WDH), Walvis Bay(Rooikop) (WVB). The longest runway is at Hosea Kutako International Airport: 4,575 metres. The highest is Hosea Kutako International Airport, at 1,719 metres above sea level.
