The airports of Namibia in numbers

Of the 31 airports in Namibia that carry an IATA code, 9 have scheduled airline flights and 22 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 2 large airports, 6 medium airports, 1 small airport. That places Namibia at number 75 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Katima Mulilo Airport (MPA) and the most southerly is Oranjemund Airport (OMD). The two that are farthest apart are Katima Mulilo Airport and Oranjemund Airport: about 1,451 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 15 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Khomas Region (2), ǁKaras Region (2), Oshikoto Region (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 4 m at Oranjemund Airport to 1,719 m at Hosea Kutako International Airport, and the average is about 880 metres above sea level. 2 of them stand above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 8 airports with runway figures, 3 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 1 more reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Namibia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with FY: the first letter stands for central and southern Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is O, which opens 8 of the 31 codes. In 87% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Namibia with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Windhoek), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
