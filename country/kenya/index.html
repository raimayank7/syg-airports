Airports in Kenya

Airport codes of Kenya: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Kenya has 45 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 12 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Eldoret (EDL), Kisumu (KIS), Mombasa (MBA), Nairobi (NBO). The longest runway is at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport: 4,117 metres. The highest is Eldoret International Airport, at 2,116 metres above sea level.
