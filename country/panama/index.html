The airports of Panama in numbers

Of the 35 airports in Panama that carry an IATA code, 12 have scheduled airline flights and 23 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 7 medium airports, 4 small airports. That places Panama at number 68 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Changuinola Captain Manuel Niño International Airport (CHX) and the most southerly is Capt. J. Montenegro Airport (PDM). The two that are farthest apart are Bahia Piña Airport and Changuinola Captain Manuel Niño International Airport: about 520 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 10 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Panamá Province (3), Bocas del Toro Province (2), Darién Province (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Bocas del Toro "Isla Colón" International Airport to 45 m at Capt. J. Montenegro Airport, and the average is about 18 metres above sea level. Of the 11 airports with runway figures, 5 reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 5 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft. The main runway is hard (asphalt or concrete) at 10, unpaved (gravel, grass, earth, coral or similar) at 1.

The codes used in Panama

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with MP: the first letter stands for Mexico and Central America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is P, which opens 6 of the 35 codes. In 46% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name.
