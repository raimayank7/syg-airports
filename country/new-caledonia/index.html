Airports in New Caledonia

Airport codes of New Caledonia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

The longest runway is at La Tontouta International Airport: 3,250 metres. The highest is Île des Pins Airport, at 96 metres above sea level.

The airports of New Caledonia in numbers

Of the 13 airports in New Caledonia that carry an IATA code, 11 have scheduled airline flights and 2 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 8 medium airports, 2 small airports. That places New Caledonia at number 72 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Île Art - Waala Airport (BMY) and the most southerly is Île des Pins Airport (ILP). The two that are farthest apart are Île Art - Waala Airport and Île des Pins Airport: about 507 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 05 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Province Nord (4), Province des îles Loyauté (4), Province Sud (3).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Nouméa Magenta Airport to 96 m at Île des Pins Airport, and the average is about 32 metres above sea level. Of the 10 airports with runway figures, 1 has a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 9 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft. The main runway is hard (asphalt or concrete) at 8, unpaved (gravel, grass, earth, coral or similar) at 2.

The codes used in New Caledonia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with NW: the first letter stands for the South Pacific. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is K, which opens 2 of the 13 codes. In 54% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of New Caledonia with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Pacific/Noumea), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
