Airports in Sri Lanka

Airport codes of Sri Lanka: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Sri Lanka has 32 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 12 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Colombo (CMB), Jaffna (JAF), Colombo (RML). The longest runway is at Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport: 3,500 metres. The highest is Polgolla Reservoir Seaplane Base, at 449 metres above sea level.
