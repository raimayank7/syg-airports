Airports in Sri Lanka

Airport codes of Sri Lanka: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Sri Lanka has 32 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 12 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Colombo (CMB), Jaffna (JAF), Colombo (RML). The longest runway is at Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport: 3,500 metres. The highest is Polgolla Reservoir Seaplane Base, at 449 metres above sea level.

The airports of Sri Lanka in numbers

Of the 32 airports in Sri Lanka that carry an IATA code, 12 have scheduled airline flights and 20 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports, 4 medium airports, 5 seaplane bases. That places Sri Lanka at number 70 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Jaffna International Airport (JAF) and the most southerly is Koggala Airport (KCT). The two that are farthest apart are Hambantota Seaplane Base and Jaffna International Airport: about 423 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 00 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Western Province (4), Southern Province (4), Eastern Province (2).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 0 m at Hambantota Seaplane Base to 449 m at Polgolla Reservoir Seaplane Base, and the average is about 51 metres above sea level. Of the 7 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 1 more reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 2 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Sri Lanka

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with VC: the first letter stands for South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is K, which opens 5 of the 32 codes. In 59% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Sri Lanka with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Asia/Colombo), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Sri Lanka to the rest of the world

Measured from Bandaranaike International Colombo Airport (CMB), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Tuticorin Airport (TCR, India) at 267 km, Madurai Airport (IXM, India) at 355 km, Thiruvananthapuram International Airport (TRV, India) at 357 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 2,395 km (about 3 h 25 min); Mumbai 1,526 km (about 2 h 20 min); Dubai 3,287 km (about 4 h 30 min); London Heathrow 8,715 km (about 11 h 00 min); New York JFK 14,054 km (about 17 h 25 min); Los Angeles 15,053 km (about 18 h 40 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
