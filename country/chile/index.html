Airports in Chile

Airport codes of Chile: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Chile has 51 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 19 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Antofagasta (ANF), Concepcion (CCP), Isla De Pascua (IPC), Iquique (IQQ), Puerto Montt (PMC), Punta Arenas (PUQ), Santiago (SCL), Temuco (ZCO). The longest runway is at Comodoro Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport: 3,750 metres. The highest is El Loa Airport, at 2,299 metres above sea level.
