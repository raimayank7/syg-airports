Airports in Chile

Airport codes of Chile: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Chile has 51 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 19 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Antofagasta (ANF), Concepcion (CCP), Isla De Pascua (IPC), Iquique (IQQ), Puerto Montt (PMC), Punta Arenas (PUQ), Santiago (SCL), Temuco (ZCO). The longest runway is at Comodoro Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport: 3,750 metres. The highest is El Loa Airport, at 2,299 metres above sea level.

The airports of Chile in numbers

Of the 51 airports in Chile that carry an IATA code, 19 have scheduled airline flights and 32 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 8 large airports, 9 medium airports, 2 small airports. That places Chile at number 45 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Chacalluta International Airport (ARI) and the most southerly is President Carlos Ibáñez International Airport (PUQ). The two that are farthest apart are Mataveri International Airport and President Carlos Ibáñez International Airport: about 4,274 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 5 h 40 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Los Lagos Region (3), Antofagasta Region (2), Atacama Region (2).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 8 m at Carriel Sur International Airport to 2,299 m at El Loa Airport, and the average is about 334 metres above sea level. 2 of them stand above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 19 airports with runway figures, 3 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 9 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Chile

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with SC: the first letter stands for South America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is C, which opens 7 of the 51 codes. In 57% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Chile do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Santiago, Punta Arenas). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.

From Chile to the rest of the world

Measured from President Carlos Ibáñez International Airport (PUQ), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are 28 de Noviembre Airport (RYO, Argentina) at 181 km, Piloto Civil Norberto Fernández International Airport (RGL, Argentina) at 187 km, Gobernador Ramón Trejo Noel International Airport (RGA, Argentina) at 223 km.
