Airports in Maldives

Airport codes of Maldives: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Maldives has 20 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 15 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Gan (GAN), Haa Dhaalu Atoll (HAQ), Malé (MLE). The longest runway is at Gan International Airport: 3,658 metres. The highest is Kooddoo Airport, at 9 metres above sea level.

The airports of Maldives in numbers

Of the 20 airports in Maldives that carry an IATA code, 15 have scheduled airline flights and 5 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports, 4 medium airports, 8 small airports. That places Maldives at number 61 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Hoarafushi Airport (HRF) and the most southerly is Gan International Airport (GAN). The two that are farthest apart are Gan International Airport and Hoarafushi Airport: about 853 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 30 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Gaafu Dhaalu Atoll (3), Haa Dhaalu Atoll (2), Baa Atoll (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 1 m at Kaadedhdhoo Airport to 9 m at Kooddoo Airport, and the average is about 3 metres above sea level. Of the 9 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 2 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 3 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in Maldives

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with VR: the first letter stands for South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is F, which opens 3 of the 20 codes. In 60% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Maldives with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Indian/Maldives), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Maldives to the rest of the world

Measured from Gan International Airport (GAN), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Koggala Airport (KCT, Sri Lanka) at 1,089 km, Bentota River Waterdrome (BJT, Sri Lanka) at 1,097 km, Thiruvananthapuram International Airport (TRV, India) at 1,102 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 3,279 km (about 4 h 25 min); Mumbai 2,200 km (about 3 h 10 min); Dubai 3,461 km (about 4 h 40 min); London Heathrow 8,943 km (about 11 h 15 min); New York JFK 14,463 km (about 17 h 55 min); Los Angeles 16,127 km (about 19 h 55 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
