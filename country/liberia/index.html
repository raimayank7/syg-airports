The airports of Liberia in numbers

Of the 14 airports in Liberia that carry an IATA code, 2 have scheduled airline flights and 12 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 1 medium airport. That places Liberia at number 173 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The two airports with flights, Spriggs Payne Airport (MLW) and Roberts International Airport (ROB), are about 44 km apart in a straight line.

Heights and runways

The longest runway, at Roberts International Airport, measures 3,353 metres: long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft.

The codes used in Liberia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with GL: the first letter stands for West Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is R, which opens 2 of the 14 codes. In 64% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Liberia with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Monrovia), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
