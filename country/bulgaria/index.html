Airports in Bulgaria

Airport codes of Bulgaria: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Bulgaria has 7 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 4 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Burgas (BOJ), Plovdiv (PDV), Sofia (SOF), Varna (VAR). The longest runway is at Sofia Airport: 3,600 metres. The highest is Sofia Airport, at 531 metres above sea level.

The airports of Bulgaria in numbers

Of the 7 airports in Bulgaria that carry an IATA code, 4 have scheduled airline flights and 3 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports. That places Bulgaria at number 123 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Varna Airport (VAR) and the most southerly is Plovdiv International Airport (PDV). The two that are farthest apart are Sofia Airport and Varna Airport: about 363 km in a straight line. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Burgas Province (1), Plovdiv Province (1), Sofia City Province (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 41 m at Burgas Airport to 531 m at Sofia Airport, and the average is about 206 metres above sea level. Of the 4 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 2 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Bulgaria

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LB: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of Bulgaria with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Europe/Sofia), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Bulgaria to the rest of the world

Measured from Sofia Airport (SOF), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Niš Constantine the Great Airport (INI, Serbia) at 146 km, Skopje International Airport (SKP, North Macedonia) at 169 km, Craiova International Airport (CRA, Romania) at 184 km.
