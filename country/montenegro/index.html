Airports in Montenegro

Airport codes of Montenegro: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Montenegro has 3 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 2 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Podgorica (TGD). The longest runway is at Tivat Airport: 2,502 metres. The highest is Podgorica Airport / Podgorica Golubovci Airbase, at 43 metres above sea level.

The airports of Montenegro in numbers

Of the 3 airports in Montenegro that carry an IATA code, 2 have scheduled airline flights and 1 does not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 1 medium airport. That places Montenegro at number 185 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The two airports with flights, Podgorica Airport / Podgorica Golubovci Airbase (TGD) and Tivat Airport (TIV), are about 44 km apart in a straight line.

Heights and runways

The longest runway, at Tivat Airport, measures 2,502 metres: enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Montenegro

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LY: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of Montenegro with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Europe/Podgorica), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
