The airports of Chad in numbers

Of the 19 airports in Chad that carry an IATA code, 1 has scheduled airline flights and 18 do not. That places Chad at number 222 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Heights and runways

N'Djamena International Airport stands 295 metres above sea level. The longest runway, at N'Djamena International Airport, measures 2,800 metres: enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Chad

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with FT: the first letter stands for central and southern Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is A, which opens 6 of the 19 codes. In 74% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name.
