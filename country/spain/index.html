Airports in Spain

Airport codes of Spain: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Spain has 52 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 44 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are San Bartolomé (ACE), Málaga (AGP), Alicante (ALC), Barcelona (BCN), Bilbao (BIO), El Matorral (FUE), Girona (GRO), Ibiza (Eivissa) (IBZ), Gran Canaria Island (LPA), Madrid (MAD), Mahón (Maó) (MAH), Ranón (OVD) and 9 more. The longest runway is at Adolfo Suárez Madrid–Barajas Airport: 4,350 metres. The highest is León Airport, at 916 metres above sea level.

The airports of Spain in numbers

Of the 52 airports in Spain that carry an IATA code, 44 have scheduled airline flights and 8 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 21 large airports, 21 medium airports, 1 small airport, 1 heliport. That places Spain at number 19 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Asturias Airport (OVD) and the most southerly is El Hierro Airport (VDE). The two that are farthest apart are Girona-Costa Brava Airport and El Hierro Airport: about 2,439 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 3 h 25 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Canary Islands (8), Andalucía (6), Catalonia (5).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Ceuta Heliport to 916 m at León Airport, and the average is about 224 metres above sea level. Of the 44 airports with runway figures, 11 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 21 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 5 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft. The main runway is hard (asphalt or concrete) at 43, unpaved (gravel, grass, earth, coral or similar) at 1.

The codes used in Spain

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here do not share one opening: they begin with L (34), G (10). Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is V, which opens 5 of the 52 codes. In 60% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

The airports of Spain do not all keep the same time: they fall in 2 time zone areas (Madrid, Canary). A ticket always shows the local time of each airport, so on a flight across the country the hours on the ticket do not simply subtract.

From Spain to the rest of the world

Measured from Adolfo Suárez Madrid–Barajas Airport (MAD), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Bragança Airport (BGC, Portugal) at 303 km, Vila Real Airport (VRL, Portugal) at 359 km, Aerodromo Goncalves Lobato (Viseu Airport) (VSE, Portugal) at 365 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 7,255 km (about 9 h 15 min); Mumbai 7,518 km (about 9 h 35 min); Dubai 5,650 km (about 7 h 20 min); London Heathrow 1,244 km (about 2 h 00 min); New York JFK 5,760 km (about 7 h 25 min); Los Angeles 9,385 km (about 11 h 50 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
