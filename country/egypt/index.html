Airports in Egypt

Airport codes of Egypt: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Egypt has 29 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 17 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are El Arish (AAC), Aswan (ASW), Asyut (ATZ), Cairo (CAI), El Alamein (DBB), Alexandria (HBE), Suhaj (HMB), Hurghada (HRG), Luxor (LXR), Marsa Matruh (MUH), Port Said (PSD), Marsa Alam (RMF) and 2 more. The longest runway is at Hurghada International Airport: 4,015 metres. The highest is Taba International Airport, at 739 metres above sea level.

The airports of Egypt in numbers

Of the 29 airports in Egypt that carry an IATA code, 17 have scheduled airline flights and 12 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 14 large airports, 3 medium airports. That places Egypt at number 48 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Mersa Matruh International Airport (MUH) and the most southerly is Abu Simbel Airport (ABS). The two that are farthest apart are Abu Simbel Airport and Mersa Matruh International Airport: about 1,086 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 50 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Aswan Governorate (2), Cairo Governorate (2), Matruh Governorate (2).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Port Said International Airport to 739 m at Taba International Airport, and the average is about 136 metres above sea level. Of the 17 airports with runway figures, 9 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 8 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Egypt

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with HE: the first letter stands for east and north-east Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is A, which opens 5 of the 29 codes. In 55% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. From Egypt to the rest of the world

Measured from Cairo International Airport (CAI), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Ramon International Airport (ETM, Israel) at 351 km, King Hussein International Airport (AQJ, Jordan) at 354 km, Ben Gurion International Airport (TLV, Israel) at 394 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 4,403 km (about 5 h 50 min); Mumbai 4,342 km (about 5 h 45 min); Dubai 2,417 km (about 3 h 25 min); London Heathrow 3,533 km (about 4 h 45 min); New York JFK 9,017 km (about 11 h 20 min); Los Angeles 12,219 km (about 15 h 15 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
