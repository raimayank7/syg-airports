Airports in Egypt

Airport codes of Egypt: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Egypt has 29 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 17 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are El Arish (AAC), Aswan (ASW), Asyut (ATZ), Cairo (CAI), El Alamein (DBB), Alexandria (HBE), Suhaj (HMB), Hurghada (HRG), Luxor (LXR), Marsa Matruh (MUH), Port Said (PSD), Marsa Alam (RMF) and 2 more. The longest runway is at Hurghada International Airport: 4,015 metres. The highest is Taba International Airport, at 739 metres above sea level.
