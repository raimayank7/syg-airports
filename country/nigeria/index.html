Airports in Nigeria

Airport codes of Nigeria: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Nigeria has 26 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 24 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Asaba (ABB), Abuja (ABV), Bauchi (BCU), Enegu (ENU), Ilorin/Ogbomosho (ILR), Kaduna (KAD), Kano (KAN), Lagos (LOS), Maiduguri (MIU), Port Harcourt (PHC), Sokoto (SKO). The longest runway is at Murtala Muhammed International Airport: 3,900 metres. The highest is Yakubu Gowon Airport, at 1,290 metres above sea level.

The airports of Nigeria in numbers

Of the 26 airports in Nigeria that carry an IATA code, 24 have scheduled airline flights and 2 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 11 large airports, 13 medium airports. That places Nigeria at number 33 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Umaru Musa Yar'adua Airport (DKA) and the most southerly is Port Harcourt City Airport / Port Harcourt Air Force Base (PHG). The two that are farthest apart are Murtala Muhammed International Airport and Maiduguri International Airport: about 1,221 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 00 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Delta State (2), Rivers State (2), Abuja Capital Territory (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 17 m at Port Harcourt City Airport / Port Harcourt Air Force Base to 1,290 m at Yakubu Gowon Airport, and the average is about 286 metres above sea level. Of the 24 airports with runway figures, 8 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 14 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Nigeria

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with DN: the first letter stands for West Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is A, which opens 3 of the 26 codes. In 85% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Nigeria with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Lagos), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Nigeria to the rest of the world

Measured from Murtala Muhammed International Airport (LOS), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Cotonou Cadjehoun International Airport (COO, Benin) at 106 km, Lomé–Tokoin International Airport (LFW, Togo) at 233 km, Kotoka International Airport (ACC, Ghana) at 401 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 8,076 km (about 10 h 15 min); Mumbai 7,624 km (about 9 h 40 min); Dubai 5,896 km (about 7 h 35 min); London Heathrow 5,004 km (about 6 h 30 min); New York JFK 8,444 km (about 10 h 40 min); Los Angeles 12,419 km (about 15 h 30 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
