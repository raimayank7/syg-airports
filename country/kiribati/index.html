Airports in Kiribati

Airport codes of Kiribati: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Kiribati has 21 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 4 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Kiritimati (CXI), South Tarawa (TRW). The longest runway is at Cassidy International Airport: 2,103 metres. The highest is Bonriki International Airport, at 3 metres above sea level.

The airports of Kiribati in numbers

Of the 21 airports in Kiribati that carry an IATA code, 4 have scheduled airline flights and 17 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 2 large airports, 2 small airports. That places Kiribati at number 117 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Butaritari Airport (BBG) and the most southerly is Aranuka Airport (AAK). The two that are farthest apart are Butaritari Airport and Cassidy International Airport: about 3,317 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 4 h 30 min in a jet.

Heights and runways

Heights run from 2 m at Butaritari Airport to 3 m at Bonriki International Airport, and the average is about 2 metres above sea level. The longest runway, at Cassidy International Airport, measures 2,103 metres: right for narrow-body jets and regional aircraft.

The codes used in Kiribati

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here do not share one opening: they begin with N (3), P (1). Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is T, which opens 6 of the 21 codes. In 81% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Kiribati with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Pacific/Tarawa), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Kiribati to the rest of the world
