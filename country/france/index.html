Airports in France

Airport codes of France: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

France has 121 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 49 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Brest (BES), Bastia (BIA), Bordeaux (BOD), Bâle / Mulhouse (BSL), Beauvais (BVA), Paris (Roissy-en-France, Val-d'Oise) (CDG), Clermont-Ferrand (CFE), Figari (FSC), Lesquin (LIL), Colombier-Saugnieu, Rhône (LYS), Montpellier/Méditerranée (MPL), Marignane, Bouches-du-Rhône (MRS) and 5 more. The longest runway is at Charles de Gaulle International Airport: 4,215 metres. The highest is Le Puy-Loudes Airfield, at 832 metres above sea level.
