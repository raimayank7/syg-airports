Airports in Taiwan

Airport codes of Taiwan: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Taiwan has 19 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 13 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Hualien City (HUN), Kaohsiung (Xiaogang) (KHH), Huxi (MZG), Taichung (Qingshui) (RMQ), Tainan (Rende) (TNN), Taoyuan (TPE), Taipei (Songshan) (TSA). The longest runway is at Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport: 3,660 metres. The highest is Taichung International Airport / Ching Chuang Kang Air Base, at 202 metres above sea level.
