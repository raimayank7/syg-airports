Airports in Paraguay

Airport codes of Paraguay: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Paraguay has 14 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 3 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Ciudad del Este (AGT), Asunción (ASU), Encarnación (ENO). The longest runway is at Guaraní International Airport: 3,400 metres. The highest is Guaraní International Airport, at 258 metres above sea level.

The airports of Paraguay in numbers

Of the 14 airports in Paraguay that carry an IATA code, 3 have scheduled airline flights and 11 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 3 large airports. That places Paraguay at number 138 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The airports with flights are Ciudad del Este (AGT), Asunción (ASU), Encarnación (ENO).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 89 m at Silvio Pettirossi International Airport to 258 m at Guaraní International Airport, and the average is about 183 metres above sea level. Of the 3 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft.

The codes used in Paraguay

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with SG: the first letter stands for South America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is P, which opens 4 of the 14 codes. In 71% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Paraguay with flights keeps the same clock time (zone America/Asuncion), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
