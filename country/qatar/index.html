Airports in Qatar

Airport codes of Qatar: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Qatar has 3 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 1 of them has scheduled airline flights and has its own page here. The large airports are Doha (DOH). The longest runway is at Hamad International Airport: 4,850 metres.

The airports of Qatar in numbers

Of the 3 airports in Qatar that carry an IATA code, 1 has scheduled airline flights and 2 do not. That places Qatar at number 202 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Heights and runways

Hamad International Airport stands 4 metres above sea level. The longest runway, at Hamad International Airport, measures 4,850 metres: long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft.

The codes used in Qatar

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with OT: the first letter stands for the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. How the two kinds of code work.

From Qatar to the rest of the world

Measured from Hamad International Airport (DOH), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Delma Airport (ZDY, United Arab Emirates) at 112 km, Bahrain International Airport (BAH, Bahrain) at 147 km, Al-Ahsa International Airport (HOF, Saudi Arabia) at 213 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 2,548 km (about 3 h 35 min); Mumbai 2,291 km (about 3 h 15 min); Dubai 378 km (about 55 min); London Heathrow 5,241 km (about 6 h 50 min); New York JFK 10,770 km (about 13 h 30 min); Los Angeles 13,347 km (about 16 h 35 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
