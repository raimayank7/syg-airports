Airports in Afghanistan

Airport codes of Afghanistan: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Afghanistan has 32 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 6 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Guzara (HEA), Kabul (KBL), Kandahar (KDH), Mazar-i-Sharif (MZR). The longest runway is at Kabul International Airport: 3,500 metres. The highest is Kabul International Airport, at 1,791 metres above sea level.

The airports of Afghanistan in numbers

Of the 32 airports in Afghanistan that carry an IATA code, 6 have scheduled airline flights and 26 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports, 1 medium airport, 1 small airport. That places Afghanistan at number 98 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Fayzabad Airport (FBD) and the most southerly is Ahmad Shah Baba International Airport (KDH). The two that are farthest apart are Fayzabad Airport and Herat - Khwaja Abdullah Ansari International Airport: about 816 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 30 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Badakhshan Province (1), Herat Province (1), Kabul Province (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 391 m at Mazar-i-Sharif International Airport to 1,791 m at Kabul International Airport, and the average is about 1,106 metres above sea level. 1 of them stands above 1,500 metres, where thin air makes take-off runs longer. Of the 6 airports with runway figures, 2 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 3 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets. The main runway is hard (asphalt or concrete) at 5, unpaved (gravel, grass, earth, coral or similar) at 1.

The codes used in Afghanistan

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with OA: the first letter stands for the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is O, which opens 6 of the 32 codes. In 59% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Afghanistan with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Asia/Kabul), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Afghanistan to the rest of the world

Measured from Kabul International Airport (KBL), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Bacha Khan International Airport (PEW, Pakistan) at 221 km, Chitral Airport (CJL, Pakistan) at 277 km, Termez Airport (TMJ, Uzbekistan) at 348 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 1,002 km (about 1 h 40 min); Mumbai 1,758 km (about 2 h 35 min); Dubai 1,686 km (about 2 h 30 min); London Heathrow 5,730 km (about 7 h 25 min); New York JFK 10,835 km (about 13 h 35 min); Los Angeles 12,356 km (about 15 h 25 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
