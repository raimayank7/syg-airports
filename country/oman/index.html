Airports in Oman

Airport codes of Oman: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Oman has 18 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 5 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Duqm (DQM), Muscat/Seeb (MCT), Suhar (OHS), Salalah (SLL). The longest runway is at Muscat International Airport: 4,080 metres. The highest is Duqm International Airport, at 111 metres above sea level.

The airports of Oman in numbers

Of the 18 airports in Oman that carry an IATA code, 5 have scheduled airline flights and 13 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 4 large airports, 1 medium airport. That places Oman at number 114 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Khasab Airport (KHS) and the most southerly is Salalah International Airport (SLL). The two that are farthest apart are Khasab Airport and Salalah International Airport: about 1,039 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 45 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Al Wusta Governorate (1), Musandam Governorate (1), Masqat Governorate (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 6 m at Suhar International Airport to 111 m at Duqm International Airport, and the average is about 37 metres above sea level. Of the 5 airports with runway figures, 4 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 1 more reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Oman

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with OO: the first letter stands for the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is M, which opens 3 of the 18 codes. In 56% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Oman with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Asia/Muscat), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Oman to the rest of the world

Measured from Muscat International Airport (MCT), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Jask Airport (JSK, Iran) at 234 km, Fujairah International Airport (FJR, United Arab Emirates) at 260 km, Al Ain International Airport (AAN, United Arab Emirates) at 282 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 1,955 km (about 2 h 50 min); Mumbai 1,590 km (about 2 h 25 min); Dubai 347 km (about 55 min); London Heathrow 5,833 km (about 7 h 30 min); New York JFK 11,324 km (about 14 h 10 min); Los Angeles 13,607 km (about 16 h 55 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
