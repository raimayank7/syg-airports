Airports in Morocco

Airport codes of Morocco: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Morocco has 21 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 17 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Agadir (Temsia) (AGA), Oulad Yaich (BEM), Casablanca (CMN), Saïss (FEZ), Al Aaroui (NDR), Ahl Angad (OUD), Zagora (OZG), Ouarzazate (OZZ), Marrakesh (RAK), Rabat (RBA), Tangier (TNG), Tétouan (TTU). The longest runway is at Mohammed V International Airport: 3,720 metres. The highest is Ouarzazate International Airport, at 1,153 metres above sea level.

The airports of Morocco in numbers

Of the 21 airports in Morocco that carry an IATA code, 17 have scheduled airline flights and 4 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 12 large airports, 5 medium airports. That places Morocco at number 49 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Tangier Ibn Battuta Airport (TNG) and the most southerly is Tan Tan Airport (TTA). The two that are farthest apart are Oujda Angads Airport and Tan Tan Airport: about 1,122 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 1 h 50 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Tanger-Tétouan-Al Hoceïma Region (3), Drâa-Tafilalet Region (3), L'Oriental Region (3).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 3 m at Sania Ramel Airport to 1,153 m at Ouarzazate International Airport, and the average is about 410 metres above sea level. Of the 16 airports with runway figures, 9 have a runway of 3,200 metres or more, long enough for the largest long-distance aircraft; 6 more reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in Morocco

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with GM: the first letter stands for West Africa. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is O, which opens 3 of the 21 codes. In 57% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Morocco with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Africa/Casablanca), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Morocco to the rest of the world

Measured from Mohammed V International Airport (CMN), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Ceuta Heliport (JCU, Spain) at 350 km, Gibraltar Airport (GIB, Gibraltar) at 371 km, Jerez Airport (XRY, Spain) at 400 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 7,859 km (about 10 h 00 min); Mumbai 7,995 km (about 10 h 10 min); Dubai 6,081 km (about 7 h 50 min); London Heathrow 2,094 km (about 3 h 00 min); New York JFK 5,795 km (about 7 h 30 min); Los Angeles 9,620 km (about 12 h 05 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
