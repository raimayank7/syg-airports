The airports of French Guiana in numbers

Of the 8 airports in French Guiana that carry an IATA code, 3 have scheduled airline flights and 5 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 2 medium airports. That places French Guiana at number 143 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The airports with flights are Matoury (CAY), Saint-Laurent-du-Maroni (LDX), Maripasoula (MPY).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 5 m at Saint-Laurent-du-Maroni Airport to 124 m at Maripasoula Airport, and the average is about 46 metres above sea level. Of the 3 airports with runway figures, 1 reaches 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 2 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft.

The codes used in French Guiana

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with SO: the first letter stands for South America. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is O, which opens 2 of the 8 codes. In 38% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of French Guiana with flights keeps the same clock time (zone America/Cayenne), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
