Airports in North Macedonia

Airport codes of North Macedonia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

North Macedonia has 2 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 2 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Ohrid (OHD), Ilinden (SKP). The longest runway is at Skopje International Airport: 2,950 metres. The highest is Ohrid St. Paul the Apostle Airport, at 705 metres above sea level.

The airports of North Macedonia in numbers

Of the 2 airports in North Macedonia that carry an IATA code, 2 have scheduled airline flights. By kind, the airports with flights are: 2 large airports. That places North Macedonia at number 178 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The two airports with flights, Ohrid St. Paul the Apostle Airport (OHD) and Skopje International Airport (SKP), are about 113 km apart in a straight line.

Heights and runways

The longest runway, at Skopje International Airport, measures 2,950 metres: enough for most passenger jets.

The codes used in North Macedonia

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with LW: the first letter stands for southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code work.

Clock times

Every airport of North Macedonia with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Europe/Skopje), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.
