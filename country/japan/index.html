Airports in Japan

Airport codes of Japan: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Japan has 97 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 83 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Aomori (AOJ), Sapporo (CTS), Makinohara / Shimada (FSZ), Fukuoka (FUK), Hiroshima (HIJ), Hakodate (HKD), Tokyo (HND), Saga (HSG), Omitama (IBR), Osaka (ITM), Nankoku (KCZ), Niigata (KIJ) and 16 more. The longest runway is at Kansai International Airport: 4,000 metres. The highest is Shinshu-Matsumoto Airport, at 665 metres above sea level.
