Airports in Indonesia

Airport codes of Indonesia: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Indonesia has 243 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 121 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Ambon (AMQ), Banjarbaru (BDJ), Balikpapan (BPN), Batam (BTH), Banda Aceh (BTJ), Jakarta (CGK), Sentani (DJJ), Kuta, Badung (DPS), Jakarta (HLP), Beringin (KNO), Mataram (Pujut, Lombok Tengah) (LOP), Manado (MDC) and 7 more. The longest runway is at Hang Nadim International Airport: 4,029 metres. The highest is Bilorai Airport, at 2,240 metres above sea level.
