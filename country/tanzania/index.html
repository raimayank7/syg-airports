Airports in Tanzania

Airport codes of Tanzania: every airport with an IATA code, with its city. Type to filter.

Tanzania has 33 airports with an IATA code in the open data. 16 of them have scheduled airline flights and have their own page here. The large airports are Dar es Salaam (DAR), Arusha (JRO), Mwanza (MWZ), Zanzibar (ZNZ). The longest runway is at Kilimanjaro International Airport: 3,600 metres. The highest is Seronera Airport, at 1,548 metres above sea level.
