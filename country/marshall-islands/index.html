The airports of Marshall Islands in numbers

Of the 31 airports in Marshall Islands that carry an IATA code, 16 have scheduled airline flights and 15 do not. By kind, the airports with flights are: 1 large airport, 1 medium airport, 14 small airports. That places Marshall Islands at number 54 among the 234 countries and territories on this site, counted by airports with scheduled flights.

Where they are

The most northerly airport with flights is Eniwetok Airport (ENT) and the most southerly is Kili Airport (KIO). The two that are farthest apart are Enejit Airport and Eniwetok Airport: about 1,214 km in a straight line, which would take roughly 2 h 00 min in a jet. By state or region, the most airports with flights are in Mili (2), Aur (1), Eniwetok (1).

Heights and runways

Heights run from 1 m at Mili Island Airport to 9 m at Enejit Airport, and the average is about 4 metres above sea level. Of the 8 airports with runway figures, 2 reach 2,300 metres, enough for most passenger jets; 4 have nothing longer than 1,500 metres and take mainly propeller aircraft. The main runway is unpaved (gravel, grass, earth, coral or similar) at 5, hard (asphalt or concrete) at 3.

The codes used in Marshall Islands

The four-letter ICAO codes used by pilots here begin with PK: the first letter stands for the North Pacific, including Alaska and Hawaii. Among the three-letter IATA codes, the most common first letter is E, which opens 5 of the 31 codes. In 81% of them the code starts with the same letter as the city or airport name. Clock times

Every airport of Marshall Islands with flights keeps the same clock time (zone Pacific/Majuro), so departure and arrival times on a domestic ticket can be subtracted directly.

From Marshall Islands to the rest of the world

Measured from Marshall Islands International Airport (MAJ), the nearest airports with scheduled flights in other countries are Butaritari Airport (BBG, Kiribati) at 474 km, Bonriki International Airport (TRW, Kiribati) at 665 km, Aranuka Airport (AAK, Kiribati) at 809 km. Straight-line distances to some major airports: Delhi 10,037 km (about 12 h 35 min); Mumbai 10,625 km (about 13 h 20 min); Dubai 12,215 km (about 15 h 15 min); London Heathrow 13,458 km (about 16 h 45 min); New York JFK 11,536 km (about 14 h 25 min); Los Angeles 7,757 km (about 9 h 50 min). These are distances over the globe with a rough time for a direct jet. They do not mean that a direct flight exists.
