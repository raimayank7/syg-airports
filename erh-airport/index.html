Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ERH means Moulay Ali Cherif Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: GMFK. ICAO codes that begin with G belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 31.9475° N, 4.3983° W. It stands 1,045 metres (3,428 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 13/31, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 13 is heading about 130° on the compass, and one using the other end about 310°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 1,045 metres, Moulay Ali Cherif Airport stands higher than about 90% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 17 airports of Morocco with a height in the data, it is number 3 from the top. In a standard atmosphere the air pressure at this height is about 88% of the pressure at sea level. Engines and wings both work less well in thinner air, so the same aircraft needs more runway here than at the coast. It is about 3,550 km north of the equator.
