Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, IZA means Presidente Itamar Franco Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SBZM. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

The airport lies at 21.5131° S, 43.1731° W. It stands 411 metres (1,348 feet) above sea level.
