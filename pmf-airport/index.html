Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PMF means Parma Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: LIMP. ICAO codes that begin with L belong to southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 44.8264° N, 10.2971° E. It stands 49 metres (161 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 02/20, lies roughly south to north. An aircraft using the end marked 02 is heading about 20° on the compass, and one using the other end about 200°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 49 metres, Parma Airport stands higher than about 42% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 42 airports of Italy with a height in the data, it is number 17 from the top. It is about 4,980 km north of the equator.
