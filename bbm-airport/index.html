Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BBM means Battambang Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VDBG. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 13.0956° N, 103.2240° E. It stands 18 metres (59 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 07/25, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 07 is heading about 70° on the compass, and one using the other end about 250°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 18 metres, Battambang Airport stands higher than about 27% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 5 airports of Cambodia with a height in the data, it is number 3 from the top. It is about 1,460 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Battambang Airport there are 11 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. The nearest large airport is Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport (SAI), about 112 km away, close enough to reach by road in most cases.
