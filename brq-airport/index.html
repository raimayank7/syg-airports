Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BRQ means Brno-Tuřany Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: LKTB. ICAO codes that begin with L belong to southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 49.1513° N, 16.6940° E. It stands 237 metres (778 feet) above sea level.
