Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ABA means Abakan International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: UNAA. ICAO codes that begin with U belong to Russia and neighbouring states. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 53.7400° N, 91.3850° E. It stands 253 metres (831 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 02/20, lies roughly south to north. An aircraft using the end marked 02 is heading about 20° on the compass, and one using the other end about 200°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 253 metres, Abakan International Airport stands higher than about 69% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 147 airports of Russia with a height in the data, it is number 32 from the top. It is about 5,980 km north of the equator.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Abakan International Airport there are 3 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. That is few: the region is thinly served by air. The nearest other large airport is Krasnoyarsk International Airport (KJA), about 280 km away.
