Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TWT means Sanga Sanga Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: RPMN. ICAO codes that begin with R belong to East Asia, including Japan, Korea and the Philippines. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 5.0482° N, 119.7433° E. It stands 5 metres (15 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Lahad Datu, about 157 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 02/20, lies roughly south to north. An aircraft using the end marked 02 is heading about 20° on the compass, and one using the other end about 200°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 5 metres above sea level, Sanga Sanga Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 42 airports of Philippines with a height in the data, it is number 31 from the top. It is about 560 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
