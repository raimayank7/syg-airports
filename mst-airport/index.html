Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MST means Maastricht Aachen Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: EHBK. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 50.9111° N, 5.7694° E. It stands 114 metres (375 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Düsseldorf, about 79 km away in a straight line.

Maastricht Aachen Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 2,750 metres (9,022 feet). That is long enough for most passenger jets. Among the 5 airports of Netherlands on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 3.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 03/21, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 03 is heading about 30° on the compass, and one using the other end about 210°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 114 metres, Maastricht Aachen Airport stands higher than about 56% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 5 airports of Netherlands with a height in the data, it is the highest. It is about 5,660 km north of the equator.

Other airports around it

The nearest other large airport is Eindhoven Airport (EIN), about 66 km away, close enough to reach by road in most cases.
