Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ASF means Astrakhan Narimanovo Boris M. Kustodiev International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: URWA. ICAO codes that begin with U belong to Russia and neighbouring states. How the two kinds of code differ.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 09/27, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 09 is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

Astrakhan Narimanovo Boris M. Kustodiev International Airport lies below sea level, which very few airports do. Among the 147 airports of Russia with a height in the data, it is the lowest. It is about 5,150 km north of the equator.

Other airports around it

The nearest other large airport is Atyrau International Airport (GUW) in Kazakhstan, about 305 km away, roughly 50 min in the air.
