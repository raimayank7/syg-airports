Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, JCU means Ceuta Heliport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: GECE. ICAO codes that begin with G belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 35.8927° N, 5.3063° W. It stands 2 metres (8 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 07/25, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 07 is heading about 70° on the compass, and one using the other end about 250°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 2 metres above sea level, Ceuta Heliport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 44 airports of Spain with a height in the data, it is the lowest. It is about 3,990 km north of the equator.
