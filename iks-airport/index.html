Tiksi, Sakha (Yakutia) Republic, Russia

Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, IKS means Tiksi Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: UEST. ICAO codes that begin with U belong to Russia and neighbouring states. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 71.6977° N, 128.9030° E. It stands 8 metres (26 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Tiksi Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 3,001 metres (9,845 feet). That is long enough for most passenger jets. Among the 136 airports of Russia on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 41.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 03/21, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 03 is heading about 30° on the compass, and one using the other end about 210°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 8 metres above sea level, Tiksi Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 147 airports of Russia with a height in the data, it is number 141 from the top. It is about 7,970 km north of the equator, inside the Arctic Circle, where the sun does not set for part of the summer and does not rise for part of the winter.

Other airports around it

Tiksi Airport is an isolated airport: no other airport with scheduled flights lies within 300 km of it. The nearest is Ust-Kuyga Airport (UKG), about 309 km away. The nearest large airport is Platon Oyunsky Yakutsk International Airport (YKS), about 1,069 km away, roughly 1 h 45 min in the air.
