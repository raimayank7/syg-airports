Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, GMP means Seoul Gimpo International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: RKSS. ICAO codes that begin with R belong to East Asia, including Japan, Korea and the Philippines. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 37.5583° N, 126.7910° E. It stands 18 metres (59 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 14L/32R, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 14L is heading about 140° on the compass, and one using the other end about 320°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. All 2 runways here point the same way, so they can be used side by side.
