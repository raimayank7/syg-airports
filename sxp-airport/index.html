Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 09W/27W, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 09W is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 3 runways point in 3 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 4 metres above sea level, Nunam Iqua Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 645 airports of United States with a height in the data, it is number 620 from the top. It is about 6,950 km north of the equator, far enough from it for very long summer days and very short winter ones.
