Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, XIY means Xi'an Xianyang International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: ZLXY. ICAO codes that begin with Z belong to China, Mongolia and North Korea. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 34.4422° N, 108.7624° E. It stands 479 metres (1,572 feet) above sea level.
