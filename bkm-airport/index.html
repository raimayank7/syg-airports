Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BKM means Bakalalan Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: WBGQ. ICAO codes that begin with W belong to Indonesia, Malaysia and nearby island countries. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 3.9740° N, 115.6180° E. It stands 884 metres (2,900 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Miri, about 186 km away in a straight line.
