Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, JOI means Lauro Carneiro de Loyola Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SBJV. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

The airport lies at 26.2245° S, 48.7974° W. It stands 5 metres (15 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 15/33, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 15 is heading about 150° on the compass, and one using the other end about 330°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 5 metres above sea level, Lauro Carneiro de Loyola Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 129 airports of Brazil with a height in the data, it is number 127 from the top. It is about 2,920 km south of the equator.
