Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TMS means São Tomé International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: FPST. ICAO codes that begin with F belong to central and southern Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 0.3782° N, 6.7122° E. It stands 10 metres (33 feet) above sea level.
