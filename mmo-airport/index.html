Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MMO means Maio Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: GVMA. ICAO codes that begin with G belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 15.1559° N, 23.2137° W. It stands 11 metres (36 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Praia, about 41 km away in a straight line.

Runways

Maio Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,200 metres (3,937 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 7 airports of Cape Verde on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 6.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 01/19, lies roughly south to north. An aircraft using the end marked 01 is heading about 10° on the compass, and one using the other end about 190°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 11 metres above sea level, Maio Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 7 airports of Cape Verde with a height in the data, it is the lowest. It is about 1,690 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Maio Airport there are 6 other airports with scheduled flights, and 1 of them is within 100 km. The nearest large airport is Nelson Mandela International Airport (RAI), about 38 km away, close enough to reach by road in most cases.
