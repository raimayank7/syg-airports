Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, KMS means Prempeh I International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: DGSI. ICAO codes that begin with D belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 6.7146° N, 1.5908° W. It stands 287 metres (942 feet) above sea level.
