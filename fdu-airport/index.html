Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, FDU means Bandundu Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: FZBO. ICAO codes that begin with F belong to central and southern Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 3.3113° S, 17.3817° E. It stands 324 metres (1,063 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 11/29, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 11 is heading about 110° on the compass, and one using the other end about 290°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

It is about 370 km south of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
