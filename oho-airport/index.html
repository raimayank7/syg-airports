Okhotsk Airport (OHO)

Okhotsk, Khabarovsk Krai, Russia

Okhotsk Airport is a medium-sized airport serving Okhotsk, in Khabarovsk Krai, Russia. It has scheduled airline flights. Its IATA code, the three letters printed on tickets and baggage tags, is OHO.

Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, OHO means Okhotsk Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: UHOO. ICAO codes that begin with U belong to Russia and neighbouring states. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 59.4101° N, 143.0565° E. It stands 14 metres (45 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Okhotsk Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 2,000 metres (6,562 feet). That suits narrow-body jets and regional aircraft. Among the 136 airports of Russia on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 105.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 03/21, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 03 is heading about 30° on the compass, and one using the other end about 210°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 14 metres above sea level, Okhotsk Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 147 airports of Russia with a height in the data, it is number 138 from the top. It is about 6,610 km north of the equator, far enough from it for very long summer days and very short winter ones.

Other airports around it

Okhotsk Airport is an isolated airport: no other airport with scheduled flights lies within 300 km of it. The nearest is Sokol Airport (GDX), about 434 km away. The nearest large airport is Platon Oyunsky Yakutsk International Airport (YKS), about 779 km away, roughly 1 h 25 min in the air.
