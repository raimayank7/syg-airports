Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SNN means Shannon Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: EINN. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 52.7020° N, 8.9248° W. It stands 14 metres (46 feet) above sea level.
