Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, GPS means Seymour Galapagos Ecological Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SEGS. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 0.4538° S, 90.2659° W. It stands 63 metres (207 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Seymour Galapagos Ecological Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 2,401 metres (7,877 feet). That is long enough for most passenger jets. Among the 15 airports of Ecuador on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 9.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 14/32, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 14 is heading about 140° on the compass, and one using the other end about 320°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

It is about 50 km south of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
