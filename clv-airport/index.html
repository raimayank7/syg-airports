Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CLV means Nelson Ribeiro Guimarães Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SBCN. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 17.7251° S, 48.6063° W. It stands 703 metres (2,307 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Catalão, about 85 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 9/27, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 9 is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

It is about 1,970 km south of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
