Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MUX means Multan International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: OPMT. ICAO codes that begin with O belong to the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. How the two kinds of code differ.

The airport lies at 30.2032° N, 71.4191° E. It stands 123 metres (403 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 18/36, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 18 is heading about 180° on the compass, and one using the other end about 360°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.
