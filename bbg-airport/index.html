Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BBG means Butaritari Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: NGTU. ICAO codes that begin with N belong to the South Pacific. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 3.0858° N, 172.8110° E. It stands 2 metres (5 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Tarawa, about 196 km away in a straight line.

Height and position

At 2 metres above sea level, Butaritari Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. It is about 340 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Butaritari Airport there is 1 other airport with scheduled flights. That is few: the region is thinly served by air. The nearest large airport is Bonriki International Airport (TRW), about 193 km away.
