Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PUJ means Punta Cana International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MDPC. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 18.5671° N, 68.3646° W. It stands 14 metres (47 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 08/26, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 08 is heading about 80° on the compass, and one using the other end about 260°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 14 metres above sea level, Punta Cana International Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 8 airports of Dominican Republic with a height in the data, it is number 5 from the top. It is about 2,060 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
