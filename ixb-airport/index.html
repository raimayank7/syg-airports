Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, IXB means Bagdogra Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VEBD. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 26.6812° N, 88.3286° E. It stands 126 metres (412 feet) above sea level.
