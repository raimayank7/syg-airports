Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PMQ means Perito Moreno Jalil Hamer Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SAWP. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 46.5379° S, 70.9787° W. It stands 430 metres (1,410 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Coyhaique, about 136 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 10/28, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 10 is heading about 100° on the compass, and one using the other end about 280°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 3 runways point in 3 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.
