Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SKG means Thessaloniki Macedonia International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: LGTS. ICAO codes that begin with L belong to southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 40.5193° N, 22.9700° E. It stands 7 metres (22 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 10/28, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 10 is heading about 100° on the compass, and one using the other end about 280°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 7 metres above sea level, Thessaloniki Macedonia International Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 39 airports of Greece with a height in the data, it is number 29 from the top. It is about 4,510 km north of the equator.
