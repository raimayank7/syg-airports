Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, JOL means Jolo Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: RPMJ. ICAO codes that begin with R belong to East Asia, including Japan, Korea and the Philippines. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 6.0537° N, 121.0110° E. It stands 36 metres (118 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 09/27, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 09 is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 36 metres, Jolo Airport stands higher than about 38% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 42 airports of Philippines with a height in the data, it is number 12 from the top. It is about 670 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Jolo Airport there are 3 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. That is few: the region is thinly served by air. The nearest large airport is Zamboanga International Airport (ZAM), about 151 km away.
