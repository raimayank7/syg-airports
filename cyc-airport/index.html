Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CYC means Caye Chapel Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MZCP. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 17.6838° N, 88.0450° W. It stands 1 metres (3 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Chetumal, about 97 km away in a straight line.

Height and position

At 1 metre above sea level, Caye Chapel Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 12 airports of Belize with a height in the data, it is number 10 from the top. It is about 1,970 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
