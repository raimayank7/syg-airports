Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TBH means Tugdan Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: RPVU. ICAO codes that begin with R belong to East Asia, including Japan, Korea and the Philippines. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 12.3110° N, 122.0850° E. It stands 3 metres (10 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Roxas City, about 108 km away in a straight line.

Runways

Tugdan Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,390 metres (4,560 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 43 airports of Philippines on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 35.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 03/21, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 03 is heading about 30° on the compass, and one using the other end about 210°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 3 metres above sea level, Tugdan Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 42 airports of Philippines with a height in the data, it is number 39 from the top. It is about 1,370 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Tugdan Airport there are 16 other airports with scheduled flights, and 2 of them are within 100 km. The nearest large airport is Kalibo International Airport (KLO), about 77 km away, close enough to reach by road in most cases.
