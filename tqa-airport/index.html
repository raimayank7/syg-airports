Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TQA means Tasiusaq Heliport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: BGTA. ICAO codes that begin with B belong to Greenland, Iceland and Kosovo. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 73.3731° N, 56.0603° W. It stands 55 metres (181 feet) above sea level.
