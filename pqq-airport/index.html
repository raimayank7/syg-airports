Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PQQ means Port Macquarie Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: YPMQ. ICAO codes that begin with Y belong to Australia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 31.4358° S, 152.8630° E. It stands 4 metres (12 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Newcastle, about 195 km away in a straight line.
