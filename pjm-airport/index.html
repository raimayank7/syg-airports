Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PJM means Puerto Jimenez Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MRPJ. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 8.5333° N, 83.3000° W. It stands 2 metres (7 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is San José, about 178 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 16/34, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 16 is heading about 160° on the compass, and one using the other end about 340°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 2 metres above sea level, Puerto Jimenez Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 9 airports of Costa Rica with a height in the data, it is the lowest. It is about 950 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
