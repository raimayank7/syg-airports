Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PDL means João Paulo II Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: LPPD. ICAO codes that begin with L belong to southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 37.7412° N, 25.6979° W. It stands 79 metres (259 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 12/30, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 12 is heading about 120° on the compass, and one using the other end about 300°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.
