Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, FDH means Bodensee Airport Friedrichshafen. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: EDNY. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 47.6713° N, 9.5115° E. It stands 417 metres (1,367 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Zürich, about 80 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 06/24, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 06 is heading about 60° on the compass, and one using the other end about 240°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. All 2 runways here point the same way, so they can be used side by side.
