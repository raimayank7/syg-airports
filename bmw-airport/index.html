Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BMW means Bordj Badji Mokhtar Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: DATM. ICAO codes that begin with D belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 21.3778° N, 0.9270° E. It stands 397 metres (1,303 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Bordj Badji Mokhtar Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 3,000 metres (9,843 feet). That is long enough for most passenger jets. Among the 31 airports of Algeria on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 12.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 08/26, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 08 is heading about 80° on the compass, and one using the other end about 260°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 397 metres, Bordj Badji Mokhtar Airport stands higher than about 77% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 31 airports of Algeria with a height in the data, it is number 16 from the top. It is about 2,380 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
