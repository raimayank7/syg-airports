Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CDR means Chadron Municipal Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KCDR. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 42.8376° N, 103.0950° W. It stands 1,005 metres (3,297 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Rapid City, about 139 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 03/21, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 03 is heading about 30° on the compass, and one using the other end about 210°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 1,005 metres, Chadron Municipal Airport stands higher than about 90% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 645 airports of United States with a height in the data, it is number 65 from the top. In a standard atmosphere the air pressure at this height is about 89% of the pressure at sea level. Engines and wings both work less well in thinner air, so the same aircraft needs more runway here than at the coast. It is about 4,760 km north of the equator.
