Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TAS means Tashkent International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: UZTT. ICAO codes that begin with U belong to Russia and neighbouring states. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 41.2579° N, 69.2812° E. It stands 432 metres (1,417 feet) above sea level.
