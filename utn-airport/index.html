Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, UTN means Upington Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: FAUP. ICAO codes that begin with F belong to central and southern Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 28.4002° S, 21.2636° E. It stands 848 metres (2,782 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 17/35, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 17 is heading about 170° on the compass, and one using the other end about 350°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 3 runways point in 3 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 848 metres, Upington Airport stands higher than about 88% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 22 airports of South Africa with a height in the data, it is number 9 from the top. It is about 3,160 km south of the equator.
