Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, IXP means Pathankot Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VIPK. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 32.2336° N, 75.6344° E. It stands 310 metres (1,017 feet) above sea level.
