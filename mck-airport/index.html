Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MCK means McCook Ben Nelson Regional Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KMCK. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 40.2078° N, 100.5928° W. It stands 787 metres (2,583 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is North Platte, about 103 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 12/30, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 12 is heading about 120° on the compass, and one using the other end about 300°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 3 runways point in 3 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.
