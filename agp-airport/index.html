Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, AGP means Málaga-Costa del Sol Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: LEMG. ICAO codes that begin with L belong to southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code differ.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 13/31, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 13 is heading about 130° on the compass, and one using the other end about 310°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.
