Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, DCA means Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KDCA. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 38.8521° N, 77.0377° W. It stands 5 metres (15 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 01/19, lies roughly south to north. An aircraft using the end marked 01 is heading about 10° on the compass, and one using the other end about 190°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 3 runways point in 3 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 5 metres above sea level, Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 645 airports of United States with a height in the data, it is number 594 from the top. It is about 4,320 km north of the equator.
