Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, LWK means Lerwick / Tingwall Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: EGET. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 60.1922° N, 1.2436° W. It stands 13 metres (43 feet) above sea level.
