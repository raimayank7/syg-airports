Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ABY means Southwest Georgia Regional Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KABY. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 31.5329° N, 84.1962° W. It stands 60 metres (197 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Tallahassee, about 122 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 05/23, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 05 is heading about 50° on the compass, and one using the other end about 230°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.
