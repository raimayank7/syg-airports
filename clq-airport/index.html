Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CLQ means Licenciado Miguel de la Madrid International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MMIA. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 19.2770° N, 103.5770° W. It stands 752 metres (2,467 feet) above sea level.
