Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TML means Yakubu Tali International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: DGLE. ICAO codes that begin with D belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 9.5539° N, 0.8661° W. It stands 169 metres (553 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 05/23, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 05 is heading about 50° on the compass, and one using the other end about 230°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 169 metres, Yakubu Tali International Airport stands higher than about 61% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 5 airports of Ghana with a height in the data, it is number 3 from the top. It is about 1,060 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it
