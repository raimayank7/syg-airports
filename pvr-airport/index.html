Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PVR means Puerto Vallarta International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MMPR. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 20.6799° N, 105.2544° W. It stands 7 metres (23 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 04/22, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 04 is heading about 40° on the compass, and one using the other end about 220°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 7 metres above sea level, Puerto Vallarta International Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 63 airports of Mexico with a height in the data, it is number 58 from the top. It is about 2,300 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Puerto Vallarta International Airport there are 5 other airports with scheduled flights, and 1 of them is within 100 km. The nearest other large airport is Guadalajara International Airport (GDL), about 203 km away.
