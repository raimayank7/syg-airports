Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SNX means Semnan Municipal Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: OIIS. ICAO codes that begin with O belong to the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 35.5911° N, 53.4951° E. It stands 1,117 metres (3,665 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 04/22, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 04 is heading about 40° on the compass, and one using the other end about 220°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 1,117 metres, Semnan Municipal Airport stands higher than about 91% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 45 airports of Iran with a height in the data, it is number 17 from the top. In a standard atmosphere the air pressure at this height is about 87% of the pressure at sea level. Engines and wings both work less well in thinner air, so the same aircraft needs more runway here than at the coast. It is about 3,960 km north of the equator.
