Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TRI means Tri-Cities Regional TN/VA Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KTRI. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 36.4752° N, 82.4074° W. It stands 463 metres (1,519 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Knoxville, about 147 km away in a straight line.
