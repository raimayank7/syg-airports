Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BMV means Buon Ma Thuot Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VVBM. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 12.6683° N, 108.1200° E. It stands 527 metres (1,729 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 09/27, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 09 is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 527 metres, Buon Ma Thuot Airport stands higher than about 82% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 22 airports of Vietnam with a height in the data, it is number 3 from the top. It is about 1,410 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
