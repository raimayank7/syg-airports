Santa Cruz/Graciosa Bay/Luova Airport is a small airport serving Santa Cruz/Graciosa Bay/Luova, in Temotu Province, Solomon Islands. It has scheduled airline flights. Its IATA code, the three letters printed on tickets and baggage tags, is SCZ.

Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SCZ means Santa Cruz/Graciosa Bay/Luova Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: AGGL. ICAO codes that begin with A belong to the western South Pacific. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 10.7203° S, 165.7950° E. It stands 5 metres (18 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Santa Cruz/Graciosa Bay/Luova Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 914 metres (2,999 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 18 airports of Solomon Islands on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 7.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 05/23, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 05 is heading about 50° on the compass, and one using the other end about 230°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 5 metres above sea level, Santa Cruz/Graciosa Bay/Luova Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 18 airports of Solomon Islands with a height in the data, it is number 10 from the top. It is about 1,190 km south of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Santa Cruz/Graciosa Bay/Luova Airport is an isolated airport: no other airport with scheduled flights lies within 300 km of it. The nearest is Santa Ana Airport (NNB), about 365 km away. The nearest large airport is Honiara International Airport (HIR), about 645 km away, roughly 1 h 15 min in the air.
