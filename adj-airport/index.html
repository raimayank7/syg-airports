Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ADJ means Marka International (Amman Civil) Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: OJAM. ICAO codes that begin with O belong to the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 31.9727° N, 35.9916° E. It stands 779 metres (2,555 feet) above sea level.
