Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ADF means Adıyaman Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: LTCP. ICAO codes that begin with L belong to southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 37.7314° N, 38.4689° E. It stands 675 metres (2,216 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Şanlıurfa, about 69 km away in a straight line.
