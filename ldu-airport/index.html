Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, LDU means Lahad Datu Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: WBKD. ICAO codes that begin with W belong to Indonesia, Malaysia and nearby island countries. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 5.0324° N, 118.3238° E. It stands 14 metres (45 feet) above sea level.
