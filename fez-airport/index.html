Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, FEZ means Fes Saïss International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: GMFF. ICAO codes that begin with G belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 33.9273° N, 4.9780° W. It stands 579 metres (1,900 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 09/27, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 09 is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 579 metres, Fes Saïss International Airport stands higher than about 83% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 17 airports of Morocco with a height in the data, it is number 5 from the top. It is about 3,770 km north of the equator.
