Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CPX means Benjamin Rivera Noriega Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: TJCP. ICAO codes that begin with T belong to the Caribbean. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 18.3130° N, 65.3039° W. It stands 15 metres (49 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Carolina, about 69 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 13/31, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 13 is heading about 130° on the compass, and one using the other end about 310°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 15 metres above sea level, Benjamin Rivera Noriega Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 8 airports of Puerto Rico with a height in the data, it is number 2 from the top. It is about 2,040 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
