Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, YOL means Yola Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: DNYO. ICAO codes that begin with D belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 9.2576° N, 12.4304° E. It stands 183 metres (599 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 17/35, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 17 is heading about 170° on the compass, and one using the other end about 350°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 183 metres, Yola Airport stands higher than about 63% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 24 airports of Nigeria with a height in the data, it is number 13 from the top. It is about 1,030 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
