Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CAP means Cap Haitien International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MTCH. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 19.7255° N, 72.2007° W. It stands 3 metres (10 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Port-de-Paix, about 70 km away in a straight line.
