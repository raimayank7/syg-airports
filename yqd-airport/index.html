Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, YQD means The Pas Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CYQD. ICAO codes that begin with C belong to Canada. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 53.9714° N, 101.0910° W. It stands 270 metres (887 feet) above sea level.

Runways

The Pas Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,799 metres (5,901 feet). That suits narrow-body jets and regional aircraft. Among the 245 airports of Canada on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 80.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 13/31, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 13 is heading about 130° on the compass, and one using the other end about 310°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 270 metres, The Pas Airport stands higher than about 70% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 255 airports of Canada with a height in the data, it is number 77 from the top. It is about 6,000 km north of the equator.
