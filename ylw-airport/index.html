Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, YLW means Kelowna International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CYLW. ICAO codes that begin with C belong to Canada. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 49.9561° N, 119.3780° W. It stands 433 metres (1,421 feet) above sea level.
