Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TCD means Tarapacá Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SKRA. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 2.8958° S, 69.7498° W. It stands 77 metres (253 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is São Paulo de Olivença, about 111 km away in a straight line.
