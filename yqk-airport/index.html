Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, YQK means Kenora Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CYQK. ICAO codes that begin with C belong to Canada. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 49.7883° N, 94.3631° W. It stands 406 metres (1,332 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Winnipeg, about 200 km away in a straight line.
