Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PUB means Pueblo Memorial Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KPUB. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 38.2891° N, 104.4970° W. It stands 1,440 metres (4,726 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Colorado Springs, about 67 km away in a straight line.
