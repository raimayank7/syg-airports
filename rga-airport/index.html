Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, RGA means Gobernador Ramón Trejo Noel International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SAWE. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

The airport lies at 53.7777° S, 67.7494° W. It stands 20 metres (65 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 7/25, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 7 is heading about 70° on the compass, and one using the other end about 250°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.
