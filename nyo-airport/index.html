Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, NYO means Stockholm Skavsta Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: ESKN. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 58.7897° N, 16.9115° E. It stands 43 metres (140 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Linköping, about 86 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 08/26, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 08 is heading about 80° on the compass, and one using the other end about 260°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 43 metres, Stockholm Skavsta Airport stands higher than about 40% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 38 airports of Sweden with a height in the data, it is number 26 from the top. It is about 6,540 km north of the equator, far enough from it for very long summer days and very short winter ones.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Stockholm Skavsta Airport there are 19 other airports with scheduled flights, and 4 of them are within 100 km. The nearest other large airport is Linköping City Airport (LPI), about 83 km away, close enough to reach by road in most cases.
