Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, JSK means Jask Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: OIZJ. ICAO codes that begin with O belong to the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 25.6548° N, 57.8017° E. It stands 6 metres (19 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Fujairah, about 158 km away in a straight line.
