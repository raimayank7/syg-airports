Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, AKY means Sittwe Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VYSW. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 20.1332° N, 92.8707° E. It stands 8 metres (27 feet) above sea level.
