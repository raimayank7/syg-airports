Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MOU means Mountain Village Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: PAMO. ICAO codes that begin with P belong to the North Pacific, including Alaska and Hawaii. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 62.0954° N, 163.6820° W. It stands 103 metres (337 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Mountain Village Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,067 metres (3,501 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 675 airports of United States on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 577.
