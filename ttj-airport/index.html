Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TTJ means Tottori Sand Dunes Conan Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: RJOR. ICAO codes that begin with R belong to East Asia, including Japan, Korea and the Philippines. How the two kinds of code differ.
