Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, HVG means Honningsvåg Airport, Valan. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: ENHV. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 71.0097° N, 25.9836° E. It stands 13 metres (44 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Alta, about 153 km away in a straight line.
