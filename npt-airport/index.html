Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, NPT means Newport State Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KUUU. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 41.5322° N, 71.2810° W. It stands 52 metres (172 feet) above sea level.
