Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, RSI means Red Sea International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: OERS. ICAO codes that begin with O belong to the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 25.6280° N, 37.0889° E. It stands 43 metres (140 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Yanbu, about 197 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 15L/33R, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 15L is heading about 150° on the compass, and one using the other end about 330°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 43 metres, Red Sea International Airport stands higher than about 40% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 29 airports of Saudi Arabia with a height in the data, it is number 23 from the top. It is about 2,850 km north of the equator.
