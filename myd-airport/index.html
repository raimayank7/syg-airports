Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MYD means Malindi International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: HKML. ICAO codes that begin with H belong to east and north-east Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 3.2293° S, 40.1017° E. It stands 24 metres (80 feet) above sea level.
