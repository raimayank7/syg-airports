Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, AJF means Al-Jawf International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: OESK. ICAO codes that begin with O belong to the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 29.7833° N, 40.1009° E. It stands 689 metres (2,261 feet) above sea level.
