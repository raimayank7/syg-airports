Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BGI means Grantley Adams International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: TBPB. ICAO codes that begin with T belong to the Caribbean. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 13.0747° N, 59.4910° W. It stands 52 metres (169 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 09/27, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 09 is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 52 metres, Grantley Adams International Airport stands higher than about 43% of the 4,004 airports on this site. It is about 1,450 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
