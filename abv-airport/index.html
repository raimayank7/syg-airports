Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ABV means Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: DNAA. ICAO codes that begin with D belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 9.0068° N, 7.2632° E. It stands 342 metres (1,123 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 04/22, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 04 is heading about 40° on the compass, and one using the other end about 220°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

It is about 1,000 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
