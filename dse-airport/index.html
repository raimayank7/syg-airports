Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, DSE means Kombolcha Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: HADC. ICAO codes that begin with H belong to east and north-east Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 11.1098° N, 39.7259° E. It stands 1,865 metres (6,120 feet) above sea level. That is high for an airport. Thin air at this height means aircraft need a longer run to take off.

Height and position

At 1,865 metres, Kombolcha Airport stands higher than about 97% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 24 airports of Ethiopia with a height in the data, it is number 7 from the top. In a standard atmosphere the air pressure at this height is about 80% of the pressure at sea level. Engines and wings both work less well in thinner air, so the same aircraft needs more runway here than at the coast. It is about 1,240 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
