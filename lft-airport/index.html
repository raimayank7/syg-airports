Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, LFT means Lafayette Regional Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KLFT. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 04R/22L, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 04R is heading about 40° on the compass, and one using the other end about 220°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 3 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 13 metres above sea level, Lafayette Regional Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 645 airports of United States with a height in the data, it is number 516 from the top. It is about 3,360 km north of the equator.
