Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, YNP means Natuashish Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CNH2. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 55.9139° N, 61.1844° W. It stands 9 metres (30 feet) above sea level.

Runways

The open data has no runway details for this airport.

Height and position

At 9 metres above sea level, Natuashish Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 255 airports of Canada with a height in the data, it is number 244 from the top. It is about 6,220 km north of the equator, far enough from it for very long summer days and very short winter ones.
