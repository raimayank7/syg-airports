Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, XTL means Tadoule Lake Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CYBQ. ICAO codes that begin with C belong to Canada. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 58.7063° N, 98.5111° W. It stands 281 metres (923 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Tadoule Lake Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 976 metres (3,201 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 245 airports of Canada on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 224.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 07/25, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 07 is heading about 70° on the compass, and one using the other end about 250°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 281 metres, Tadoule Lake Airport stands higher than about 71% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 255 airports of Canada with a height in the data, it is number 73 from the top. It is about 6,530 km north of the equator, far enough from it for very long summer days and very short winter ones.
