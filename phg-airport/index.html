Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PHG means Port Harcourt City Airport / Port Harcourt Air Force Base. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: DNPM. ICAO codes that begin with D belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 4.8461° N, 7.0214° E. It stands 17 metres (57 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 04/26, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 04 is heading about 40° on the compass, and one using the other end about 220°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 17 metres, Port Harcourt City Airport / Port Harcourt Air Force Base stands higher than about 27% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 24 airports of Nigeria with a height in the data, it is the lowest. It is about 540 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
