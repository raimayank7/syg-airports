Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MCY means Sunshine Coast Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: YBSU. ICAO codes that begin with Y belong to Australia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 26.5933° S, 153.0832° E. It stands 5 metres (15 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 13/31, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 13 is heading about 130° on the compass, and one using the other end about 310°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 5 metres above sea level, Sunshine Coast Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 152 airports of Australia with a height in the data, it is number 135 from the top. It is about 2,960 km south of the equator.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Sunshine Coast Airport there are 8 other airports with scheduled flights, and 1 of them is within 100 km. The nearest other large airport is Brisbane International Airport (BNE), about 88 km away, close enough to reach by road in most cases.
