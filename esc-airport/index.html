Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ESC means Delta County Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KESC. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 45.7232° N, 87.0886° W. It stands 186 metres (609 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Green Bay, about 153 km away in a straight line.
