Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MNX means Manicoré Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SBMY. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 5.8114° S, 61.2783° W. It stands 53 metres (174 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 5/23, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 5 is heading about 50° on the compass, and one using the other end about 230°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 53 metres, Manicoré Airport stands higher than about 44% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 129 airports of Brazil with a height in the data, it is number 97 from the top. It is about 650 km south of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Manicoré Airport there is 1 other airport with scheduled flights. That is few: the region is thinly served by air. The nearest large airport is Eduardo Gomes International Airport (MAO), about 337 km away, roughly 55 min in the air.
