Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, OSS means Osh International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: UCFO. ICAO codes that begin with U belong to Russia and neighbouring states. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 40.6090° N, 72.7933° E. It stands 892 metres (2,927 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 12/30, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 12 is heading about 120° on the compass, and one using the other end about 300°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 892 metres, Osh International Airport stands higher than about 89% of the 4,004 airports on this site. It is about 4,520 km north of the equator.
