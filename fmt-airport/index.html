Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, FMT means Faresmaathoda Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VRQF. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.
