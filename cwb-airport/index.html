Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CWB means Curitiba-Afonso Pena International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SBCT. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

The airport lies at 25.5285° S, 49.1758° W. It stands 911 metres (2,988 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 15/33, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 15 is heading about 150° on the compass, and one using the other end about 330°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.
