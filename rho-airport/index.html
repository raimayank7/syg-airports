Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, RHO means Rhodes International Airport "Diagoras". Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: LGRP. ICAO codes that begin with L belong to southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 36.4054° N, 28.0862° E. It stands 5 metres (17 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Aydın, about 162 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 06/24, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 06 is heading about 60° on the compass, and one using the other end about 240°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 5 metres above sea level, Rhodes International Airport "Diagoras" is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 39 airports of Greece with a height in the data, it is number 32 from the top. It is about 4,050 km north of the equator.
