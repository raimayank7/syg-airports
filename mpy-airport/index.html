Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MPY means Maripasoula Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SOOA. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 3.6559° N, 54.0394° W. It stands 124 metres (406 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Maripasoula Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,200 metres (3,938 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 07/25, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 07 is heading about 70° on the compass, and one using the other end about 250°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 124 metres, Maripasoula Airport stands higher than about 56% of the 4,004 airports on this site. It is about 410 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Maripasoula Airport there are 7 other airports with scheduled flights, and 1 of them is within 100 km. The nearest large airport is Cayenne – Félix Eboué Airport (CAY), about 227 km away.
