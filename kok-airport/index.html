Kokkola-Pietarsaari Airport (KOK)

Kokkola / Kruunupyy, Central Ostrobothnia Region, Finland

Kokkola-Pietarsaari Airport is a medium-sized airport serving Kokkola / Kruunupyy, in Central Ostrobothnia Region, Finland. It has scheduled airline flights. Its IATA code, the three letters printed on tickets and baggage tags, is KOK.

Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, KOK means Kokkola-Pietarsaari Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: EFKK. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 63.7212° N, 23.1431° E. It stands 26 metres (84 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Umeå, about 142 km away in a straight line.
