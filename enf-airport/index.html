Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ENF means Enontekio Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: EFET. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 68.3626° N, 23.4243° E. It stands 306 metres (1,005 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Kiruna, about 144 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 03/21, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 03 is heading about 30° on the compass, and one using the other end about 210°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 306 metres, Enontekio Airport stands higher than about 72% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 20 airports of Finland with a height in the data, it is the highest. It is about 7,600 km north of the equator, inside the Arctic Circle, where the sun does not set for part of the summer and does not rise for part of the winter.
