Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ORB means Örebro Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: ESOE. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 59.2237° N, 15.0380° E. It stands 57 metres (188 feet) above sea level.
