Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PHW means Hendrik Van Eck Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: FAPH. ICAO codes that begin with F belong to central and southern Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 23.9372° S, 31.1554° E. It stands 436 metres (1,432 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 01/19, lies roughly south to north. An aircraft using the end marked 01 is heading about 10° on the compass, and one using the other end about 190°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

At 436 metres, Hendrik Van Eck Airport stands higher than about 79% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 22 airports of South Africa with a height in the data, it is number 13 from the top. It is about 2,660 km south of the equator.
