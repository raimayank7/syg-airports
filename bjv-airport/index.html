Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BJV means Milas Bodrum International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: LTFE. ICAO codes that begin with L belong to southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 37.2493° N, 27.6640° E. It stands 6 metres (21 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Aydın, about 68 km away in a straight line.
