Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, XNA means Northwest Arkansas National Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KXNA. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

The airport lies at 36.2819° N, 94.3068° W. It stands 392 metres (1,287 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Broken Arrow, about 136 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 16L/34R, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 16L is heading about 160° on the compass, and one using the other end about 340°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. All 2 runways here point the same way, so they can be used side by side.
