Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ORV means Robert (Bob) Curtis Memorial Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: PFNO. ICAO codes that begin with P belong to the North Pacific, including Alaska and Hawaii. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 66.8179° N, 161.0190° W. It stands 17 metres (55 feet) above sea level.
