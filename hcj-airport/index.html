Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, HCJ means Hechi Jinchengjiang Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: ZGHC. ICAO codes that begin with Z belong to China, Mongolia and North Korea. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 24.8043° N, 107.7108° E. It stands 677 metres (2,221 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Hechi, about 40 km away in a straight line.
