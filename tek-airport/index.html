Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TEK means Tatitlek Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: PAKA. ICAO codes that begin with P belong to the North Pacific, including Alaska and Hawaii. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 60.8714° N, 146.6903° W. It stands 19 metres (62 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Anchorage, about 177 km away in a straight line.
