Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ART means Watertown International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KART. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 43.9919° N, 76.0217° W. It stands 99 metres (325 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Kingston, about 45 km away in a straight line.
