Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, DUR means King Shaka International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: FALE. ICAO codes that begin with F belong to central and southern Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 29.6134° S, 31.1179° E. It stands 90 metres (295 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 06/24, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 06 is heading about 60° on the compass, and one using the other end about 240°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 90 metres, King Shaka International Airport stands higher than about 51% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 22 airports of South Africa with a height in the data, it is number 19 from the top. It is about 3,290 km south of the equator.
