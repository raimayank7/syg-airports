Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, FMO means Münster Osnabrück Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: EDDG. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 52.1338° N, 7.6885° E. It stands 49 metres (160 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 07/25, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 07 is heading about 70° on the compass, and one using the other end about 250°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 49 metres, Münster Osnabrück Airport stands higher than about 42% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 38 airports of Germany with a height in the data, it is number 19 from the top. It is about 5,800 km north of the equator.
