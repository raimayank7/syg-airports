Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, YRG means Rigolet Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CCZ2. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 54.1797° N, 58.4575° W. It stands 55 metres (180 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Rigolet Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 762 metres (2,500 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 245 airports of Canada on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 240.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 11/29, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 11 is heading about 110° on the compass, and one using the other end about 290°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 55 metres, Rigolet Airport stands higher than about 44% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 255 airports of Canada with a height in the data, it is number 166 from the top. It is about 6,020 km north of the equator.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Rigolet Airport there are 12 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. The nearest large airport is St. John's International Airport (YYT), about 831 km away, roughly 1 h 30 min in the air.
