Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MGA means Augusto C. Sandino (Managua) International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MNMG. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 12.1415° N, 86.1682° W. It stands 59 metres (194 feet) above sea level.
