Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, YWM means Williams Harbour Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CCA6. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 52.5674° N, 55.7849° W. It stands 21 metres (70 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Williams Harbour Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 671 metres (2,200 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 245 airports of Canada on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 243.
