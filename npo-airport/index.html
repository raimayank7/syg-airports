Nanga Pinoh Airport (NPO)

Nanga Pinoh-Borneo Island, Kalimantan Barat (West Kalimantan), Indonesia

Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, NPO means Nanga Pinoh Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: WIOG. ICAO codes that begin with W belong to Indonesia, Malaysia and nearby island countries. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 0.3486° S, 111.7462° E. It stands 37 metres (123 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Sintang, about 54 km away in a straight line.
