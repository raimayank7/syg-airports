Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, UNI means Union Island International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: TVSU. ICAO codes that begin with T belong to the Caribbean. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 12.6001° N, 61.4119° W. It stands 5 metres (16 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Calliaqua, about 63 km away in a straight line.
