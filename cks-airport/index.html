Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CKS means Carajás Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SBCJ. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 6.1178° S, 50.0034° W. It stands 629 metres (2,064 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 10/28, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 10 is heading about 100° on the compass, and one using the other end about 280°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 629 metres, Carajás Airport stands higher than about 84% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 129 airports of Brazil with a height in the data, it is number 32 from the top. It is about 680 km south of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
