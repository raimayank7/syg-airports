Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PEU means Puerto Lempira Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MHPL. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 15.2622° N, 83.7812° W. It stands 10 metres (33 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Puerto Cabezas, about 143 km away in a straight line.
