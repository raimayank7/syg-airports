Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, YHY means Hay River / Merlyn Carter Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CYHY. ICAO codes that begin with C belong to Canada. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 60.8397° N, 115.7830° W. It stands 165 metres (541 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Yellowknife, about 194 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 14/32, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 14 is heading about 140° on the compass, and one using the other end about 320°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 165 metres, Hay River / Merlyn Carter Airport stands higher than about 61% of the 4,004 airports on this site. It is about 6,760 km north of the equator, far enough from it for very long summer days and very short winter ones.
