Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, FNR means Funter Bay Seaplane Base. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: PANR. ICAO codes that begin with P belong to the North Pacific, including Alaska and Hawaii. How the two kinds of code differ.
