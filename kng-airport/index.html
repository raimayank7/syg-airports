Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, KNG means Utarom Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: WASK. ICAO codes that begin with W belong to Indonesia, Malaysia and nearby island countries. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 3.6446° S, 133.6951° E. It stands 6 metres (19 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Bintuni, about 172 km away in a straight line.
