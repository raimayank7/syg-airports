Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MUZ means Musoma Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: HTMU. ICAO codes that begin with H belong to east and north-east Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 1.5030° S, 33.8021° E. It stands 1,160 metres (3,806 feet) above sea level.
