Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PRA means General Urquiza Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SAAP. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 31.7948° S, 60.4804° W. It stands 74 metres (242 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 1/19, lies roughly south to north. An aircraft using the end marked 1 is heading about 10° on the compass, and one using the other end about 190°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 74 metres, General Urquiza Airport stands higher than about 48% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 45 airports of Argentina with a height in the data, it is number 28 from the top. It is about 3,540 km south of the equator.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of General Urquiza Airport there are 2 other airports with scheduled flights, and 1 of them is within 100 km. That is few: the region is thinly served by air. The nearest large airport is Rosario Islas Malvinas International Airport (ROS), about 127 km away, close enough to reach by road in most cases.
