Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PTY means Tocumen International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MPTO. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 9.0714° N, 79.3835° W. It stands 41 metres (135 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 03R/21L, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 03R is heading about 30° on the compass, and one using the other end about 210°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. All 2 runways here point the same way, so they can be used side by side.

Height and position

At 41 metres, Tocumen International Airport stands higher than about 40% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 12 airports of Panama with a height in the data, it is number 2 from the top. It is about 1,010 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
