Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BYM means Carlos Manuel de Cespedes Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MUBY. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 20.3964° N, 76.6214° W. It stands 62 metres (203 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Holguín, about 66 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 07/25, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 07 is heading about 70° on the compass, and one using the other end about 250°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 62 metres, Carlos Manuel de Cespedes Airport stands higher than about 46% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 15 airports of Cuba with a height in the data, it is number 8 from the top. It is about 2,270 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
