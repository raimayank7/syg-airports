Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, WGP means Umbu Mehang Kunda Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: WATU. ICAO codes that begin with W belong to Indonesia, Malaysia and nearby island countries. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 9.6692° S, 120.3020° E. It stands 10 metres (33 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Labuan Bajo, about 138 km away in a straight line.
