Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CZE means José Leonardo Chirinos Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SVCR. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

The airport lies at 11.4149° N, 69.6809° W. It stands 16 metres (52 feet) above sea level.
