Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BTS means M. R. Štefánik Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: LZIB. ICAO codes that begin with L belong to southern Europe, Israel and Turkey. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 48.1702° N, 17.2127° E. It stands 133 metres (436 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 13/31, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 13 is heading about 130° on the compass, and one using the other end about 310°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 133 metres, M. R. Štefánik Airport stands higher than about 58% of the 4,004 airports on this site. It is about 5,360 km north of the equator.
