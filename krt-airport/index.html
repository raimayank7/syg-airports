Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, KRT means Khartoum International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: HSSK. ICAO codes that begin with H belong to east and north-east Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 15.5895° N, 32.5532° E. It stands 386 metres (1,265 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 18/36, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 18 is heading about 180° on the compass, and one using the other end about 360°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 386 metres, Khartoum International Airport stands higher than about 77% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 7 airports of Sudan with a height in the data, it is number 5 from the top. It is about 1,730 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Khartoum International Airport is an isolated airport: no other airport with scheduled flights lies within 300 km of it. The nearest is El-Obeid Airport (EBD), about 369 km away. The nearest other large airport is Port Sudan New International Airport (PZU), about 655 km away, roughly 1 h 15 min in the air.
