Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MCP means Macapá - Alberto Alcolumbre International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SBMQ. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 0.0507° N, 51.0722° W. It stands 17 metres (56 feet) above sea level.
