Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, NTT means Kuini Lavenia Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: NFTP. ICAO codes that begin with N belong to the South Pacific. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 15.9773° S, 173.7911° W. It stands 9 metres (30 feet) above sea level.
