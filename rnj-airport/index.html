Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, RNJ means Yoron Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: RORY. ICAO codes that begin with R belong to East Asia, including Japan, Korea and the Philippines. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 27.0438° N, 128.4016° E. It stands 16 metres (52 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Uruma, about 92 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 14/32, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 14 is heading about 140° on the compass, and one using the other end about 320°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 16 metres, Yoron Airport stands higher than about 25% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 83 airports of Japan with a height in the data, it is number 52 from the top. It is about 3,010 km north of the equator.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Yoron Airport there are 7 other airports with scheduled flights, and 1 of them is within 100 km. The nearest large airport is Naha International Airport (OKA), about 121 km away, close enough to reach by road in most cases.
