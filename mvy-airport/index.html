Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, MVY means Martha's Vineyard Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KMVY. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 41.3931° N, 70.6143° W. It stands 20 metres (67 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 06/24, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 06 is heading about 60° on the compass, and one using the other end about 240°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.
