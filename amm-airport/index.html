Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, AMM means Queen Alia International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: OJAI. ICAO codes that begin with O belong to the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 31.7226° N, 35.9932° E. It stands 730 metres (2,395 feet) above sea level.
