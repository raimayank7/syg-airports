Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BKO means Modibo Keita International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: GABS. ICAO codes that begin with G belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 12.5335° N, 7.9499° W. It stands 380 metres (1,247 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 06/24, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 06 is heading about 60° on the compass, and one using the other end about 240°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 380 metres, Modibo Keita International Airport stands higher than about 76% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 5 airports of Mali with a height in the data, it is the highest. It is about 1,390 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Modibo Keita International Airport is an isolated airport: no other airport with scheduled flights lies within 300 km of it. The nearest is Bobo Dioulasso Airport (BOY), about 422 km away. The nearest other large airport is Bobo Dioulasso Airport (BOY) in Burkina Faso, about 422 km away, roughly 1 h 00 min in the air.
