Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CUQ means Coen Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: YCOE. ICAO codes that begin with Y belong to Australia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 13.7611° S, 143.1133° E. It stands 162 metres (532 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Coen Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,252 metres (4,107 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 156 airports of Australia on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 141.
