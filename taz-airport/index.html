Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, TAZ means Dashoguz International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: UTAT. ICAO codes that begin with U belong to Russia and neighbouring states. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 41.7599° N, 59.8361° E. It stands 83 metres (272 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Nukus, about 80 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 08/26, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 08 is heading about 80° on the compass, and one using the other end about 260°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 83 metres, Dashoguz International Airport stands higher than about 50% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 6 airports of Turkmenistan with a height in the data, it is number 5 from the top. It is about 4,640 km north of the equator.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Dashoguz International Airport there are 2 other airports with scheduled flights, and 2 of them are within 100 km. That is few: the region is thinly served by air. The nearest other large airport is Urgench International Airport (UGC) in Uzbekistan, about 70 km away, close enough to reach by road in most cases.
