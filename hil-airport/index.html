Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, HIL means Shilavo Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: HASL. ICAO codes that begin with H belong to east and north-east Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 6.0775° N, 44.7637° E. It stands 395 metres (1,296 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Warder, about 118 km away in a straight line.
