Oulad Yaich, Béni Mellal-Khénifra Region, Morocco

Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BEM means Beni Mellal Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: GMMD. ICAO codes that begin with G belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 32.4019° N, 6.3159° W. It stands 516 metres (1,694 feet) above sea level.
