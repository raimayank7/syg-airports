Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CEN means Ciudad Obregón International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: MMCN. ICAO codes that begin with M belong to Mexico and Central America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 27.3926° N, 109.8330° W. It stands 74 metres (243 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 13/31, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 13 is heading about 130° on the compass, and one using the other end about 310°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

At 74 metres, Ciudad Obregón International Airport stands higher than about 48% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 63 airports of Mexico with a height in the data, it is number 33 from the top. It is about 3,050 km north of the equator.
