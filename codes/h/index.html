The letter H in numbers

There are 676 possible three-letter codes that begin with H, from HAA to HZZ. 322 of them, 48%, belong to an airport in this data, and 161 of those airports have scheduled airline flights and a page of their own here. Codes beginning with H make up 3.6% of all 9,053 codes on this site, where an even share for each of the 26 letters would be 3.8%.

Which countries use H most

The countries with the most codes on this page are United States (103), China (37), Australia (24), Papua New Guinea (11).

Do the letters match the place?

In 63% of the codes on this page, the city or the airport name itself begins with H. In the rest, the H comes from somewhere else: an older name of the city, the name of the airport and not the city, a letter added because the obvious code was taken, or a national habit. The most common second letter is A (24 codes, from HAA to HAZ).
