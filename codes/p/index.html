The letter P in numbers

There are 676 possible three-letter codes that begin with P, from PAA to PZZ. 474 of them, 70%, belong to an airport in this data, and 224 of those airports have scheduled airline flights and a page of their own here. Codes beginning with P make up 5.2% of all 9,053 codes on this site, where an even share for each of the 26 letters would be 3.8%.

Which countries use P most

The countries with the most codes on this page are United States (128), Brazil (33), Australia (20), Colombia (18).

Do the letters match the place?

In 81% of the codes on this page, the city or the airport name itself begins with P. In the rest, the P comes from somewhere else: an older name of the city, the name of the airport and not the city, a letter added because the obvious code was taken, or a national habit. The most common second letter is A (24 codes, from PAA to PAZ).
