The letter G in numbers

There are 676 possible three-letter codes that begin with G, from GAA to GZZ. 374 of them, 55%, belong to an airport in this data, and 153 of those airports have scheduled airline flights and a page of their own here. Codes beginning with G make up 4.1% of all 9,053 codes on this site, where an even share for each of the 26 letters would be 3.8%.

Which countries use G most

The countries with the most codes on this page are United States (92), Australia (35), Brazil (19), Papua New Guinea (17).

Do the letters match the place?

In 68% of the codes on this page, the city or the airport name itself begins with G. In the rest, the G comes from somewhere else: an older name of the city, the name of the airport and not the city, a letter added because the obvious code was taken, or a national habit. The most common second letter is A (25 codes, from GAA to GAZ).
