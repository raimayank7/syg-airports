The letter Z in numbers

There are 676 possible three-letter codes that begin with Z, from ZAA to ZZZ. 119 of them, 18%, belong to an airport in this data, and 53 of those airports have scheduled airline flights and a page of their own here. Codes beginning with Z make up 1.3% of all 9,053 codes on this site, where an even share for each of the 26 letters would be 3.8%.

Which countries use Z most

The countries with the most codes on this page are Canada (38), China (9), Chile (7), Australia (5). Canada holds 32% of the codes that start with Z, against 5% of all codes, so this letter leans strongly towards it. The United States, which has 22% of all codes, has only 3% of those beginning with Z. Z is a little-used letter at the start of place names, so many of these codes take their Z from somewhere other than the city name.

Do the letters match the place?

In 30% of the codes on this page, the city or the airport name itself begins with Z. In the rest, the Z comes from somewhere else: an older name of the city, the name of the airport and not the city, a letter added because the obvious code was taken, or a national habit. The most common second letter is A (10 codes, from ZAA to ZAZ).

Using this list

The table is in alphabetical order of the code. Type in the filter box to narrow it by code, airport, city or country. A code with a link opens that airport's page, with its runways, height, local time and distances. A code without a link belongs to an airfield with no scheduled airline flights in the data. If the three letters on your ticket are not here, they may be a city code that covers several airports, such as LON or NYC.
