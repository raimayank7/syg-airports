The letter Y in numbers

There are 676 possible three-letter codes that begin with Y, from YAA to YZZ. 443 of them, 66%, belong to an airport in this data, and 265 of those airports have scheduled airline flights and a page of their own here. Codes beginning with Y make up 4.9% of all 9,053 codes on this site, where an even share for each of the 26 letters would be 3.8%.

Which countries use Y most

The countries with the most codes on this page are Canada (387), China (22), United States (6), Russia (3). Canada holds 87% of the codes that start with Y, against 5% of all codes, so this letter leans strongly towards it. The United States, which has 22% of all codes, has only 1% of those beginning with Y. Canada is the reason this list is so long. Almost every Canadian airport code begins with Y, a habit that goes back to the codes of the weather and radio stations beside the early airfields. The letters after the Y often say little about the city: Toronto is YYZ and Vancouver is YVR.

Do the letters match the place?

In 10% of the codes on this page, the city or the airport name itself begins with Y. In the rest, the Y comes from somewhere else: an older name of the city, the name of the airport and not the city, a letter added because the obvious code was taken, or a national habit. The most common second letter is A (23 codes, from YAA to YAZ).

Using this list

The table is in alphabetical order of the code. Type in the filter box to narrow it by code, airport, city or country. A code with a link opens that airport's page, with its runways, height, local time and distances. A code without a link belongs to an airfield with no scheduled airline flights in the data. If the three letters on your ticket are not here, they may be a city code that covers several airports, such as LON or NYC.
