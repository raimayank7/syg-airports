The letter T in numbers

There are 676 possible three-letter codes that begin with T, from TAA to TZZ. 496 of them, 73%, belong to an airport in this data, and 243 of those airports have scheduled airline flights and a page of their own here. Codes beginning with T make up 5.5% of all 9,053 codes on this site, where an even share for each of the 26 letters would be 3.8%.

Which countries use T most

The countries with the most codes on this page are United States (85), Papua New Guinea (27), Australia (26), Indonesia (25).

Do the letters match the place?

In 78% of the codes on this page, the city or the airport name itself begins with T. In the rest, the T comes from somewhere else: an older name of the city, the name of the airport and not the city, a letter added because the obvious code was taken, or a national habit. The most common second letter is K (26 codes, from TKA to TKZ).
