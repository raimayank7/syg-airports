The letter K in numbers

There are 676 possible three-letter codes that begin with K, from KAA to KZZ. 505 of them, 75%, belong to an airport in this data, and 252 of those airports have scheduled airline flights and a page of their own here. Codes beginning with K make up 5.6% of all 9,053 codes on this site, where an even share for each of the 26 letters would be 3.8%.

Which countries use K most

The countries with the most codes on this page are United States (71), Papua New Guinea (49), Australia (37), Russia (26).

Do the letters match the place?

In 73% of the codes on this page, the city or the airport name itself begins with K. In the rest, the K comes from somewhere else: an older name of the city, the name of the airport and not the city, a letter added because the obvious code was taken, or a national habit. The most common second letter is A (26 codes, from KAA to KAZ).
