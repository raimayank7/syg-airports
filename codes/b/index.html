The letter B in numbers

There are 676 possible three-letter codes that begin with B, from BAA to BZZ. 603 of them, 89%, belong to an airport in this data, and 276 of those airports have scheduled airline flights and a page of their own here. Codes beginning with B make up 6.7% of all 9,053 codes on this site, where an even share for each of the 26 letters would be 3.8%.

Which countries use B most

The countries with the most codes on this page are United States (136), Australia (49), Brazil (24), Papua New Guinea (23).

Do the letters match the place?

In 82% of the codes on this page, the city or the airport name itself begins with B. In the rest, the B comes from somewhere else: an older name of the city, the name of the airport and not the city, a letter added because the obvious code was taken, or a national habit. The most common second letter is D (26 codes, from BDA to BDZ).
