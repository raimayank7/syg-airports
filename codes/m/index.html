The letter M in numbers

There are 676 possible three-letter codes that begin with M, from MAA to MZZ. 631 of them, 93%, belong to an airport in this data, and 268 of those airports have scheduled airline flights and a page of their own here. Codes beginning with M make up 7.0% of all 9,053 codes on this site, where an even share for each of the 26 letters would be 3.8%.

Which countries use M most

The countries with the most codes on this page are United States (175), Australia (61), Papua New Guinea (30), Brazil (19).

Do the letters match the place?

In 83% of the codes on this page, the city or the airport name itself begins with M. In the rest, the M comes from somewhere else: an older name of the city, the name of the airport and not the city, a letter added because the obvious code was taken, or a national habit. The most common second letter is D (26 codes, from MDA to MDZ).
