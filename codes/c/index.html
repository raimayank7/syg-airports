The letter C in numbers

There are 676 possible three-letter codes that begin with C, from CAA to CZZ. 550 of them, 81%, belong to an airport in this data, and 246 of those airports have scheduled airline flights and a page of their own here. Codes beginning with C make up 6.1% of all 9,053 codes on this site, where an even share for each of the 26 letters would be 3.8%.

Which countries use C most

The countries with the most codes on this page are United States (158), Australia (58), Brazil (43), Mexico (18).

Do the letters match the place?

In 79% of the codes on this page, the city or the airport name itself begins with C. In the rest, the C comes from somewhere else: an older name of the city, the name of the airport and not the city, a letter added because the obvious code was taken, or a national habit. The most common second letter is M (26 codes, from CMA to CMZ).
