The letter L in numbers

There are 676 possible three-letter codes that begin with L, from LAA to LZZ. 458 of them, 68%, belong to an airport in this data, and 209 of those airports have scheduled airline flights and a page of their own here. Codes beginning with L make up 5.1% of all 9,053 codes on this site, where an even share for each of the 26 letters would be 3.8%.

Which countries use L most

The countries with the most codes on this page are United States (106), Australia (31), China (28), Indonesia (22).

Do the letters match the place?

In 74% of the codes on this page, the city or the airport name itself begins with L. In the rest, the L comes from somewhere else: an older name of the city, the name of the airport and not the city, a letter added because the obvious code was taken, or a national habit. The most common second letter is B (26 codes, from LBA to LBZ).
