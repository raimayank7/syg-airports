Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, PEW means Bacha Khan International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: OPPS. ICAO codes that begin with O belong to the Middle East, Pakistan and Afghanistan. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 33.9939° N, 71.5146° E. It stands 353 metres (1,158 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 17/35, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 17 is heading about 170° on the compass, and one using the other end about 350°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 353 metres, Bacha Khan International Airport stands higher than about 75% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 23 airports of Pakistan with a height in the data, it is number 10 from the top. It is about 3,780 km north of the equator.
