Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, BHJ means Bhuj Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VABJ. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 23.2878° N, 69.6702° E. It stands 82 metres (268 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 05/23, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 05 is heading about 50° on the compass, and one using the other end about 230°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 82 metres, Bhuj Airport stands higher than about 50% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 115 airports of India with a height in the data, it is number 83 from the top. It is about 2,590 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
