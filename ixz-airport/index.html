Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, IXZ means Veer Savarkar International Airport / INS Utkrosh. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VOPB. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

The airport lies at 11.6402° N, 92.7290° E. It stands 4 metres (13 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 04/22, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 04 is heading about 40° on the compass, and one using the other end about 220°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 4 metres above sea level, Veer Savarkar International Airport / INS Utkrosh is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 115 airports of India with a height in the data, it is the lowest. It is about 1,290 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Veer Savarkar International Airport / INS Utkrosh is an isolated airport: no other airport with scheduled flights lies within 300 km of it. The nearest is Pathein Airport (BSX), about 616 km away. The nearest other large airport is Yangon International Airport (RGN) in Myanmar, about 691 km away, roughly 1 h 20 min in the air.
