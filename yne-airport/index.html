Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, YNE means Norway House Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: CYNE. ICAO codes that begin with C belong to Canada. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 53.9583° N, 97.8442° W. It stands 224 metres (734 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Norway House Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,189 metres (3,902 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 245 airports of Canada on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 161.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 06/24, lies roughly south-west to north-east. An aircraft using the end marked 06 is heading about 60° on the compass, and one using the other end about 240°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 224 metres, Norway House Airport stands higher than about 67% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 255 airports of Canada with a height in the data, it is number 96 from the top. It is about 6,000 km north of the equator.
