Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, KEP means Nepalgunj Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VNNG. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 28.1036° N, 81.6670° E. It stands 165 metres (540 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 08/26, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 08 is heading about 80° on the compass, and one using the other end about 260°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 165 metres, Nepalgunj Airport stands higher than about 61% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 19 airports of Nepal with a height in the data, it is number 14 from the top. It is about 3,120 km north of the equator.
