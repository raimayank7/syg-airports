Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, AZO means Kalamazoo/Battle Creek International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KAZO. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 42.2321° N, 85.5496° W. It stands 266 metres (874 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Grand Rapids, about 82 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 17/35, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 17 is heading about 170° on the compass, and one using the other end about 350°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 3 runways point in 3 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.
