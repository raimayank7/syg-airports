Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, IDA means Idaho Falls Regional Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KIDA. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 02/20, lies roughly south to north. An aircraft using the end marked 02 is heading about 20° on the compass, and one using the other end about 200°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 2 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.

Height and position

At 1,446 metres, Idaho Falls Regional Airport stands higher than about 94% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 645 airports of United States with a height in the data, it is number 39 from the top. In a standard atmosphere the air pressure at this height is about 84% of the pressure at sea level. Engines and wings both work less well in thinner air, so the same aircraft needs more runway here than at the coast. It is about 4,840 km north of the equator.
