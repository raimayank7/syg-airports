Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CVG means Cincinnati Northern Kentucky International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: KCVG. ICAO codes that begin with K belong to the mainland United States. How the two kinds of code differ.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 09/27, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 09 is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. The 4 runways point in 2 different directions, which gives a choice when the wind swings round.
