Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, GOT means Göteborg Landvetter Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: ESGG. ICAO codes that begin with E belong to northern Europe. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 57.6628° N, 12.2798° E. It stands 154 metres (506 feet) above sea level.
