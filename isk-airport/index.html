Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ISK means Nashik International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: VAOZ. ICAO codes that begin with V belong to South Asia and mainland South-East Asia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 20.1191° N, 73.9129° E. It stands 579 metres (1,900 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 09/27, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 09 is heading about 90° on the compass, and one using the other end about 270°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 579 metres, Nashik International Airport stands higher than about 83% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 115 airports of India with a height in the data, it is number 19 from the top. It is about 2,240 km north of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
