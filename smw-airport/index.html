Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, SMW means Smara Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: GMMA. ICAO codes that begin with G belong to West Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 26.7320° N, 11.6837° W. It stands 107 metres (350 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Laayoune, about 156 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 16/34, lies roughly north to south. An aircraft using the end marked 16 is heading about 160° on the compass, and one using the other end about 340°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 107 metres, Smara Airport stands higher than about 54% of the 4,004 airports on this site. It is about 2,970 km north of the equator.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Smara Airport there are 3 other airports with scheduled flights, and none of them is within 100 km. That is few: the region is thinly served by air. The nearest large airport is Laayoune Hassan I International Airport (EUN), about 159 km away.
