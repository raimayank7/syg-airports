Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, NZC means Maria Reiche Neuman Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: SPZA. ICAO codes that begin with S belong to South America. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 14.8540° S, 74.9615° W. It stands 567 metres (1,860 feet) above sea level. The nearest large city in our data is Ica, about 120 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 07/25, lies roughly west to east. An aircraft using the end marked 07 is heading about 70° on the compass, and one using the other end about 250°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 567 metres, Maria Reiche Neuman Airport stands higher than about 83% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 25 airports of Peru with a height in the data, it is number 11 from the top. It is about 1,650 km south of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

The nearest large airport is Alejandro Velasco Astete International Airport (CUZ), about 357 km away, roughly 55 min in the air.
