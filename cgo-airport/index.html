Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, CGO means Zhengzhou Xinzheng International Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: ZHCC. ICAO codes that begin with Z belong to China, Mongolia and North Korea. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 34.5265° N, 113.8492° E. It stands 151 metres (495 feet) above sea level.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 12L/30R, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 12L is heading about 120° on the compass, and one using the other end about 300°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it. All 2 runways here point the same way, so they can be used side by side.

Height and position

At 151 metres, Zhengzhou Xinzheng International Airport stands higher than about 60% of the 4,004 airports on this site. Among the 212 airports of China with a height in the data, it is number 132 from the top. It is about 3,840 km north of the equator.
