Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, LEL means Lake Evella Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: YLEV. ICAO codes that begin with Y belong to Australia. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 12.4989° S, 135.8060° E. It stands 78 metres (256 feet) above sea level.

Runways

Lake Evella Airport has 1 runway in the data. The longest is 1,065 metres (3,494 feet). That is a short runway, used mostly by propeller aircraft. Among the 156 airports of Australia on this site with runway data, its longest runway ranks number 144.
