Ketapang, Kalimantan Barat (West Kalimantan), Indonesia

Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, KTG means Rahadi Osman Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: WIOK. ICAO codes that begin with W belong to Indonesia, Malaysia and nearby island countries. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 1.8172° S, 109.9635° E. It stands 14 metres (46 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Manismata, about 139 km away in a straight line.
