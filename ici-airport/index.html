Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, ICI means Cicia Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: NFCI. ICAO codes that begin with N belong to the South Pacific. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 17.7433° S, 179.3420° W. It stands 4 metres (13 feet) above sea level.

Height and position

At 4 metres above sea level, Cicia Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 16 airports of Fiji with a height in the data, it is number 12 from the top. It is about 1,970 km south of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.
