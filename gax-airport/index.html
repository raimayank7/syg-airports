Airport codes

On a ticket or a baggage tag, GAX means Gamba Airport. Pilots and air traffic controllers use a different, four-letter ICAO code: FOGX. ICAO codes that begin with F belong to central and southern Africa. How the two kinds of code differ.

Where it is

The airport lies at 2.7853° S, 10.0472° E. It stands 9 metres (30 feet) above sea level. The nearest town in our data is Tchibanga, about 105 km away in a straight line.

Which way the runway points

The longest runway, 13/31, lies roughly north-west to south-east. An aircraft using the end marked 13 is heading about 130° on the compass, and one using the other end about 310°. Which end is in use on a given day depends on the wind, since aircraft land and take off into it.

Height and position

At 9 metres above sea level, Gamba Airport is close to sea level, like most airports built on coasts and river plains. Among the 6 airports of Gabon with a height in the data, it is number 5 from the top. It is about 310 km south of the equator, within the tropics, where days are close to twelve hours long all year.

Other airports around it

Within 300 km of Gamba Airport there is 1 other airport with scheduled flights. That is few: the region is thinly served by air. The nearest large airport is Port Gentil International Airport (POG), about 272 km away.
